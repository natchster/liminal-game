## Not faults

- **Every gap flagged** (0, 4 and 5 views a floor): the anti-aliased edge pixel of a mass's silhouette against
  the void, 30–40 m off, where a pixel spans about 6 cm and the gap rays' 3 cm rim is too small
  (`s4242 L3_0786_at:44.2200,-4.3800,22.0000,1.57080,0.00000`; six views of the Stair Halls flag the same
  pixels). The detector is tuned (the rim grows with distance) against a known answer.
- **Coplanar pairs that do not fight**: a walk slab's edge on the wall or parapet (522 pairs on the Well), a
  rail post's face on its rail (2,098 on the Works), a pipe's flange on the wall: none flickers in its views.
- **Body and clip in the Stair Halls' wall slabs** (`box 4.08x3.10x0.26`, at the void's edge): the stand is
  2 cm inside the slab's box, but nothing is cut or seen through in its six views.
- **The corner post** (`0.22x3.00x0.22`, 3 mm): nothing seen.
- **The hard edges** of lamps and lit doorways at the frame's border flagged as flicker.

## For a person

- **The walls under the door lamps** wash out to near-white (a mean of 0.53–0.88 of white where the Well's band
  is 0.06–0.17): `STYLE.md` asks for weak fluorescent light.
- **The void** (the Well's bottom, the Stair Halls' open dark, the Works' shafts) is drawn as a flat, even
  cool grey (the clear colour), lighter than the stone, the masses' feet cut against it: `STYLE.md` says "real
  darkness between the lit parts"; `PILLARS.md` 1 has the fog rising from far below.
- **The Lobby's yellow wallpaper** round the exit door on the Works' concrete: meant as the way into the rooms?
- **The pale mint rectangles** on the Stair Halls' far walls (lit windows?), without frames or glass.
- **The door lamp** a featureless white block from 2–3 m; the hood's top edge a near-white sliver.

## What will be fixed

1. **The gaps detector** (not the game): the rim of the gap rays grows with the ray's length (a pixel's span),
   held by a known answer (the edge views of this record clean, the rooms' ceiling crack still found).
2. **F1**: the cell door's frame stands off the pier's face by a few millimetres (`world/building_dress.rs`),
   not on it; toured again on all three floors.
3. **F2 and F3** (planned: solids along each railing and before the exit door): **not built**. Both were the
   tour's wrong body (see the tour again); F3's flat look is F4's work.
4. **F4**: the metalwork's materials worn as the props are (`wear`, a picture where a photograph exists),
   judged by a blind look before and after, never by the flag. (Tried: not preferred, reverted; open.)
5. **F5**: the photographed surfaces' scale held in the world, not by each face's size, if the mapping allows
   it within this work; else recorded for its own milestone. (The mapping is already in the world, a fixed
   repeat a material; the blotches are a facade photograph made to be seen whole, one repeat in 16 m, seen
   from 20 cm beside a finer one: it wants a close-range detail map. Open, in `QUALITY.md`.)
6. **F6** (severity 1) and the questions for a person: left open, in `docs/QUALITY.md`.
