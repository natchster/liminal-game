   made to be seen whole, one repeat in 16 m, seen from 35 cm beside a finer one: it wants a close-range detail
   for the Building's kinds (`photo::detail_for` has none). Open as `MILESTONES.md` 17.
6. **F6**: the posts 4.5 cm wide, run from under the floor into the top rail to its centreline (VP10 stopped
   them under it and the rail floated: fixed in review B).
7. **F7**: decided in review B on what a lamp post is: a real post stops a body. Its box goes into the solids
