## What it found

Nothing of F8's own. In the vaults (495 views on 7's, 567 on 4242's): no flicker, two
gap views, and four flagged things, all kinds the floors had before: a cardboard stack
on the floor's skirt, and the high-bay's fitting read as plain (the halls' high-bays
are, in F7's tour).

## Not faults

- The floors drew anew round their vaults (a zone of them became one), so their
  props stand elsewhere: the clips that are new (almond water, a locker, a filing
  cabinet, a payphone) are the floors' own kinds at new places.
- The plain things that are new are the tunnels' jar lamps' fittings (F5's), more of
  them lit: a vault's region is never one the rot puts out, so the floor's dark falls
  on other regions than before.

## For a person

  cords and the cobwebs where the arch springs (8° and 25°), toward a blind end.
- The liner plate's ribs far off (the blind review saw a slight moiré).
