## For a person

- **The brick** (7 L5, 30 views; 7 L7, with the orange paint vivid and even): clean, saturated orange-red, its
  mortar even and crisp, no chips, no grime at its foot, beside the photographed concrete. `STYLE.md`: "Nothing is
  clean, nothing is saturated".
- **The flashlight at 35 cm** from pale tile or concrete washes the frame to a flat white disc, its texture gone
  (7 L5, 13 views; 4242 L12, 18, a whole near pillar white in `L12_0817_at`; 7 L7, 4, a third of the frame). `STYLE.md`: no bloom, weak light;
  whether a light held against a wall may wash out is taste.
- **The yellow wallpaper's dark leaf and stem shapes** read as plants' shadows with no plant (7 L5, 5 views); the
  stains of sharp-edged, overlapping translucent ellipses read as drawn (4242 L12, 8 views: queued, `MILESTONES.md`
  24, the census's "wall stains drawn as overlapping outlined ellipses"), and the yellow wallpaper is soft beside
  the crisp concrete up close (`L12_2401c_up`).
- **The exit door's warm strip** (`L12_2878c_at`): light at the door's gap, or a glow `PILLARS.md` 4 forbids.
- **Straight edges bow outward near the frame's sides** (7 L5, 3 views): the renderer has no lens distortion (a
  pinhole projection, `Camera::projection`), so a straight edge is drawn straight; what reads as bowing is the
  wide field of view's stretch toward the sides (a setting: `UI.md`, Settings). Not a fault.
- **A clear bottle on a cabinet top** is meant to be found by looking, not by a glow (`almond_water/brief.md`):
  whether players walk past it is asked in `PLAYTEST.md`.
