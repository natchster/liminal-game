## What the tours found in the doors, and kept

- **The leaf's back and its frame's stop, on one plane** (the coplanar flag on L3, L5 and L9: 0.032-0.035 m²,
  at x 102.000 on L9, the wall's plane): both face into the wall, where no view from the floor reaches. The
  inspector found the seam clean where it could see it (`L3_0010_left`: "false").
- **The lever, the closer and the sign's housing are plain** (L3, L5, L9; severity 1): satin chrome, sprayed
  aluminium and white thermoplastic are one colour at their size ("plausible as painted metal or plastic").
- **The Well's taped door's tape is plain** (L1, `#9a927c`; severity 1): it is tape.
- **Red-orange dots down a hermetic leaf's edge** (L1 `L1_0007_right`, `L1_0010_right`; severity 1, low): the
  thin edge takes one column of its face's chipped paint. Left.
- **The veneer's grain mirrored about the leaf's middle** (L3; severity 1, low): a book-matched veneer face is.
- **The exit door clean beside stained walls, the steel door pale and clean** (L9, L5; severity 1, medium):
  the leaves carry their wear where hands and feet put it (`core_props::doors`, the painters), which the
  tour's lamp at 35 cm burns out (the flashlight finding below). For a person.
- **The Works' door in a panel of the yellow rooms' wallpaper, its warm strip at its foot** (L3; severity 2,
  "a question"): the canon's (`LORE.md`, *The Works*: "the yellow of the rooms round it and a warm light over
  it ... It is the only place they do").
- **The broken lift's black and its red bar** (L4; severity 2, questions): the black is the shaft's, what
  looks back out of it is never seen but in it (`LORE.md`, *A broken lift*), and the red lamp over it was the
  old lift's too (the blind review saw it on both sides). For a person.
- **Two door-to-nowhere "leaves" on one plane** (7 L9, `coplanar_alike`, not flagged): one door's two
  buffers in one paint; the placer makes one at most a floor (one draw, one choice), and the census finds
  one (`place_wall_doors__7_L9_299`).
