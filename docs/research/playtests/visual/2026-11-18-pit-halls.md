## What it found, and what was done

- **F1. The pit hall had no walls** (the first tour, the blind review's "bright
  flat openings, like daylight windows"). Its carver opens the zone, and the
  seams that wall a hall are built only between two open kinds of zone: it ran
  into the zones round it, its long sightlines out past the rooms' far plane
  (90 m) onto the background. **Fixed**: it takes the seams a hall has, a few
  doorways in each.
- **F2. The void over a low wall, seen past a taller wall's end** (the second
  tour: 82 views of 4242's pit hall with a blob of 2000 px or more, by a
  hall's 7.1 m wall row and the Maze's 3.1 m walls). A wall's box need not fill
  its tile and no ceiling closes a wall tile, and the bulkheads skipped every
  step between two walls. Not F9's alone: the tour before F9 had 57 such views
  elsewhere, and seed 7 level 5's pool hall golden a bright wedge of it.
  **Fixed** on every floor: such a step is closed where either wall touches
  open floor (`world::arch`); no large gap is left on either floor.
- **F3. The flicker in 4242's pit hall** (73 views at first): gone with its walls
  (12).

## Not faults

- The small gaps left in the pit halls (blobs under 500 px on 7's, mostly under
  2000 on 4242's) are far doorways in its walls onto lit zones beyond the far
  plane: a long hall sees far.
- 4242's plain things (87, from 24) are one barricade of office furniture the
  floor drew anew at its far edge, its parts in flat paint (a kind the floors
  had before).
- 7's flicker rose (26 to 61), 56 of it outside the pit hall on the floor drawn
  anew; its largest (9261 px) is a lamp's pool stuttering on a wall a few
  centimetres from the camera: the lamps stutter. The rest were not each looked
  at.
