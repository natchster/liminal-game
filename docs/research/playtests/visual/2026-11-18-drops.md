What the blind review and the `lore-keeper` found in the same work (not the tour's):
a column on a drop's edge thinned to a post or lost its band and capital (the drop's
tiles made a wall run with it: the walls are now built as before the cut, and a test
holds that a drop changes no wall but its own); a catwalk was a wall tile, a place no
creature follows (now floor of the grid); a catwalk over the dark (none now); and an
audit of ten runs' deep floors found the exit door hung on a dark drop's edge, her camp
backed onto a pit, and her page on a catwalk (a pit is no wall to hang on:
`Game::is_hang_wall`; nothing is put on a drop: a test).

## Not faults

- The gaps: the tour stands at every drop's rail (1362 more views on 7 L13), and from
  there sees across the hall the same lit openings into other zones the floor's gaps
  were before F7 (493 gap views away from the drops, 495 before). None is at a drop.
- Flagged things that are new elsewhere (payphones, vending machines, the exit door,
  cardboard stacks, a barricade): the floors' own kinds at new places, their placement
  drawn anew round the drops' tiles. None is a drop's part.
- 4242's level 6 has no drop and every count is the same: a floor without drops is
  untouched.

## For a person

- Whether a dark drop reads as a pit you cannot see the bottom of, a room's depth, and
  not as the Building's void.
- The railing is new paint, flat (the blind review: "pristine"); its wear is F11's.
- The formwork photograph repeats on a pit's sides (4 m square, as on the slab).

## What will be fixed and what will not

F1-F3 are fixed. The railing's wear goes with the steel's to F11.
