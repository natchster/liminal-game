# Prop census review (rev_census1)

Question: does each prop look like a real, worn object (STYLE.md: real buildings, worn materials, muted colour; PILLARS 2: "props look like real objects, not toys"), or like a toy, cartoon or placeholder? Every cell was looked at full-size and zoomed (crops of the prop, upscaled).

Totals over 102 cells: real 5, plain 55, cartoon 42, unseen 0.

Common pattern across the cartoon grades: flat decals with hard polygon or ellipse edges standing in for organic marks (stains, mould, rust, holes, cobweb), objects made of one primitive with no thinness or fold (paper cups, napkins, plates, paperbacks, card decks, receipts, paper planes), and toy primaries or candy colours (garland, crown, apron, book trio). Most cartoon props are also spotless; almost nothing in the census shows wear, apart from the vending machine, the cardboard boxes and the elevator.

Note on every cell: a centred HUD caption ("Almond water — ...", "The switch flipped on its own", or a zone name) is drawn over the prop in each frame. It is not part of the prop and was ignored in the grades.
