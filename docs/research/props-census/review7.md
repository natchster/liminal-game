# Prop census, sheets 099–109: review

Question: does each prop read as a real, worn object (STYLE.md: real
buildings photographed badly, muted colour, nothing clean), or as a toy,
cartoon or placeholder? Every cell was cropped and zoomed (2× cell, 4×
centre, and 8–13× on small props) before grading. The memory file has no
human verdicts yet; its one calibration note (saturated colour is not
"decay") was applied to the coloured props below.

Grades: `real`, `plain`, `cartoon`, `unseen`.

## Sheet 099

1. **place_ancient_portraits**: a framed portrait whose picture is a flat
   grey avatar silhouette (an oval head on trapezoid shoulders), the
   "missing profile photo" icon. The frame is four flat bars that overlap
   at the corners instead of meeting in mitres. Nothing about it is old.
   **cartoon**
2. **place_baby_bottles**: a cream cylinder with a collar ring and a tan
   teat, lying on its side on the tiles. Simple, but the proportions are
   right and it reads as a bottle. It has no measure marks and no wear.
   **plain**
3. **place_baby_crib_fragments**: a crib end panel: corner posts with
   ball finials, a top rail and ten spindles. The proportions are right.
   The pale wood is clean and unworn. It stands upright on the tiles with
   no support and no contact shadow. **plain**
4. **place_bird_baths**: a concrete pedestal bird bath (plinth, column,
   bowl) whose shape and stone material are good. The water is an opaque,
   saturated swimming-pool cyan disc filled to the brim, the most
   saturated thing on the sheet. It reads as painted plastic, not water,
   and jars against the muted carpet. **cartoon**
5. **place_bird_nests**: two thin polygonal rings of twig segments with
   one egg on the floor in the middle. There is no woven cup or body, so
   it reads as a wreath outline or wire diagram, not a nest. **cartoon**
6. **place_birdcages**: a small domed wire cage on a thin pole with a
   round foot, in a dark corridor. It is dim and small, but the
   silhouette (dome, tapered bars, stand) reads as a birdcage. **plain**

## Broken or questionable

- **105/5 servant_hall_bell_racks**: the prop is not in frame. The camera
  shows a blank wall with two grey squares at the bottom edge, so the
  framing missed or the rack is hidden.
- **104/2 cuckoo clock**: the three weights appear to float below the
  case because their chains are sub-pixel at this distance.
- **102/3 garden hoe**: only a small dark stub of the blade shows below
  the handle line. The blade may be sunk into the floor.
- **Standing without support**: the crib panel (099/3) stands upright on
  its edge, the record sleeves (105/2) stand on edge, and the spinning
  top (108/6) is balanced on its point at rest.
- **106/1 sink faucet**: on a bare wall with no basin below it.
- **Eyes that glow like lamps**: the cat (106/4) and owl (107/5)
  figurines have self-lit yellow eyes. STYLE.md asks for eyeshine, never
  lamps. The crystal skull (101/1) also emits a green glow.
- **Exposure and colour (not props)**: the white tiled pillar in 100/4
  is blown out to near-white by the lamp. The lobby wallpaper under the
  lamp in 100/1, 100/6, 101/2 and 105/3 is a strong lemon yellow, more
  saturated than STYLE.md's faded yellow-grey.
- **HUD**: an "Almond water — …" (or zone or switch) toast sits in the
  same place in every cell and overlaps the prop in several (099/1,
  099/3, 100/1, 100/6, 104/4, 105/3, 109/4). The census renders should
  hide it.
- No black or magenta surfaces, missing walls or stretched textures were
  seen.
