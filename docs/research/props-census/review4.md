# Prop census: review

**The question:** does each prop look like a real, worn object, as STYLE.md asks, or like a toy, a cartoon or a placeholder? Grades: `real`, `plain`, `cartoon`, `unseen`.

**How I looked:** I viewed every sheet whole. I then cut each cell out at full resolution and enlarged it 3–10× (lanczos) so the small props could be judged. A prop that reads only once enlarged is noted as such. Overall almost nothing is worn: nearly every prop is clean, with crisp edges and no grime, even when its shape is right. So `plain` here means "reads as the thing", not "meets STYLE.md's wear rule".
