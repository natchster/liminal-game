# Prop census, review 3

Question: does each prop read as a real object, worn (`STYLE.md`: real
materials, muted colour, wear; PILLARS 2: "props look like real objects, not
toys"), or as a toy, a cartoon or a placeholder? Each sheet was viewed whole
and every cell zoomed (cropped and scaled 3 to 10 times). Grades: `real`,
`plain`, `cartoon`, `unseen`. "Borderline" marks a grade that could go
either way.

Memory read first: no human verdicts recorded yet. The calibration note
(warmer and more colourful is not more liminal) was applied: toy primaries
and toy pinks counted against a prop.

A pattern across the sheets: almost nothing is worn. Props are clean and
evenly coloured, with no scuffs, dust, fading or chipped edges. Even the
`real` and `plain` grades are clean objects. The wall props are also often
washed toward white by the lamp's hotspot (see "Broken and other flags").
