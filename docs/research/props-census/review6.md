# Prop census, round 6: review

Question: does each prop read as a real, worn object (STYLE.md: real buildings, muted colour, wear, "props look like real objects, not toys"), or as a toy, cartoon or placeholder?

I looked at every sheet whole, then at each cell cropped and enlarged 2x and 4x, and at 6x to 32x for the small props (bobby pins, snifter, fan, bulb, matchbook, earring, tape roll, ring, whisk, dust bunnies). A test I used throughout: is the one detail that makes the object what it is there (a colander's perforations, a glass's stem, a kite's spars, fluff on a dust bunny)? Grades: real / plain / cartoon / unseen.

Almost every cell has an interaction caption ("Almond water — ...") across its upper middle, and in some it lies over the prop (024-1, 036-4, 036-6, 042-4, 096-3, 096-4). That comes from the capture, and I have not held it against the props.
