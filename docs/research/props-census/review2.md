# Prop census: review

Seventeen sheets, 101 props (sheet098 has five; its sixth cell is empty and black). For each cell:
what the object is and what makes it read as real or not, then the grade. Judged against
`docs/STYLE.md`: real, worn objects; muted colour; no toy primaries.

**Totals:** real 5, plain 65, cartoon 31, unseen 0.

## What recurs

- **Cloth is always a slab.** The coat on the rack (002/2), whatever hangs on the coat hook (092/4),
  the towel (086/5) and the apron (038/1) are flat planks with no folds, shoulders or drape.
- **Wall marks are hard-edged rectangles.** Chalk marks (020/3), the chalk arrow (020/2), peeled
  paint (056/2) and peeling wallpaper (056/3) have ruler edges, uniform fills and no grain; the
  chalk is set in a typeface, and so is the chalkboard's writing (092/2).
- **One primitive for perfume, used twice.** `place_perfume_bottles` (056/5) and
  `place_small_perfumes` (074/3) are the same cube, cylinder cap and loose sphere in different
  colours. `place_perfumes` (056/6) shows the shape the other two should have.
- **Toy primaries on toys.** Pinwheel (074/6), xylophone (080/2), party horn (074/2), piñata
  (074/5) and the cauldron's glowing green (068/2) are the saturated colours STYLE rules out.
- **Mounted trophies are flat cut-outs.** Antlers (050/5) and fish (050/6).
- **The best props** (CRT TV, filing cabinet, chandelier, safety cone, wooden bucket) are
  the core, modelled ones. Most `place_*` props are plain: they read, but they are clean and
  have none of the wear STYLE asks for.
