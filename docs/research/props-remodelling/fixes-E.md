# Placement fixes, batch E (b15, b16, b17 notes)

One line per logged placement bug. `+` marks an extra bug found in the same placer while fixing a logged one. Every change is inside the named placer, and only changes positions, rotations (including Euler order), pivots (wrapper Groups), scales or loop logic. No primitive or `pg(...)` wrapper was touched. Random draws, probability gates, materials, animators (their targets were moved to pivot Groups where needed: metronome arm, elephant ears) and tracked position arrays are unchanged.

Several "tipped on its side" variants rotated about Z, which stands a long object on its end. They now roll about the object's own long axis (X), and the Euler order is `'YXZ'` (roll first, then the random yaw). With the default 'XYZ' order, a world-X rotation after the yaw would tilt the object instead.

Checked on real levels (level 4 seed 7/9 and level 2 seed 5), in close-up renders (several azimuths, rare variants forced) and with per-part bounds dumps.

Totals: 112 logged-bug lines, of which 93 are FIXED, 1 is PARTLY FIXED and 18 are SKIPPED. The skips are mostly already handled by the new parts or by the central placement pass, or would need a new model. There are also 12 extra `+` bugs: 10 fixed, 2 skipped (both need new geometry).
