# b06 notes (office)

107 parts, 15 980 triangles, wrapping 113 primitives. Shared parts: `CarKeys__bow` (both key heads), `VintageTypewriters__key` and `VintageTypewriters__lever` (also used by place_small_typewriters).

Wall placers (Diplomas, KitchenCalendars, TravelPosters, PropagandaPosters, VintageAds, FramedNewspapers, PeriodicTables, FramedMaps, MissionPatches, AnatomyCharts, WideBookshelves) all share one placement bug. The group is put at `wx(x) + dx * 0.485`, but CELL is 4, so the prop hangs in mid-air about 1.5 m out from the wall. Shots measured a diploma at y 1.41–1.69 m and a bookshelf at y 0.87–1.77 m, both 0.48 m from the tile centre. Local +z also points *toward* the wall, so from the room you see the back of the frame. That is why every frame part has a rabbet and a recessed backing board on its back. The fix belongs in the placer: offset by about `CELL/2 - 0.02` and flip the facing.

- **place_coffee_mugs**: parts `CoffeeMugs__body` (hollow tapered mug with a foot ring and a recessed base; the coffee disc sits inside the wall), `__rim` (smooth rolled lip) and `__handle` (half ring with a D section, thicker at the roots). Left as primitives: the coffee circle_geo! (liquid-surface decal), the steam sphere_geo! (transparent) and the stain ring_geo! (transparent). Placement: the handle's `rotation.y = PI/2` puts the half-torus in the plane tangent to the mug, 7 mm off its wall (x = bodyR + 0.012). The result is an arch standing beside the mug, not a C-handle attached to it.
- **place_newspapers**: parts `Newspapers__sheet` (opened page with a soft fold ridge, lifted corners and paper grain), `__fold` and `__scrap` (crumpled, bowed scraps). Placement: fold1 is tilted (z 0.35, x 0.2) so that about half of it is under the floor (lowest corner at y ≈ −0.020), and fold2 sinks about 8 mm. Only a sliver pokes up.
