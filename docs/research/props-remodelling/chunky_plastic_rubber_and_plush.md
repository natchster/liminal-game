# b16 notes (toys and party)

97 parts, 19,308 triangles, 0 errors, 0 warnings. Every part is within its budget, and the largest frame deviation is 10 % (the elephant ear's thin axis). The parts wrap 110 primitives in 24 placers. Two placers are untouched (Marbles, PuzzlePieces).
Checked part by part, in the game on levels 4 and 1 (seeds 7, 9, 11) and in zoomed shots (level 2, seed 5).
Heights were measured over 6–10 runs of each placer.
Shared parts: `TeddyBears__eye` (a round bead) is also the eyes of the rabbits, elephants, dolls and hobby horses. `TeddyBears__nose` is the rabbit's nose too, and `TeddyBears__arm` the rabbit's arms. `SmallBalloons__string` covers both string constructors.
Local helpers in b16.py: `rsphere` (a sphere with a radius function), `gauss` (angular falloff for bumps), `ring_lathe`, `arc_sweep` (a torus or partial torus with a custom, scalable section), `loft`, `rr4` (a rounded rect with per-corner radii), `ystack` (a flat moulding) and `half_torus_frame`.
