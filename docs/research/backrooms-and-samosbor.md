# Backrooms entities and the Samosbor: research for the roster

Scope: Part 1 covers the well-known Backrooms entities and how video and game adaptations use them. Part 2 covers the creatures, people and rules of the Samosbor (самосбор). Part 3 covers what this means for our roster.

## Part 1: Backrooms entities

### 1.1 Where the canon lives, and the argument over monsters

- **Origin.** The Backrooms began on 4chan's /x/ board on 12 May 2019, as a photo of an empty yellow room. The photo was later traced to a 2002 furniture store in Oshkosh being refitted as a HobbyTown ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Backrooms)).
- **The split over entities.** Fans added levels and hostile "entities". Fans who preferred the bare concept split off into r/TrueBackrooms ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Backrooms)). Its FAQ says it is about the feeling of the place, not monsters or lore ([r/TrueBackrooms FAQ mirror](https://lr.us.psf.lt/r/TrueBackrooms/wiki/faq)).
- **The critics' case.** An essay on liminal space quotes the common complaint that monsters made it "not creepy anymore" ([Kneeling Bus, "Liminal Inferno"](https://kneelingbus.substack.com/p/liminal-inferno)). Our pillar 1 ("emptiness is the threat") sides with the purists, so every creature has to earn its place against the empty room.
- **The wikis contradict each other.** The Fandom wiki came first and is looser; Wikidot runs an SCP-style rating system ([pixiv dictionary](https://dic.pixiv.net/history/Backrooms/7549596/source)). Their numbers differ (Fandom's 7 is "The Mandela Virus", Wikidot's is Jerry), and so does behaviour: Wikidot Smilers are drawn to light, Fandom Smilers are hurt by it ([Fandom list](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Entity_List)). There is no single canon to "get right".
- **The 2021 archival wiki.** [The Backrooms Archive Wiki](https://the-backrooms-archive.fandom.com/wiki/The_Backrooms_Archive_Wiki) keeps deleted pages. Its first Fandom Level 0 is "by far the safest level", where you are unlikely to meet anything but "a hound or duller" ([First Fandom Level 0](https://the-backrooms-archive.fandom.com/wiki/First_Fandom_Level_0)). Today's Wikidot Level 0 reports only "dark figures", familiar voices calling, and scratching in the walls ([Level 0](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-0)).
- **How entities are pictured.** Because every Wikidot image needs a compatible licence, pages mix original drawings (Angel Dream's Smiler, chompgwenarts' Hound, garb's Skin-Stealer), stock photos (Unsplash for Facelings, Pixabay for the Stalker) and edited Commons photos (credited on the pages below). The Fandom Hound's header is framed as a photo found in "a heavily damaged Kodak camera" ([Entity 8, Fandom](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Entity_8)). The house style is the found photograph, which matches our "photographed badly".

### 1.2 Survey of the prominent entities

Unless marked otherwise, the entity numbers below are Wikidot's. The text of every page cited is CC BY-SA 3.0.

**Smilers (Entity 3).** [Wikidot](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-3) (original by Palico22, rewrite by Stretchsterz; image by Angel Dream, CC BY-SA).
- **Look:** a long grin of many sharp teeth and white, reflective eyes that gleam in the dark. The body is hard to see, possibly with bent limbs. It is almost always pictured as a black frame holding only the eyes and the grin.
- **Behaviour:** drawn to light, and chases anyone carrying one. It attacks mainly if you panic and retreat, or if there is a loud noise.
- **Rule:** keep eye contact, stay quiet and back away slowly. Turn the light off, or throw it as a decoy.
- **Habitat:** dark areas, especially Levels 2 and 3. The earliest modern report was a Reddit post about a "Glowy Smiling Thing".
- **Fandom version.** The [Fandom rewrite](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Entity_3) makes it an amorphous, pitch-black, flame-like "spectral" being that taunts its victims. It is hurt by light and burned by fire, the opposite of Wikidot.
- **Iconic or overdone:** the most recognisable Backrooms monster, and the most overused. Every game has one.

**Hounds (Entity 8).** [Wikidot](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-8) (Palico22, rewrite by 1000dumplings; header image by chompgwenarts, CC BY-SA 4.0).
- **Look:** a thin, sickly person on all fours, with long black hair over a large toothed mouth, and claws.
- **Behaviour:** hostile on sight. It grows more aggressive when the person it sees acts aggressively.
- **Rule:** hold eye contact, which cows it briefly. Hide if you hear growling. Don't run the instant you see one.
- **Fandom version.** On [Fandom](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Entity_8) Hounds are humans changed by a virus. They hear from two miles away, show "positive phototaxis" (they seek light), limp, and claw their own faces to the bone.
- **Iconic or overdone:** the image borrows the wet-haired ghost girl of J-horror, which is now a cliché. The stare-down rule is the useful part.

**Facelings (Entity 9).** [Wikidot](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-9) (original co-authors Jo and Higher Being Love Cereal!; rewrites by 1000dumplings and Nikuchan; photos from Unsplash).
- **Look:** people with no facial features. "Evolved" ones have one eye or a lipless mouth, and "shadow" ones are pitch black and drawn to light.
- **Behaviour:** mostly docile. They imitate human routines, roles and customs without understanding them, and communicate silently. They anger easily and defend each other fiercely, especially children.
- **Rule:** be kind, respect their boundaries, and never provoke or look threatening.
- **Iconic:** one of the few non-hostile entities. It is uncanny because the routines are right and the faces are missing.

**Skin-Stealers (Entity 10).** [Wikidot](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-10) (AndromedaMapping1, rewrite by 1000dumplings; art by garb).
- **Look:** tall, pale yellow humanoids with sunken white eyes and suckered skin. They stretch a victim's skin over themselves.
- **Behaviour:** they repeat speech they have heard without understanding it, to lure people in.
- **Rule:** their blood is clear, not red. Distrust anyone who speaks little or in scraps, and walk away calmly.
- **Iconic or overdone:** the clear-blood test needs companions, so it does not work alone. The voice lure does.

**Partygoers (Entity 67).**
- **Original, 2021:** the archived "Level Fun =)" page is written in a chirpy voice ("We have so much fun here! =)"). It shows a yellow figure holding a red balloon, "covered in fun" ([archive](https://the-backrooms-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Level_Fun_%3D)).
- **Wikidot rewrite** (Robert Goerman and MC_Crafter_24_7 from 1000dumplings): tall yellow leathery bodies with a carved, bloody smiley face and noodle arms ending in toothed suckers. They hunt in packs and use party posters and fake database entries as lures. The rule is to run, because they are short-sighted and slow ([Entity 67](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-67)).
- **Fandom rewrite:** now a virus. Victims are hobbling husks under a large yellowed sheet, with self-inflicted wounds bleeding through it in a smile-shaped stain ([Sanguine Festivus](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Sanguine_Festivus)). This sits close to our Watcher's dust sheet.
- **Overdone:** "=)" is a meme and the tone is camp. It does not fit us.

**Deathmoths (Entity 4).** [Wikidot](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-4) (Kekcroc, rewrite by DrAkimoto). Moths with 30–45 cm wingspans, up to 3 m "Empresses", nesting in fleshy growths over doorways. The females swarm and kill by blood loss, and bright light draws them, so the rule is to dim your light and leave. Giant insects read as B-movie.

**Clumps (Entity 5).** [Wikidot](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-5) (Kekcroc, rewrite by TrailmixNCocoa).
- **Look:** a tangle of human limbs about 1 m across, with an eye or ear here and there. It smells of burnt flesh and dahlias.
- **Behaviour:** it moves by swiping at the floor. Within about 8 ft it shoots out its longest limb (8–10 ft) to drag prey to a toothed mouth. After eating it burrows in and sleeps.
- **Rule:** stay silent and stay out of reach. It lives in tight spaces (Levels 2, 3, 10).
- **Iconic:** a clear reach rule. Its look is the "body horror" mass that the Samosbor also uses.

**Wretches (Entity 15).** [Wikidot](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-15) (Stretchsterz and etoisle; art by PandaShark71004).
- **Look:** skeletal people with flaky red-brown skin full of holes, sometimes with misplaced eyes or extra limbs.
- **Behaviour:** people become Wretches through the "Wretched Cycle", caused by going without food, water and sleep and sped up by isolation. Almond Water stalls it. Their calls tell you what you face: low calls mean weak ones, high calls mean smart or fast ones.
- **Rule:** eat, drink, sleep, and listen.
- **Iconic:** this is the only entity that is *your* future. It could be told through M.'s notes without ever showing one.

**Windows (Entity 2).** [Wikidot](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-2) (concept by u/glassydude101, rewrite by DrAkimoto).
- **Look:** a window in a wall, in the style of its level, showing each viewer a different blurred landscape that feels familiar. An occupied one holds a dark silhouette that may look like someone you know.
- **Behaviour:** immobile. It whispers and beckons until you are within 1–2 m, then forms an arm and pulls you in.
- **Rule:** avoid walls full of windows, cover windows, and don't act on the urge to approach.
- **Iconic:** quiet, psychological, and cheap to build.

**Dullers (Entity 6).** [Wikidot](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-6) (anonymous original, rewrite by Stretchsterz).
- **Look:** tall, dark grey, skeletal and faceless, with arms long enough to reach through walls.
- **Behaviour:** it stands in the next corridor and pushes an arm through the wall to drag you into its hallway. It usually runs from anyone who approaches it.
- **Rule:** never stand on the far side of a wall from one. Running *at* it makes it retreat. Almond Water repels it.
- **Iconic:** one of the oldest entities, from the first Level 0. The arm through the wall is a strong, rare idea.

**Wranglers (Entity 75).** [Wikidot](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-75) (Stretchsterz, DrainedWell). Translucent burrowing serpents "10 to 90 miles" long; the males have a smiling human face. You hear them drilling before they arrive, and the rule is to evacuate when the ground rumbles. The scale is absurd; the rumble is good.

**Mannequins (Fandom Entity 29).** [Fandom](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Entity_29). Shop mannequins with needles for arms. "Static Ones" hold still while watched and stab you from behind. A Weeping Angel copy; our Watcher already uses the "moves only when unseen" rule.

**Lesser or off-tone entities, in brief:**
- **Crawlers (Entity 17):** a cordyceps-like fungus turns a person into an arm-crawler in about 12 minutes; the rule is quarantine. Zombie-fungus territory ([Wikidot](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-17); image by CheapCamera, CC BY 4.0).
- **The Neighborhood Watch (Wikidot 96, Fandom 196):** car-sized floating eyeballs and six-legged "Striders" on the suburban Level 9 that kill electronics; the rule is to stay hidden and power nothing on. A monster-movie look ([Wikidot](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-96)).
- **Death Rats (Entity 24):** black rats with red-tipped horns, mostly harmless ([Wikidot](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-24)). Inside the Backrooms makes them eyeless and sound-drawn, so you step aside and let one pass ([wiki](https://inside-the-backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Rat)).
- **Bursters (Entity 11):** a curled creature whose back calluses spray acid at anyone close, on Level 3; the rule is to cover your face ([Wikidot](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-11)).
- **Jerry (Entity 7):** a talking macaw with a cult. Dark comedy ([Wikidot](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-7)).
- **Lighters (Fandom 36):** hot floating lights that chase the living ([Fandom](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Entity_36)). This is the nearest thing to the brief's "Lightbringers"; neither wiki lists that name, nor "the Insanity".
- **Silhouettes (Fandom 73):** wall shadows that peel off into 3D. They overlap our SCP-1983 shadows ([Fandom](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Entity_73)).
- **Chatterers (Fandom 57):** a toy telephone on wheels that leads you to exits. A joke ([Fandom](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Entity_57)).
- **"Hollow":** a place (Level 193), not an entity ([Fandom list](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Entity_List)).
- **Bacteria, "the Lifeform":** archived as "Life Form or Howler", a black wire-like skeleton with teeth ([archive](https://the-backrooms-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Bacteria)). The fan name comes from a render Kane Parsons titled "Bacteria"; he calls it "the Lifeform" ([All Species Wiki](https://allspecies.fandom.com/wiki/Bacillus_sapiens)). It is his copyrighted design, so avoid it, even though Escape the Backrooms and Inside the Backrooms both use it.

**Stalkers (Entity 150).** [Wikidot](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-150) (Spibsboft1; Pixabay photo).
- **Look:** ordinary-looking people who never blink, breathe or show a pulse.
- **Behaviour:** they follow you at 5–100 ft and back off when approached.
- **Rule:** ignore it and keep your mind busy. Don't run, hide or photograph it.
- **Iconic:** pure paranoia, close to our grey figure.

**Mimic (Entity 400).** [Wikidot](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-400) (Poliacci).
- **Look:** an ordinary room with a door left slightly ajar, sometimes with arrows painted nearby. When it attacks, the inside turns to flesh and the doorway to teeth.
- **Behaviour:** it baits the threshold with Almond Water, first-aid kits or axes. It senses vibration.
- **Rule:** don't enter, don't touch the door, and paint over its arrows with warnings.

**Predatory Doors (Fandom Entity 63).** [Fandom](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Entity_63).
- **Look:** the same idea as an exit door. It is blind and counts prey by the vibration of footsteps.
- **Behaviour:** it strikes when prey goes quiet or tries the handle.

**Familiar Face (Fandom Entity 13).** [Fandom](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Entity_13).
- **Look:** a pale, mask-like face with wet, streaming hair on an impossibly long neck that bends round corners, through railings and out of vents. The constant drip is the only clue.
- **Behaviour:** it moves only when unobserved. Its face slowly becomes someone you know, with matching voices, and up close the jaw opens wide.
- **Iconic:** very close to our Stairwell face.

### 1.3 Video and games: tone and how they implement behaviour (ideas only)

**Kane Pixels' The Backrooms (copyrighted; tone only).** The series is found footage: recovered tapes, security feeds and institutional debriefings. The creature is held back, glimpsed at the edges of the frame or as the recording cuts out. Sound carries the dread (alarms, distant crowds, a phone ringing out). The organisation's polished cover stories against what the tape shows breed distrust. Pacing runs slow and mundane, then turns abruptly violent ([summary](https://theghostinmymachine.com/2023/02/06/backrooms-found-footage-a-full-summary-of-the-kane-pixels-backrooms-universe-so-far/)). Wikipedia notes the series is separate from the wiki lore. A24's film directed by Parsons opened on 29 May 2026 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Backrooms)).

What we can take: show less, let sound lead, and use institutional paperwork (the cleaners' log) as a voice that lies by omission.

**Escape the Backrooms (Fancy Games, co-op; from its CC BY-SA fan wiki).**
- **Smilers:** they appear when Level 1's garage lights flicker and die. Only the lit halls are safe, and a chasing Smiler breaks the lights it passes under ([Smiler](https://escapethebackrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Smiler)).
- **Hounds:** faster than you. Shine a flashlight in the eyes three times to stun it and send it back. Hounds can't open doors, so shutting one ends a chase ([Hound](https://escapethebackrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Hound)).
- **Skin-Stealers:** they wear a hazmat "skin" that drops when they close in ([Skin-Stealer](https://escapethebackrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Skin-Stealer)).
- **Level 0 "Bacteria":** spawns after three minutes and knocks to lure you. Turning corners breaks its line of sight, and reaching a dead end makes it respawn elsewhere ([Bacteria](https://escapethebackrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Bacteria)).
- **Partygoers:** they stand still until one spots you, then the whole group chases. Hiding under a table resets them ([Partygoer](https://escapethebackrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Partygoer)).

**Inside the Backrooms (MrFatcat, 2022; from its CC BY-SA fan wiki).**
- **Hound:** a "staring contest". Eye contact scares it off for a while, and looking away or running starts a chase. It travels through ceiling vents, which you can close with a screwdriver. Whistling draws it ([Hound](https://inside-the-backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Hound)).
- **Smiler:** the opposite rule. Looking at it makes your flashlight flicker, and staring too long drains sanity. You stop, keep your distance and let it fade, but it can appear behind you ([Smiler](https://inside-the-backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Smiler)).
- **Bacteria:** speeds up the longer a chase lasts, and nearby lights turn red when it is close ([Bacteria](https://inside-the-backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Bacteria)).
- **Skin Stealer:** drawn to noise, such as eating pills, and senses you from farther away when you are injured ([Skin Stealer](https://inside-the-backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Skin_Stealer)).

**The Backrooms Game (Pie on a Plate, July 2019).** A single roaming "Being" you may not meet at all, plus a watch to check every 30 seconds for sanity. Reviewers praised the atmosphere and criticised the length ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Backrooms); [store listing via DekuDeals](https://www.dekudeals.com/items/the-backrooms-game-free-edition)).

**Takeaways for design.** The games converge on four tools:
1. A **light tell**: lights flicker, die or turn red near the creature.
2. A **gaze rule**, which differs per creature: stare down the Hound, look away from the Smiler.
3. **Doors and hide spots** that end a chase.
4. **Sound attraction**: whistling, eating, footsteps.

They are most readable when two creatures have *opposite* gaze rules, as in Inside the Backrooms. That is a pattern we can use deliberately. Players coming from these games will expect light to be safe from Smilers. Our Wikidot-style "drawn to light" Smiler reverses that, so the first encounter must teach it fairly.

## Part 2: The Samosbor (самосбор)

### 2.1 Origin and authorship

- **The founding post.** The myth began as an anonymous *bugurt* (a short greentext in the "сидишь…" form) on 2ch's /b/ board. The archived "САМОСБОР ТРЕД" opens with these lines: you live on floor 556 of structure P-46; a samosbor came again last night and flooded even your floor; someone is moaning in the stairwell so you are afraid to go out; the liquidators will come in a couple of hours at best; the smell of raw meat is seeping into the room. A later post says it will be a year old in May ([2ch archive, Feb 2019](https://2ch.su/b/arch/2019-02-15/res/191363969.html)). Secondary sources date it to 2018 ([DTF](https://dtf.ru/flood/4518938-vselennaya-samosbora); [Eksmo's anthology blurb via LitRes](https://www.litres.ru/book/denis-kilesov/gigahrusch-72459352/chitat-onlayn/)).
- **Early history.** The first half-year was a burst of anonymous art, text and game projects, much of it lost because threads were not archived. In spring 2019 a trolling war over "furry sharks immune to samosbor" split the community, which moved to VK. Its informal pillars were: the gigakhrushch is infinite, the samosbor is unknowable, and life is mostly crap ([DTF community history](https://dtf.ru/597176-samosbor-istoriya-soobshestva-ch-1)).
- **No single author or canon.** A VK essay by the main community says there is no single author, canon or set of truths: "Его пишут все и одновременно никто" ("everyone writes it, and at the same time no one does"). It treats the samosbor as a law of nature, not an enemy to beat or a mystery to solve. Monsters it spawns are temporary and "not worth cataloguing", and authors should not over-explain origins, factions or technology ([VK, «Как вывести Самосбор на мейнстрим»](https://m.vk.ru/@camocbop-kak-vyvesti-samosbor-na-meinstrim-i-ne-ohuet)).
- **Licence.** The myth has no author and no licence. Individual wikis are CC BY-SA. The Russian fandom wiki says plainly that it is one "how it could be" reconstruction, not the canon ([FAQ](https://samosbor.fandom.com/ru/wiki/FAQ)). Signed stories and books belong to their authors.

### 2.2 The rules residents live by

From the Russian SAMOSBOR wiki ([Самосбор](https://samosbor.fandom.com/ru/wiki/Самосбор), CC BY-SA) unless noted:

- **The siren.** A siren starts on every floor, and a loudspeaker repeats something like "Attention! Samosbor! Go to shelter immediately!" The announcement stops when the event begins, but the siren goes on until the liquidators report the area clean. Elsewhere, sensors on some floors give a few minutes' warning and often fail ([VK essay](https://m.vk.ru/@camocbop-kak-vyvesti-samosbor-na-meinstrim-i-ne-ohuet); [DTF](https://dtf.ru/flood/4518938-vselennaya-samosbora)).
- **The door rule.** Never open the hermetic door until the siren stops, even if close relatives or friends are left outside. A damaged or unsealed door lets the samosbor in, though weakened.
- **Nobody sees it.** Watching through cameras or thick glass drives the watchers mad or mutates them. You can only hear it: squealing and screeching metal, screams, and *demands to open the door*. Sometimes there is only the alarm.
- **The fog and the smell.** A purple or violet fog and a smell of raw meat come first ([DTF](https://dtf.ru/flood/4518938-vselennaya-samosbora); [ixbt.games on KLETKA](https://ixbt.games/articles/2024/12/05/strax-i-nenavist-v-gigaxrushhevke-pogruzenie-v-klet-i-vselennuyu-samosbora.amp.html)). The English archive adds brown slime leaking from pipes. It also describes neighbours' and loved ones' voices banging on doors and begging to be let in. They are a trap: whoever opens is taken, along with everyone sheltering with them ([Self-Assembly](https://samosborarchive-en.fandom.com/wiki/Self-Assembly)).
- **Duration.** A few minutes to a couple of hours, typically affecting two to five floors. It recurs every few days to months.
- **The doors themselves.** Doors are oval, of reinforced concrete, hermetic, and have *no peepholes*. Flats are 36 m² and held at 18 °C ([Жилое строение](https://samosbor.fandom.com/ru/wiki/Жилое_строение)). Doors can be round or square, manual or remotely shut floor-wide, factory-made or home-made with asbestos rope in the gaps ([Hermetic Door](https://samosborarchive-en.fandom.com/wiki/Hermetic_Door)).
- **The administration.** There is no money. Residents get ration coupons and coupons for basic goods, plus "incentive" coupons that pay for 30° vodka and toys. Laws come from unseen "administrators" ([Гигахрущёвка](https://samosbor.fandom.com/ru/wiki/Гигахрущёвка)); elsewhere it is "the Party" ([Liquidators](https://samosborarchive-en.fandom.com/wiki/Liquidators)). Food is "concentrate", with red concentrate the treat ([VK, Дневник Ликвидатора](https://m.vk.ru/@camocbop-dnevnik-likvidatora)).

### 2.3 What lives there, during and after

The Samosbor deliberately refuses a bestiary. Here is what recurs.

- **"Monsters", unclassifiable.** Attempts to classify them failed. They may be made of meat, iron, concrete, plastic, wood, rubber or wool, or have no body at all; some come as hundreds of identical small things, some fill a corridor; some pretend to be people, and some *are* people. Some die to shot, acid, fire or axe, and some only if you sing them a lullaby or beat them at draughts ([Последствия самосбора](https://samosbor.fandom.com/ru/wiki/Последствия_самосбора)). The English archive's scientist proposes classifying them by *behaviour*, because bodies vary too much ([Creatures](https://samosborarchive-en.fandom.com/wiki/Creatures)), a useful hint for a learnable game.
- **Slime, by colour** ([same page](https://samosbor.fandom.com/ru/wiki/Последствия_самосбора)). It is burned with napalm before it is raked up.

  | Colour | Effect |
  |---|---|
  | brown | stinks |
  | green | eats organic matter, including suits |
  | white | compels you to smear yourself in it |
  | red | glue you cannot escape |
  | black | forms half-sentient monsters with white bubbles for eyes that get *under* suits |
  | blue-glowing | radioactive |
  | silver | euphoric, usually lethal |
  | "серобурмалиновая" (a nonsense colour word) | maddens; don't look at it |

  The English archive gives brown to dark purple, and fresh slime mutates the legs of anyone who steps in it ([Slime](https://samosborarchive-en.fandom.com/wiki/Slime)).
- **People.** The fog's effect on people runs from a quiet death with no mark on the body to mincemeat. Survivors go mad and are deformed, and the liquidators destroy them ([same page](https://samosbor.fandom.com/ru/wiki/Последствия_самосбора)).
  - "Infected" residents weep black slime from their orifices and grow tentacles. Among them are **Mimics**: never clearly seen, they imitate the voices of close people and ask to be let through the hermetic door. If seen, they shed their skin and attack with tentacles ([Infected](https://samosborarchive-en.fandom.com/wiki/Infected)).
  - The founding thread's later posts mention **нелюди** ("non-people") who look like neighbours, relatives or yourself. They also mention a woman begging at a door while something chases her, so the resident must judge whether it is a trap ([2ch archive](https://2ch.su/b/arch/2019-02-15/res/191363969.html)).
- **Voices in the pipes.** The same thread has dead relatives' voices coming through radiators and pipes, tap water turning red and smelling of iron, people fused into corridors and walls, and hands bursting out of walls to seize passers-by ([2ch archive](https://2ch.su/b/arch/2019-02-15/res/191363969.html)).
- **Living blocks.** A first-person VK story is told by a man absorbed into his block. The samosbor is a "relocation" in which blocks lose their identities or are replaced. Absorbed people talk through pressure in water pipes, *knocking in pipes* and rustling in cupboards. New tenants dismiss the knocks as madness or drink. "A closed hermetic door is a guarantee of stability, not only for residents but for us" ([VK, «Живые блоки»](https://m.vk.ru/@camocbop-zhivye-bloki); motif only).
- **Aberrants and masses.** A liquidator story describes black slime on everything, raked into bottomless lift shafts. Some flats hold living flesh masses with barbed tendrils, and deformed "aberrants" (аберы) wander in groups. There are floors gone soft that swallow people, a bulge that regrows after being shot, endless corridors, and a rising hum from far below ([VK, «Самосбор: последствия»](https://m.vk.ru/@camocbop-samosbor-posledstviya)).
- **Concrete Eaters.** Blind, egg-laying, worm-like fauna of the structure, *not* made by the samosbor. They sense vibration in concrete through bristles and are heard as a faint tremor or "the sound of a rolling ball". They seek out dense biomass but don't hunt people actively ([Concrete Eater](https://samosborarchive-en.fandom.com/wiki/Concrete_Eater)).
- **Human factions.** Sectarians who dig into sealed floors and worship the phenomenon ([2ch archive](https://2ch.su/b/arch/2019-02-15/res/191363969.html)). The "Pure Ones" (Чистые), who worship samosbor and mould ([DTF](https://dtf.ru/flood/4518938-vselennaya-samosbora)). Poachers who burn through hermetic doors into quarantined floors ([Poachers](https://samosborarchive-en.fandom.com/wiki/Poachers)).

### 2.4 The liquidators

- **Who they are.** In the Russian wiki they are two-year conscripts plus veteran volunteers. Most die in their first samosbor. After the danger passes they clear remains, slime, bodies and dropped gear, and wash every affected room with disinfectant ([Ликвидаторы](https://samosbor.fandom.com/ru/wiki/Ликвидаторы)).
- **Gear.** A gas mask, a Kevlar suit and a rubberised cape; a pump shotgun, a fire axe and the backpack flamethrower that defines their image; a UV floodlight that blinds things with eyes and drives off bodiless ones. They are paid in extra coupons and a flat of their own.
- **Their methods.** Liquidators may burn residents, weld doors shut from outside, or fill a flat or a whole floor with foam concrete ([KLETKA wiki, Samosbor](https://kletka.wiki.gg/wiki/Samosbor)). In the English archive they are also the Party's police, giving on-the-spot "justice" ([Liquidators](https://samosborarchive-en.fandom.com/wiki/Liquidators)).
- **The knock code.** In one story a crew knocks on each door in a set pattern, and the residents must reply with a set answer. A cracked, infected door still answers correctly, which unsettles them ([VK, «Самосбор: последствия»](https://m.vk.ru/@camocbop-samosbor-posledstviya)). This is the best single source for our red marks and "doors that did not answer".
- **Feared.** Residents fear them as much as the samosbor ([ixbt.games](https://ixbt.games/articles/2024/12/05/strax-i-nenavist-v-gigaxrushhevke-pogruzenie-v-klet-i-vselennuyu-samosbora.amp.html)).

### 2.5 How it is pictured

The art draws on:
- **The khrushchyovka itself:** peeling paint, cracks, moss and repairs of older repairs, conduit along the ceilings, and the bare "Ilyich lamp" bulb ([Гигахрущёвка](https://samosbor.fandom.com/ru/wiki/Гигахрущёвка)).
- **Late-Soviet interiors:** rugs on walls, bulky TVs and radios ([DTF](https://dtf.ru/flood/4518938-vselennaya-samosbora)).
- **Civil-defence kit**, which is real-world public-domain iconography:
  - the GP-5 rubber hood mask with round glass eyes and a front canister, issued to civilians 1962–1990 and stocked in shelters ([GP-5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GP-5_gas_mask));
  - the OZK rubberised hooded cape, overboots and gloves, grey-olive outside and white inside ([ОЗК](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Общевойсковой_защитный_комплект)).

The art itself ranges from polished game art to MS Paint comics ([DTF](https://dtf.ru/flood/4518938-vselennaya-samosbora)).

Games (copyrighted; ideas only):
- **[SAMOSBOR](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1034060/)** (rocketship, 2020): you are a liquidator who must leave each infected sector before the samosbor arrives.
- **[Living Cell](https://rawg.io/games/living-cell-2)** (gas-mask filters, a flashlight, poisonous slimes) and **[Choice of Life: Samosbor](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1574570/Choice_of_Life_Samosbor/)** (a visual novel), both by Blazing Planet.

## Part 3: What this means for our roster

### 3.1 Which Backrooms entities fit

Our world is the yellow rooms *below* a Soviet megastructure. It has a survivor's trail, a lamp that drains, and a director that paces dread. A creature fits if:
- it reads first as a silhouette, eyes or a sound;
- it has one rule a player can learn alone (no companions, no weapons);
- it belongs to the rooms, or to what came down from the Building.

| Entity | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Smilers, Hounds | Keep (already ours) | Wikidot's rules are ours: drawn to light / back away slowly, and stare down / don't run. Borrow two game ideas: Hounds can't work a door (Escape), and Smilers act as a light tell (Inside). Keep the gaze rules opposite. |
| Facelings | Adapt | "Routines without understanding" turns the Building's residents into a docile, touchy crowd. This is the strongest non-hostile idea in the canon. |
| Windows | Adapt | Immobile, psychological, and cheap. A window in the rooms that looks back up at the Well joins the two halves of the world. |
| Clumps, Dullers | Adapt (merge) | A clear reach rule. Their look matches the Samosbor's "assembled" masses. |
| Mimic / Predatory Doors | Adapt | A cell door where none should be. It turns our own canon (cell plates, wheels) into the tell. |
| Wranglers | Adapt as sound only | The rumble rule is good. Never show the absurd body. |
| Wretches | Story only | Your possible future, through M.'s pages. Never a model. |
| Stalkers, Familiar Face, Mannequins | Already covered | The grey figure, the Stairwell face and the Watcher hold these slots. Adding more would repeat rules. |
| Skin-Stealers | Motif only | The clear-blood test needs companions. The voice lure goes to the Building's door (below). |
| Deathmoths, Death Rats | Optional fauna | Rats as eyeless, sound-drawn ambience in wet levels. Moths read as B-movie. |
| Partygoers, Jerry, Chatterers, Neighborhood Watch, Wranglers' bodies, Lighters | Reject | Camp, meme or monster-movie. Wrong tone for "photographed badly". |
| Bacteria / the Lifeform | Reject | Kane Parsons' copyrighted design. |
| Crawlers, Bursters | Reject | Infection timers and acid sprays need combat or co-op answers. |

### 3.2 Samosbor beings that keep "nothing walks the Building's floors"

The Samosbor itself says the event is never seen, only heard. That suits our rule exactly. Three kinds of being fit without anything walking a gallery:

1. **Behind doors and walls.** The voices begging at the hermetic door (Mimics, нелюди), and residents absorbed into the block who answer knocks through pipes. They are heard, never seen, and they act on the door, not on the floor.
2. **The cleaners, by their traces only.** Do *not* add a visible liquidator; canon says they are known only by the log and the marks. The Samosbor gives a richer vocabulary of traces: raked slime trails ending at a lift shaft, scorched patches, welded door seams, a gallery poured solid with foam concrete, the knock code with its answer on the door's plate, and an OZK cape on a hook inside a cell.
3. **What the fog leaves, down in the rooms.** The fog rises out of the void, from below, so whatever it leaves can collect in the rooms, where things may walk. Slime, assembled masses and the burrowing thing belong there. Keep it unexplained, per the community's pillar that the samosbor is unknowable.

**Note on our current roster.** Our Watcher (a figure under a dust sheet) now closely resembles Fandom's rewritten Partygoers (husks under yellowed sheets with a bloody smile stain) and its "Static" Mannequins. Our design came first in our own canon, but don't add a smile stain or other details that would make the link explicit.

### 3.3 Candidate creature concepts

**1. The Voice at the Wheel (the Building; heard, never seen).**
- **Look:** none. At most, the cell door's wheel creeps a few degrees under your hand from the other side.
- **Behaviour:** only while the siren sounds and you are sealed in a cell. It knocks, then speaks in voices the player has heard on these floors: a child, a woman reading a residents' notice. Later, once her pages are read, it borrows M.'s words. It asks to be let in and says the siren has stopped.
- **Rule the player learns:** while the siren sounds, nothing outside is a person. The true all-clear is the siren ending and the loudspeaker's tone, not a voice. Open early and the fog comes in.
- **Tells, for fairness:** it repeats phrases exactly. Its knocks come from too low on the door or from both sides at once. It falls silent the instant the siren stops.
- **Sources:** the door rule and the demands to open ([SAMOSBOR wiki](https://samosbor.fandom.com/ru/wiki/Самосбор), CC BY-SA); Mimics and the begging voices ([Samosbor Archive](https://samosborarchive-en.fandom.com/wiki/Infected), CC BY-SA); нелюди ([2ch](https://2ch.su/b/arch/2019-02-15/res/191363969.html), no licence); voice lures of Skin-Stealers and Familiar Face (CC BY-SA 3.0, credit 1000dumplings/AndromedaMapping1 and the Fandom page) as background.

**2. The Tenants (the Building's walls; never walk).**
- **Look:** under the peeling paint of a gallery, the faint relief of a face or a palm, as if plastered over. A riser pipe beside a sealed door.
- **Behaviour:** this upgrades "sometimes something knocks back". Knock on a pipe and the Tenants answer from a direction. They go silent shortly *before* the siren, earlier than any loudspeaker. They are harmless and never move.
- **Rule the player learns:** the pipes going quiet means the self-assembly is coming, so find a cell. A reply from one direction leads to a cell left unsealed, with supplies.
- **Sources:** people absorbed into blocks who knock in pipes ([VK, «Живые блоки»](https://m.vk.ru/@camocbop-zhivye-bloki), all rights reserved, so the motif only); people fused into walls and voices in radiators ([2ch](https://2ch.su/b/arch/2019-02-15/res/191363969.html)); Dullers' arms through walls ([Wikidot Entity 6](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-6), CC BY-SA 3.0, Stretchsterz).

**3. The Queue (rooms: the habitable zone and offices; docile).**
- **Look:** a dozen faceless people in house coats, slippers and padded jackets. They stand in a line along a yellow corridor to a shuttered hatch marked for issue, coupons in their hands. At a distance they are a row of silhouettes. Up close there is smooth skin where faces should be.
- **Behaviour:** they act out a routine they do not understand: shuffle, wait, hold out the coupon.
- **Rule the player learns:** don't cut the line, run among them, or shine the lamp in their faces; any of these turns every head toward you, and they close round and press you, a crush that drains nerve and blocks the doorway. Join the end and wait, and the line moves; at the hatch it opens once and gives Almond Water or a battery. A creature whose rule is patience, and a calm beat for the director.
- **Sources:** Facelings ([Wikidot Entity 9](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-9), CC BY-SA 3.0, credit Jo, Higher Being Love Cereal!, 1000dumplings, Nikuchan); coupons and issue points ([SAMOSBOR wiki](https://samosbor.fandom.com/ru/wiki/Гигахрущёвка), CC BY-SA; [Жилое строение](https://samosbor.fandom.com/ru/wiki/Жилое_строение)).

**4. The Sediment (rooms: the lowest and wettest, such as flooded offices and the poolrooms).**
- **Look:** a black-violet film on wet carpet and in drains, set with milky blisters that turn to follow your light like eyes. Where it gathers, a low mound rises with a dozen of them.
- **Behaviour:** it creeps only over wet ground, toward light and warmth. It can't cross dry carpet or climb.
- **Rule the player learns:** lamp off and it loses you. Keep to dry ground. Paper laid on it floats and marks where it lies.
- **Sources:** black slime with white bubble-eyes that gets under suits ([Последствия самосбора](https://samosbor.fandom.com/ru/wiki/Последствия_самосбора), CC BY-SA); slime that mutates legs ([Samosbor Archive Slime](https://samosborarchive-en.fandom.com/wiki/Slime), CC BY-SA); light attraction as in Wikidot Smilers and Deathmoths (CC BY-SA 3.0).

**5. The Assembly (rooms: dead ends of the utility halls and deeper levels).**
- **Look:** a knot of limbs and household things fused together and bent into one: a radiator rib, a slipper, the sleeve of a padded coat, a hand still clutching a coupon book. It is grey-pink and wet, with an eye here and there that has eyeshine.
- **Behaviour:** it stays put and drags itself along the floor with a wet sound. When anything passes within about 3 m, one long arm shoots out. Whatever it grabs, it pulls in, and then lies still for a while.
- **Rule the player learns:** listen for the drag. Keep to the far wall. Throw something (a bottle, a dead battery) to make it grab, and pass while it feeds.
- **Sources:** Clump ([Wikidot Entity 5](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-5), CC BY-SA 3.0, Kekcroc/TrailmixNCocoa); monsters made of meat, iron and concrete in any mix ([SAMOSBOR wiki](https://samosbor.fandom.com/ru/wiki/Последствия_самосбора)); flesh masses that pull people in ([2ch](https://2ch.su/b/arch/2019-02-15/res/191363969.html); [VK, «Самосбор: последствия»](https://m.vk.ru/@camocbop-samosbor-posledstviya), motif only).

**6. The False Cell (rooms: anywhere a wall is long and plain).**
- **Look:** a hermetic cell door set into yellow wallpaper where no door belongs. It stands slightly ajar, with warm light behind it and Almond Water on the threshold.
- **Behaviour:** if you step in or take the bait, the frame closes like a jaw.
- **Rule the player learns:** know a true cell. A real one has an `NNN-L` plate, a cold steel wheel and no light of its own; the false one has a blank or wrong plate, a soft wheel, and light that rises and falls like breath. M.'s pages can show her marking one with a folded X.
- **Sources:** Mimic ([Wikidot Entity 400](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-400), CC BY-SA 3.0, Poliacci); Predatory Doors ([Fandom Entity 63](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Entity_63), CC BY-SA); oval hermetic doors without peepholes ([SAMOSBOR wiki](https://samosbor.fandom.com/ru/wiki/Жилое_строение), CC BY-SA).

**7. The Window onto the Well (rooms: offices and the Lobby's edge).**
- **Look:** a window in a wallpapered wall, where there can be no outside. Through it is the Building: a panel gallery across the void, one lit window, a figure standing in it.
- **Behaviour:** the longer you look, the nearer the gallery comes. Nerve drains, and the view drifts toward the glass.
- **Rule the player learns:** don't look from close. Cross its wall with your eyes down, or paste a sheet of paper over it.
- **Sources:** Windows ([Wikidot Entity 2](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-2), CC BY-SA 3.0, u/glassydude101 and DrAkimoto); windows that show only other walls and pipes in the gigakhrushch ([post on the setting](https://friends.grishka.me/posts/635720)).

**8. The Burrower (rooms: utility halls and the electrical station; heard, never fully seen).**
- **Look:** at most, tiles lifting in a line and dust falling from the ceiling.
- **Behaviour:** a tremor in the floor, a sound like a rolling ball, then grit spraying from a seam. It is blind and hunts by footfall.
- **Rule the player learns:** when the floor hums, stop running. Get onto metal (gratings, stairs, a desk) or crouch-walk, and it passes.
- **Sources:** Wranglers' audible drilling and "evacuate if rumbling" ([Wikidot Entity 75](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-75), CC BY-SA 3.0, Stretchsterz); Concrete Eaters ([Samosbor Archive](https://samosborarchive-en.fandom.com/wiki/Concrete_Eater), CC BY-SA); Predatory Doors counting footsteps (CC BY-SA). KLETKA's Concrete Chewer is from a copyrighted game, so take the idea only.
