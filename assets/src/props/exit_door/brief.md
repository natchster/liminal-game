# exit_door

The brief (`docs/design/ASSETS.md`, *An asset's dossier*). Its
measurements' and palette's sources are in `references.toml`.

## What it is

The kind of door an office floor has at its far end, its exit: a flush,
fire-rated, solid-core door faced in red-oak veneer under a clear lacquer
gone amber (refs 1, 2, 19), 914 by 2134 mm and 44 mm thick (3'0" by 7'0"
by 1-3/4"), in a pressed-steel frame painted a warm grey, its face 51 mm
wide, its stop 16 mm (ref 13), 3 mm clear of the leaf at the sides and
19 mm under it (ref 17). It opens away from you, so its hinges do not
show: what does is a lever on its 87 mm rose with a keyed cylinder, at a
70 mm backset on the strike's line, 1024 mm up (refs 13, 15); an overhead
closer on the leaf's head, 311 by 89 by 57 mm, with its arm folded along
the frame (refs 3, 12); a stainless kick plate across its foot, 254 mm
high and 1.27 mm thick (ref 16); and the undercut, where the air is
drawn. The veneer is worn grey and
dark where hands pushed it, at the latch edge and the lever's height, and
scuffed low where feet did; the frame's paint is chipped at the strike.

Over it, an exit sign as offices have them: an internally lit box, its
face the white running man through a doorway on safety green and the
word EXIT (the pictogram ISO 7010 E002, the mirror of E001, with its word:
a real sign's own printing; no arrow; refs 7, 10), 400 by 200 mm in a box
430 by 230 by 55 mm, on the wall 170 mm over the frame's head (refs 7, 18,
20), lit from inside and lighting its corner green.

The Works' is the same door, small in the machine wall, the rooms' yellow
paper round it and a warm light over it; it is the one whose undercut
shows the warm of the yellow rooms behind it.

## How it came here

The canon: "Every level ends at an exit door, unless it is one of the
run's *anomaly* levels ... The door is the furthest place from where you
arrive, and it is found the way a real door is: its
green EXIT sign and lamps light its corner of the floor whatever the
region (the light carries down a dark hall), and it **calls**, every nine
seconds or so: air drawn under the door and the sign's relay ticking"
(`docs/design/LORE.md`, *The descent*). It is the door every office floor
has at its far end, kept as the rooms keep an office's things; who hung
it, and what is behind it, is never said, and nothing here says it.

The same door is the Loop's way in, "an **ordinary office door**, no sign
over it" (*The Loop*), and "The Loop's ordinary office door has neither"
(*The descent*: no sign and lamps, no call), and the Loop's second door, "in the side wall with
a small green EXIT sign" (its sign the same, smaller); the Borrowed House's
"**front door** (the level's exit) will not latch while the nest is lit"
(*The Borrowed House*); and the Works' way down, "a small wooden door in
the machine wall at the foot of its tower, the yellow of the rooms round
it and a warm light over it, and it is warm behind it: the yellow rooms,
showing through. It is the only place they do" (*The Building*): the
rooms' door, small in the machine wall. On the one floor whose way down
is the Stairwell, a steel door stands in its place (`steel_door`).

## How it fits

`PILLARS.md` 2: "real doorways, real materials": a real door at its real
size, its veneer, steel and stainless as they are, worn where people used
it. Pillar 4, *you are never shown everything*: it is found by its light
and its sound, as a real one is; nothing on it points the way. Only lights
glow: the sign, lit from inside; the leaf is lit by it and by the lamps
over it, never by itself; no light shows under it but the Works' ("the
only place they do"). Its sign says what a
real exit sign says about itself and nothing about the place (`LORE.md`,
*How canon reaches the player*: a real thing's own printing is dressing).

## What a hand does

What it always did: walked up to, it is opened and you go through it
("[E] ENTER THE DOOR"), heard as a door. In the Borrowed House it will not
latch while the nest is lit. Nothing else: it calls on its own, and no hand
makes it (the Loop's way in does not call).

## Where it would be found

At the far end of a floor, against a wall, where a floor's stair would
be: the wall furthest from where you come in, whatever the place there
was for, one a floor on every floor of the rooms; on the Building's floors
at the foot of the door's tower, and in the Works set in the machine wall.
On the Loop's floor its way in (no sign, no lamps, no call), and in the
Loop the side door you came in by and the one in the first room's side
wall (its small sign). On the Stairwell's floor the steel door stands in
its place. Never more than one exit door a floor.
