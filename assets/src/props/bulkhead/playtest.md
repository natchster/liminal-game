# bulkhead: why could this be here?

What the playtesters answered (frames where the layout puts it, a fresh
`placement-critic`; people's sessions, `docs/research/PLAYTEST.md`), beside
the brief's story, and the rules each difference became.

## The brief's story

A service tunnel's lamp: a caged glass jar on the crown of the Utility
Halls' corridors, never over a door.

## What they answered

**Round 1** (2026-11-17; 4 frames, seed 7 level 6):

| Where | Obvious | Could be | No idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| on the crown of a shoulder-wide concrete corridor | 3 | 0 | 0 |
| far down a dark corridor, at its mouth into a hall | 0 | 1 | 0 |

"It's the corridor's light: lamps spaced along the ceiling of a concrete
service corridor"; "a narrow concrete service passage gets a sealed
ceiling lamp so you can walk it"; far off, "a light at the corridor's mouth
into the hall, or the last working one of a dead run; barely visible, so
hard to tell".

## The differences, and the rules

- None to its story: its rule stands. The one "could be" is a lamp too far
  off to tell, not its place.
