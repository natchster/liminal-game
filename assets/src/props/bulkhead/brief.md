# bulkhead

A vapour-tight jar lamp on a tunnel's crown (`docs/plan/FACILITY.md` F3;
the research: `docs/research/facility.md`, *Light*).

## What it is

A vapour-tight "jar" fixture of the 1940s to the 1970s, nobody's make: a
cast-aluminium canopy 130 mm across and 70 mm deep on the ceiling, a
threaded guard ring, a glass jar 100 mm across and 160 mm long screwed
into it, and a guard of four cast bars over the jar to a small ring under
its foot (`references.toml`). In it, an incandescent lamp: a filament's
warm white. Its canopy painted grey, the paint gone dull.

## How it came here

The Utility Halls are "shoulder-wide corridors (1.6–2.8 m) in poured
concrete … a low ceiling (2.35–2.65 m), a caged glass jar lamp on its crown
here and there" (`LORE.md`, *Places*): the rooms took the shape of a
plant's service tunnels, and their lamps with them, as a bunker's blast
tunnel is lit by bare lamps from its crown (the research). Who put them
there is never said.

## How it fits

`PILLARS.md` 2, *familiar but wrong*: a real tunnel's lamp at its real
size. Its colour is its own (`STYLE.md`, *Light*), warm white and never the
window's sodium orange. It never hangs over a door: a lamp in a cage over a
door is a cell's (`LORE.md`, *The Building*), so within 1.6 m of a door
the layout made, the tunnel has a tube instead. A filament has no ballast:
what hums near it is F13's (`docs/plan/FACILITY.md`).

## What a hand does

Nothing: it is on the ceiling, its guard over its glass. It fails on its
own (`world::lamps`): its filament dims and comes back, never blinking.

## Where it would be found

On the crown of the Utility Halls' corridors, every few metres where they
are lit, fewer where they have rotted; never over a door, never on the
Building's floors.
