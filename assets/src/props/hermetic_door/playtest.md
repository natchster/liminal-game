# hermetic_door: why could this be here?

What the playtesters answered (frames of a cell from inside, the Well's
galleries, the knock's door, the Works' cell; a fresh `placement-critic` a
round; people's sessions, `docs/research/PLAYTEST.md`), beside the brief's
story, and the rules each difference became.

## The brief's story

The Building's shelters: the door of every cell, cut into its concrete to
be shut against what comes; and on the Well's galleries, every flat's front
door a shelter's, sealed from inside by whoever still lives there.

In concrete it reads at once: "a sealed door off a concrete service
passage, to a plant room or shelter"; "a works corridor's door to a plant

sealed themselves in, but two on one gallery reads as the game's door".

## The differences, and the rules

- **The Well's flats with shelter doors are the canon's wrong thing, kept.**
  That every flat of the block has a shelter's door, sealed from inside, is
  the Samosbor's (`LORE.md`, *The Building*, *Knocking*: "a door behind
  every one shut from inside"); the playtester's own guess, "the tenant
  sealed themselves in", is the reason the place means a player to find.
  No rule changes.
- What it took for a frame fault (the frame "stops about knee height")
  is the Well's wall: a recess in its panel with a ledge 0.6 m up, behind
  this door as behind the old one.
