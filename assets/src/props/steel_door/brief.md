# steel_door

The brief (`docs/design/ASSETS.md`, *An asset's dossier*). Its
measurements' and palette's sources are in `references.toml`.

## What it is

A hollow-metal door, the fire door of an office block's concrete stair:
two 18-gauge steel skins over a honeycomb core, 914 by 2134 mm and 44 mm
thick, its lock edge bevelled 1/8" in 2" (ref 10), factory-primed and
enamelled a flat grey, hung on three 4-1/2" butt hinges, their
centrelines 10-3/8" over the floor and 9-3/4" under the head, in a welded
16-gauge steel frame with a 51 mm face and a 16 mm stop (ref 11). Its
paint is chipped round the lock and along its foot, where rust has come
through (refs 7, 8); a cart has dented it low.

Three of them, each seen from one side. The floor's: from the side that
pushes, a cylindrical lever lockset on the strike's line, 1024 mm up (refs
11, 17), an overhead closer on its head (ref 13), a stainless kick plate
(ref 14). The stair's top door, behind you: from the side that pulls, its
three hinges and nothing to take hold of. The stair's bottom door: from
the side that pushes, a push bar across it, a rim exit device, its
centreline 1011 mm up, its centre case 203 by 70 by 60 mm (refs 2, 4,
12), its closer and kick plate.

Made up as a janitor's closet: at eye height on the floor's side an
engraved two-layer plastic plaque, 2" by 8" by 1/16", white letters cut
through black, that says STAIRS, where a closet's says JANITOR (refs 6,
18). Over it, as over every exit
door, the lit exit sign, and the lamps that light its corner; it calls as
every exit door does (`exit_door`): the disguise is the leaf and its
plaque.

## How it came here

The canon: "Some exits are a steel door made up to look like a janitor's
closet, with a plaque that says STAIRS ... The door **locks behind you**"
and "There is a door at the bottom, and it leads to the next level"; and
"**Knocking** starts on the door far above once you are deep enough"
(`docs/design/LORE.md`, *The Stairwell*). It is the door every concrete
stair of an office block has. Who made it up as a closet, and why, is
never said, and nothing here says it.

## How it fits

`PILLARS.md` 2: "real doorways, real materials": a real stair door, steel
painted grey and worn. Pillar 4: it is found as every exit door is, by
its sign's light and its call; its plaque says what it is only as a
closet's plaque would (`LORE.md`, *How canon reaches the player*: a
thing's own printing). Nothing on it glows but the sign over it.

## What a hand does

What it always did. On the floor: "[E] OPEN THE STEEL DOOR": you go
through, it slams, and it locks behind you. At the stair's bottom, by its
push bar: "[E] OPEN THE DOOR", and on to the next level. The one above
you is knocked on once you are deep; nothing on it opens it again from
the stair.

## Where it would be found

In the exit door's place, against the wall furthest from where you come
in, on the one floor a run whose way down is the Stairwell (levels 5 to
12); in the Stairwell, the door at its top behind you and the door at
its bottom.
