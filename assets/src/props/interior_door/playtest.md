# interior_door: why could this be here?

What the playtesters answered (`cargo xtask asset sheet interior_door
--kinds place_wall_doors` over forty seeds: it stands on one floor in
twenty-five; a fresh `placement-critic` a round; people's sessions,
`docs/research/PLAYTEST.md`), beside the brief's story, and the rules each
difference became.

## The brief's story

A flat's or a small office's door, shut in its frame against the wall it
would open onto, in a place that had rooms with doors; off its hinges in a
home's barricade.

## What they answered

**Round 1** (2026-11-16; 3 frames, seeds 1, 7 and 12):

| Where | Obvious | Could be | No idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| in a long wall of an office floor | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| on the face of a narrow box standing free | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| in a short wall, close to a corner | 0 | 1 | 0 |

"A door into a room off the open office floor, just where a door to a room
would be". On the narrow box: "a closet built as a box in the middle of the
floor, but narrow enough to be a door into a pillar". In the corner: "it
looks squeezed in rather than built there".

## The differences, and the rules

- No place without a reason: its rule stands. A door wants a wall as wide
  as its casing behind it and floor clear at both its edges: queued with
  the lifts' (`docs/plan/MILESTONES.md` 26), for the same reason.
