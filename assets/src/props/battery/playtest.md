# battery: why could this be here?

What the playtesters answered (`cargo xtask asset sheet battery`, a fresh
`placement-critic` a round; people's sessions, `docs/research/PLAYTEST.md`),
beside the brief's story, and the rules each difference became.

## The brief's story

A spare kept for the torches in an emergency kit, left out on what it was
kept on (a filing cabinet, a crate, a desk) in an office wing, a
maintenance run, a storage hall or the tunnels; fallen out of a torch
someone changed in the dark and rolled to a wall; swept into a corner.
Never where people wash, in a shrine, or in the Showroom.

## What they answered

**Round 1** (2026-11-15; 27 frames, seeds 4242, 77, 202, levels 5, 7, 9):

| Rule | Obvious | Could be | No idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| on a surface | 0 | 10 | 0 |
| on the floor by a wall | 0 | 4 | 7 |
| in a corner | 0 | 2 | 4 |

On boxes and desks, "set down by someone handling the boxes", "whoever
worked at this desk put a torch battery down". On the floor, a reason only
in the dark: "someone whose torch died in this dark room swapped batteries
and dropped one", "someone feeling along the wall in the dark dropped a
spare", "someone going through these dark concrete halls with a torch
dropped a spare". In a lit, empty lobby or room: "no reason; it is a
pickup", seven times.

**Round 2** (after the rules below; a fresh playtester, the same floors):

| Rule | Obvious | Could be | No idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| on a surface | 3 | 9 | 0 |
| on the floor by a wall, in the dark | 0 | 2 | 1 |
| on the floor by a wall, a utility hall | 0 | 6 | 4 |
| in a corner, in the dark | 0 | 2 | 0 |

"Dropped and rolled to the wall" now, mostly; "a maintenance worker
dropped it". Three of the no-reason frames saw a cell lying end on to the
camera as stood on its end ("as if put there on purpose"): the view, not
its place. The lit utility halls' rule gave no reason four times in ten
("a bare concrete floor with nothing round it").

## The differences, and the rules

- On the floor, a person's reason needs the dark: the floor rules of the
  rooms people lived and worked in hold only in a region with no light of
  its own (`dark = true`), a reason the brief had not given. In the halls
  the building's own people walked with torches (maintenance, the tunnels,
  storage) the playtester found one in the light too ("a spare dropped by
  someone going through the halls"): a rule of its own, at half the weight
  after round 2 (four in ten with no reason).
- Things "by a wall" sit at its foot; a lone pillar is not a wall; no two
  of a kind nearer than 6 m (as almond water's).
- Where its rules allow nowhere on a floor (the Showroom's, spotless), the
  floor has none of the floor's own (the others keep their own stories).
