# t11 notes: P3, the rare: a lodge's trophies, a parlour's ornaments

Five placers the census graded `cartoon`, made again as the real things a
hunting lodge or a parlour left behind, years after anyone dusted them.
The parts are placed as modelled (`prop_part_as_modelled`, a scale of one).
The placers live in modules of their own: `placers_p06/src/rustic/t11.rs`
(the antlers, the trout), `placers_p21/src/placers/t11.rs` (the roe's
head), `placers_p17/src/t11.rs` (the vase and its feathers) and
`placers_p25/src/t11.rs` (the fan); each keeps its draws call for call.

The census's verdicts: the antlers "a flat brown shield plaque ... two
straight cream sticks ending in balls. Antlers with no tines, no burr, no
skull cap" (review2); the fish "a smooth mint-green fish blob with a black
dot eye ... nothing of a varnished taxidermy fish" (review2); the deer
"an egg-shaped head, a round snout ball ... teddy-bear ears and stick
antlers ... a real mount has a long muzzle, glass eyes, a neck cape and
branching antlers" (review7); the vase "a green egg-shaped vase with gold
bands (it reads as a grenade) ... three straight dashed green sticks ...
no plume, no barbs and no eye" (review7); the fan "a flat, solid mustard
quarter-circle ... no pleats, ribs or painted pattern: a pie slice"
(review7).

## The parts (`t11.py`)

- **Antlers** (`Real__roeAntlers`, `…Tips`), one pair shared by the two roe
  trophies, in a frame of their own (the burrs' bases at the origin, the
  beams up +y, the brow tines toward +z): each beam a tube along its
  curve (out, then in at the top: a lyre), the brow tine forward from 55 %
  of the way up, the back tine from 80 %, the top; the burr a ring of
  nineteen pearls round each foot; the beam pearled (120 knobs a beam,
  thick at the foot, gone by the brow tine) and guttered (a noise along
  it); voxel-remeshed together. The tips are the last 17 mm of each tine,
  split off by face (rubbed white).
- **Trophy** (`Real__roeTrophyShield`, `…Skull`): the shield a heater
  (Wappen) 15 x 22 cm, its shoulders rounded, a peak at the top's middle,
  straight sides and two arcs to its foot's point, the front's edge
  rounded, a raised field moulded inside it; the skull plate of metaballs
  (the frontals' dome and the parting between them, the orbits' upper rims
  as arcs, the nasals' roots, the pedicles), sawn flat beneath and across
  the nasals. The skull's own frame (H) has the frontal level, the nose
  +z; the antlers leave it leaning back 52 degrees (`ROE_BETA`); on the
  trophy the frontal faces the room and the nose points down, so the
  antlers rise up and out (38 degrees off the vertical).
- **Trout** (`Real__trophyFishBody`, `…Fins`, `…Eye`, `…Plaque`): the body
  65 rings along it (a trout's depth and thickness profiles, the show side
  bulging out, its back a third as thick and flat to the board, the tail
  swept 13 mm off it), the gill cover's edge a curved step, the mouth cut;
  the dorsal, adipose, anal and tail fins thin plates (1.4 mm, their rays
  a ripple) in the body's middle plane, with the body (one map from the
  front); the pectoral and pelvic fins a part of their own (the body's map
  would paint them with the flank behind them); the glass eye a 12 mm
  sphere set in; the oval board rings of an ogee routed round its front.
- **Roe's head** (`Real__roeHeadShield`, `…Hide`, `…Pale`, `…Nose`,
  `…Eyes`): the shield as the trophy's, 19 x 26 cm; the head and neck of
  metaballs in H turned 25 degrees nose down (the neck from the shield up
  and out, the throat, the jaw and chin, the cheeks, the cranium, the long
  face, the muzzle and the upper lips, the orbits' rims, the pedicles under
  the hide), the nostrils and the eyes' seats subtracted, voxel-remeshed,
  the mouth's lines cut, the neck cut into the shield's field; the ears
  cupped leaves 10.6 cm long, solid 2.6 mm, a sheet inside each. The hide
  is split by face: the pale chin and lower lip and an oval throat patch,
  the black nose; the rest is the hide.
- **Vase** (`Real__peacockVase`): a lathe of 19 profile points (the foot
  ring, the baluster, the shoulder, the neck, the trumpet mouth), hollow
  (6.5 mm wall).
- **Feather** (`Real__peacockFeather`, `…Shaft`): the vane a ribbon 0.47 m
  (its herl widening up to the eye, the barbs swept back and drooping to
  its sides), dipping 6.5 cm toward its face at the tip; the rachis a tube
  along it (1.9 to 0.6 mm).
- **Fan** (`Real__fanLeaf`, `…Ribs`, `…Rivet`): the leaf 48 panels
  folded up and down in turn, the folds deepening outward, two sheets
  thick, cockled; 23 inner ribs (4.2 to 3 mm, 0.8 mm) stacked at the
  rivet and bending down to the leaf, the two guards (6.5 mm, 2 mm) full
  length, under the leaf at one end and over it at the other; the brass
  rivet and its two domed washers.

## The game side

- **Antlers**: the old wall pick and place (1.80 m up, 15 mm off the
  wall); the shield's oak (`oak_tex`: quartersawn grain and the rays'
  flecks under a dark stain, rubbed paler at the edges, the varnish
  crazed, dust; phong, dull), the bone (`bone_tex`: ivory gone yellow,
  stained brown toward the pedicles, grime in the sutures), the antlers
  (`antler_tex`: dark with old sap low down, the gutters darker, the pearls'
  crowns rubbed pale), the tips ivory (phong, polished).
- **Trout**: the old colour pick is the kind of trout (a rainbow: olive
  back, silver flank, the pink band, black specks over back, dorsal and
  tail; a brook trout: worm marks over the back, yellow spots and red ones
  ringed blue, the orange belly, red lower fins with the white edge and
  the black stripe; a brown: golden flank, black spots and red ones ringed
  pale, the adipose fin tipped orange; a lake trout: grey-green with cream
  spots); `trout_tex` paints it from the front in the fish's own metres
  (the body's depth profile as `t11.py`'s), with the gill cover's edge,
  the jaw's line, the lateral line, fine scales, the fins' rays, the
  lacquer gone amber and cracked, dust on the back; phong (a little gloss).
  The board walnut-stained (`walnut_tex`), the routed edge darker. The
  eye glass (`fish_eye_tex`: the black pupil, the gold iris; phong).
- **Roe's head**: the old candidate pick and `mount_on_wall` (2.00 m, its
  slide along the wall). The coat (`hide_tex`): a roe's winter grey-brown,
  grizzled, streaked, darker over the top, faded, a few moth-eaten spots;
  the pale (`pale_tex`, both sides): old white gone cream, the ears'
  insides grey-brown with pale tufts; the nose painted black and glossed,
  the eyes dark glass (phong): they catch the light, never make it. The
  old plaque stood 2 cm into the wall, so the settle pushed it out; the
  shield now hangs flush and stays where `mount_on_wall` put it.
- **Vase**: the old colour pick is the glaze, the old trim draw the gilt
  (`glaze_tex`, its noise wrapped round: the glaze run dark in streaks and
  pooled above the foot, pale where it breaks over the rim, the shoulder
  and the foot's turn, speckled, crazed with dirt in the cracks, three
  gilt bands and the rim's line rubbed half away, the bare buff foot ring,
  a chip off the rim, dust on the shoulder; phong). The old height scales
  the whole vase (0.89-1.11). Each feather keeps the old angle and shaft
  draws: its angle round the neck, and its length scales it (0.88-1.12)
  and leans it out (0.16-0.28 rad). The vane (`feather_tex`, alpha-tested,
  both sides): the eye pixel by pixel (the dented navy kidney, teal,
  bronze, green-gold, a dark edge, loose barbs), the herl stroked (130
  barbs a side, out and up and dipping, green-bronze, a few copper), the
  rachis.
- **Fan**: the old colour pick is the leaf's print (`leaf_tex`, painted
  on the flat leaf as seen from above: cherry blossom on rose with gold
  flecks; seigaiha waves on indigo; maple leaves on dull gold; bamboo on
  celadon; wisteria on violet; a mountain in ink on cream), each panel a
  shade in turn, rubbed pale along the folds, yellowed, foxed, the edge
  grimy, a tear between two ribs, dust. The ribs smoked bamboo under the
  dark prints, natural under the pale; the rivet brass.

Every painter mixes where its thing is into its seed (`salt`), so no two
floors' things are painted alike, and adds no level draw (the feathers'
herl strokes come from the salt, not the painter's own stream).

## Draws and evidence

On each prop's census floor and the others of the 272 where it appears
(the antlers 4242/9 and 5/10; the trout 1999/10 and 202/6; the roe's head
15/8, 15/11 and 5/10; the vase 17/12; the fan 5/10, 10/10 and 4/9),
`--dump-level` gives the same draws, the same `solidsHash` and every other
placer's objects the same (placer, pos, min, max, rot) before and after.
The wall mounts are no wider than the old ones (the trophy 15 cm for 18,
the head 22 cm for 28, the board 35 for 35). The vase's feathers repeat
within it and are instanced after the build (`instancify_static`), so the
dump's bounds of the vase are its own.
