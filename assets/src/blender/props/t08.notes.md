# t08 notes: P3, the rare: the party's and the shrine's Halloween things

Eight placers the census graded `cartoon`, made again as what a party
room or a shrine really leaves: cheap shop decorations, years on
(`docs/PROPS.md` 1, "the party and the shrine"). The parts are placed as
modelled (`prop_part_as_modelled`, a scale of one). The placers live in
modules of their own: `placers_p02/src/t08.rs` (the crow) and
`placers_p23/src/halloween/t08.rs` (the rest); each keeps its draws call
for call.

The census's verdicts: the crow "a black ellipsoid body, a sphere head, a
spike beak and a yellow dot eye ... a toy chick" (review3); the bat "a
smooth dark ball with two small triangular wing tips ... a penguin or
beetle icon" (review7); the cat "a ball head with ear nubs on an egg body
... the eyes are yellow dots that glow like lamps" (review7); the broom "a
wire-thin red stick ... five separate bristle lines ... a scratch on the
carpet" (review7); the cauldron "full of glowing saturated green liquid
that throws a green pool of light on the carpet" (review2); the ghost "a
sphere head on a cone body ... a game pawn" (review4); the pumpkin "no ribs
... the icon of a jack-o'-lantern" (review7); the skeleton hand "four
identical parallel bead-strings ... a rake or comb" (review7).

## The parts (`t08.py`)

- **Crow** (`Real__crowBody`, `…Beak`, `…Legs`, `…Eyes`): the body of
  metaballs (the body tilted breast up, the breast, belly, neck, a flat
  crowned head, the rump falling to the tail, the folded wings and the
  thighs in relief), rows of feather edges pressed in low relief across the
  back; four primaries a side crossed over the rump and the tail's seven
  feathers, each a cambered vane; the beak lofted deep at its root, the
  culmen arched down to the point; two glass beads; the tarsi and four
  toes a foot (three forward, one back), the claws into the floor.
- **Bat** (`Real__batWings`, `…Body`, `…Eyes`, `…Cord`, `…Tape`): the wing
  membranes as a bat's are built: the arm (shoulder, elbow, wrist), four
  fingers from the wrist, the membrane between them scalloped in from the
  tips and sagging between the ribs, the plagiopatagium from the arm and
  fifth finger to the leg (a Coons patch); the moulded ribs over them and
  the thumb's hook; the body of metaballs (torso, chest, a head with its
  snout and nose leaf, the legs and feet back), two tall cupped ears; the
  cord from a knot in the back to the ceiling, under a strip of masking
  tape with a corner come away. Modelled hanging from the ceiling (the
  cord's top at y = 0).
- **Cat** (`Real__catBody`, `…Eyes`, `…Pupils`): metaballs: the pelvis and
  haunches, the back rising to the shoulders, the chest, the forelegs and
  paws, the hind feet flat beside them, the neck, the head with its cheeks,
  muzzle, chin and brow, the tail round its right side to its forepaws;
  the base flattened; two cupped ears turned out; the irises (almonds,
  their outer corners up) and the slit pupils laid on the face as paint.
- **Besom** (`Real__besomHandle`, `…Twigs`, `…Twine`): the stick crooked,
  tapering 24 to 20 mm, three cut knots, its end on the floor and raised
  by the head under it; 62 twigs gathered round its end through the
  binding and splaying out (pressed flat underneath), every fourth forked;
  two helical wraps of twine.
- **Cauldron** (`Real__cauldronPot`, `…Bail`, `…Water`, `…Dust`,
  `…Wrapper`): the bowl one 2.4 mm wall (`vessel`), the rolled lip and the
  shoulder rib, three feet, two lugs bored for the bail; the bail through
  them, hooked, fallen back a little; the water a disc with its meniscus
  at 62 mm; the dust laid on the inside bottom; a twist wrapper, emptied
  and crumpled. Registered in a 'cyl' frame: its finish is laid round it.
- **Ghost** (`Real__ghostCloth`, `…Eyes`): a 44 cm square of cloth (31 x 31
  points) dropped in Blender's cloth solver over the form (a 92 mm ball on
  a bottle) and the floor, self-colliding; drawn in at the neck where the
  string was tied, refined and decimated; its faces turned out from the
  form; two felt ovals laid on its face.
- **Pumpkin** (`Real__pumpkinSkin`, `…Flesh`, `…Stem`, `…Tealight`,
  `…Wax`): the skin with its ten lobes and dimples, a wall 15 mm thick
  (the inside the skin moved in); the face cut straight through (exact
  booleans: two triangle eyes, a nose, the mouth with a tooth up from the
  bottom and one down from the top); the lid cut round the stem with the
  knife slanted in, fallen into its hole askew; then the whole gone soft
  (the top settled, the waist bulged, sunk round the lid, sags and
  wrinkles, the face's side sagged forward); split by facing into the
  skin and the flesh (the inside and every cut face). The stem dried hard,
  five-ridged and twisted. The tea light's aluminium cup and the wax left
  in it (4 mm) with its wick, on the inside floor (y = 0.0095). Skin and
  flesh in 'sphere' frames: their finish laid round them.
- **Skeleton hand** (`Real__skelHand`): every bone a lathe of its own
  profile (a base, a waisted shaft, a head), a little flat; the metacarpals
  fanning from the carpals (eight knuckled bones in two rows and the
  pisiform), three phalanges a finger, two for the thumb, curled a little;
  the radius and ulna cut square behind; the whole tipped till the wrist's
  stub and the fingertips both rest on the floor.

## In the game

- **Crow**: black polypropylene (a painted map: not quite even, rubbed
  grey in places, dust in it), the beak a greyer black, the legs, the eyes
  glass beads that catch the light (they had glowed). Turned to face +x,
  as the old one.
- **Bat**: the wings a dull black plastic (double-sided), the body black
  flock, the eyes painted dark red (they had glowed), the cord, the tape
  (polygon offset on the ceiling). It hangs from the ceiling (`WALL_H`; the
  settle pass carries it to the tile's ceiling, as before); the old sway's
  draws (60%, its phase and period) now swing and turn it on its cord,
  about where it is taped. Its squeak's place as before.
- **Cat**: black glaze (phong; a map: uneven, dusty, a few chips through to
  the grey resin), the eyes painted yellow-green and the pupils black,
  glossy; no light of their own.
- **Besom**: the bark painted along the stick (grey-brown, streaked, its
  lenticels, darker where held), the twigs, the jute. The old fourteen
  twigs' draws (length and lift each) are kept; their lengths' mean sizes
  the whole besom (+-4%). Laid from the handle's end along +x, as the old
  one lay.
- **Cauldron**: black polypropylene (a map laid round it: its parting
  lines, scuffs, dust on its shoulder). The old "glowing" half (its brew
  colour and flicker draws kept) now holds stale water, tinted by the old
  brew's colour (algae green-brown, grape gone wine-dark, a red punch's
  rust), its scum, a drowned fly; its plop is the old bubble's sound (a
  drip into it), from the same place. The rest are dry: dust in the
  bottom, two emptied sweet wrappers (a faded orange wax paper, a foil).
  No light, no glow.
- **Ghost**: the cheesecloth painted (yellowed grey-white, its weave, a run
  of the stiffener dried yellow, grime toward the hem, a tear or two cut
  out by an alpha test), double-sided; the felt eyes. The old halo light
  and its pulse are gone (a cloth does not shine) and it no longer rocks;
  their draws are kept (the 60% sway's two, the pulse's one). Its whisper's
  place as before.
- **Pumpkin**: the skin painted round it (a dull orange gone brown, its
  ten grooves, soft rot in patches, more on its sunken top and where it
  sits, white and blue-green mould, wrinkles), the flesh dried tan-brown
  and furred, the stem grey-tan, the tea light. Lit (the old 60%): the tea
  light burns, a flame (its own painted teardrop, turned to the eye) with
  a point light at it, its flicker from the old two draws; its light shows
  through the face and the lid's gap. Unlit: the tea light burnt out.
- **Skeleton hand**: moulded polystyrene gone ivory (a map: yellowed,
  grime in patches, dust), a little sheen.
