# t05: bits of the outside indoors (Props P3)

Eight placers of `placers_p03` the census graded `cartoon` (or would have:
they are on 1-6 of the 272 census floors), remodelled from the real things
(`docs/PROPS.md`): the twigs, the leaf litter, the moss, the grass, the
butterfly wing, the snail shell, the bird's nest and the spider's web. The
placers now live in `crates/placers/placers_p03/src/t05.rs`; the parts are
placed as modelled (`prop_part_as_modelled`, a scale of one, or a uniform
scale within the old size draw's 12-15%). Each placer keeps its draws call
for call (below).

## The parts (`t05.py`)

- `Real__twigA..F`: an axis bowed and zigzagging at its nodes, swept with
  bark ridges and raised lenticels, the base swelling to its rounded scar,
  lateral buds over their leaf scars, a side shoot or two with their own
  bud clusters, the tip's cluster of three to five ovoid buds; lying flat
  as it lies (its lowest point at y = 0). Three are snapped:
  `Real__twigWoodA, C, F` is the break's pale wood and splinters.
- `Real__nestCup` (a lathe of the outside, rim and cup wall, lumped where it
  was pressed, its rim uneven), `Real__nestLining` (the fine grass in the
  cup, faced into it), `Real__nestStrands` (64 dry blades wound round it as
  flat ribbons, a few trailing to the floor), `Real__nestStray` (70 short
  grass ends standing out of it every way), `Real__nestTwigs` (16 twigs
  worked into its wall and base, kept out of the cup), `Real__nestEgg`,
  `Real__nestEggBroken` (the blunt half of a shell, broken along a ragged
  line, 0.25 mm thick).
- `Real__leafBeechA..C`, `Real__leafOakA, B`: each blade a grid over its
  outline's envelope, lying in x-z with its midrib along x; cupped, wavy,
  folded, rolled under or twisted, and crumpled, by raising it in y only
  (no slope over 45 degrees), so that the top view the game paints in is
  the flat outline; the petiole swept from its base.
- `Real__wingFore`, `Real__wingHind`: gently creased sheets over the
  wings' bounding boxes.
- `Real__mossCushionA..C`: low domes, their tops furred at the shoots'
  scale; `Real__mossSetae`: nine setae, hair thin, each with its nodding
  capsule.
- `Real__grassTillerA..E`: a sheath and two to four blades, each folded
  along its midrib (three across), arching over, tapering to a boat-prow
  tip, kept off the floor; `Real__grassPanicle`; `Real__grassDead` (seven
  dead blades lying round the foot, flattened ribbons).
- `Real__snailShell`: a helicospiral (4.3 whorls growing 2.3 times a whorl,
  the section an ellipse 1.12 times as tall as wide, the last whorl
  dropping a little), its growth ribs and wrinkles; the aperture cut on a
  plane through its top, its outer edge and its foot (a look into it always
  meets the inside); `Real__snailLip` (the lip rolled out and thickened from
  the inner side's top over the outer edge to the foot, thinning to nothing
  where it meets the whorl before); `Real__snailInside` (the inner face of
  the last whorl and a half, faced in).
- `Real__spiderWebThreads`: 57 threads (guy lines to the wall and the
  ceiling, the tangle crossing every way, long lines across the angle) and
  five sagging strands heavy with dust, clumps of fluff on them; nothing
  into the wall (z = 0) or the ceiling (y = 0). `Real__spiderBody`: a dead
  cellar spider hanging belly up, its legs drawn in at the knees.
  `Real__spiderWrap`: two wrapped prey on their threads.

Every part is within its budget (the asset lint passes); 18,640 triangles
in all.

## In the game (`placers_p03/src/t05.rs`)

- **Twigs**: the old twig's length picks the twiglet and scales it within
  12%; its colour tints the bark (weathered grey-brown, brown, dark with
  damp, bleached). The bark is one painted map laid along each twig
  (streaks, lenticels, a bloom of algae). A twig that crosses one already
  down rests on it: it pivots on its far end and tilts up over the other
  (no new draw).
- **Leaf litter**: the old leaf's colour picks the leaf (three beech, two
  oak), its height which face is up (a third lie on their backs), its phase
  a little lighter or darker; one overlapping another lies on it. Each
  leaf is painted once in its part's top view: its outline cut out of the
  envelope (rounded oak lobes, a wavy or ragged margin, one tear in from
  the margin), holes eaten through and skeletonised round them, the colour
  mottled and darker at the dried margin, a few fungal spots, the midrib
  and the veins paler; alpha-tested, both faces lit. They still stir 4-5 mm
  in a draught (the old animator).
- **Moss**: a painted mat on the floor (a felt of shoots, their tips
  catching the light, green at its heart, browner toward a ragged edge
  that breaks up into small colonies, a faint ring of dried silt), from
  where it lies; the old twelve tufts are cushions (the size picks the
  cushion, scaled within 15%), their tints by the old colours, setae on the
  green ones in fruit.
- **Grass**: each old blade is a tiller (its height picks which), where it
  rose, turned as it was; a flowering stem on one in three (by its phase);
  the dead blades at the foot; a drift of silt painted under it. The blades
  are drawn from both sides and lit from above (a thin blade lets the light
  through: no side goes black). A draught stirs each tiller (0.025 rad,
  the old 0.5 Hz and phase).
- **Butterfly wing**: the old colour picks the wing: a small tortoiseshell's
  forewing, a peacock's forewing, a peacock's hindwing, a small
  tortoiseshell's hindwing (upper sides) or a peacock's forewing from
  beneath. Each is painted once in its part's top view: its outline (the
  costa, the apex, the angled outer margin, the tornus) cut out, its
  pattern (the peacock's eyespots, rust ground and dark hairy base; the
  tortoiseshell's costal blocks, spots and blue lunules in the dark
  border; the underside's bark-like lines), the discal cell and the veins
  out of it, scales rubbed off to the grey membrane at the edges and in
  patches, dust, bites out of the margin and the root torn ragged. It lies
  where the old wing's middle was.
- **Snail shell**: the old colour picks the shell: a garden snail's old and
  bleached, a garden snail's with dark flamed bands, a grove snail's
  (yellow, five dark bands, its lip dark), a garden snail's dark with damp.
  The bands are painted in the shell's sphere frame (`uv_for`'s sphere)
  along the sweep itself (the same formula as `t05.py`), so they follow
  the whorls; growth lines across them; the apex worn pale. The old "on its
  side" draw lays it on its side, else on its aperture; its lowest point on
  the floor, its middle where the old one stood. The shell's wall is drawn
  from both sides (its bands show through from within, as a real one's do).
- **Bird nest**: the old outer ring's first twig colour tones the nest; the
  eggs (the old 30% and its count) lie in the cup's floor, the second
  broken. Painted: the dry grass and stems over mud (one map, by the box's
  three ways), the lining's fine grass laid round and round (concentric
  from above), a faded robin's egg with dirt on it.
- **Spider web**: in the angle of the wall and the ceiling, where the old
  one was, its back on the wall and its top under the ceiling; the threads
  (a pale, half transparent phong), two painted sheets of the finest
  threads across the tangle and down the wall's front (from where it
  hangs: strands along the two ways it was spun, lines out to where it is
  fixed, dust in clumps, a faint veil), the dead spider in three webs of
  four and its wrapped prey in two of three (from where it hangs: no
  draw). It is marked `floats`: the placement pass never saw the old
  see-through web, and would have let the spider, the one solid thing in
  it, down to the floor.

## Draws

Every old draw is kept, in order (the comments at each say what it now
chooses, or that it is kept for its place in the stream). Checked with
`--dump-level` on all 272 census floors (the 10 seeds over levels 4-14 and
the 18 over 6-14) and 450 more (seeds 21-70, levels 6-14), between them
every one of these kinds several times (the nest on 12/9, 33/9, 43/12,
54/7, 57/13; the leaves on 16/11, 28/13, 37/7 and more; the web on 555/10,
10/13, 77/7 and more): the same draw count on every floor, every other
placer's objects (placer, position, bounds, turn) the same, and the same
`solidsHash`. (On a few floors the old nest was not in the dump's object
list at all: its identical twig meshes had all been instanced and its group
removed; the new one is listed.)

## What the look cannot do

- The camera's near plane is 5 cm: a shell or a wing seen from closer than
  that is cut open.
- A butterfly's scales are iridescent and a cobweb's silk catches the light
  in lines; here both are lambert (and the web's threads a dull phong), so
  neither glints.
- The leaves and the wings are alpha-tested cut-outs: their edges shimmer a
  little as they turn far off.
