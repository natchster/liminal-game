# r16: the kitchen and the hearth left behind (Props P2)

Seven placers the census graded `cartoon`, remodelled from the real things
(`docs/PROPS.md`, "How a prop is remodelled"). The parts are placed as
modelled (`prop_part_as_modelled`, a scale of one), each in its prop's own
frame; each placer keeps its draws call for call. Checked with `--dump-level`
on 18 floors (each prop's census floor and one or more where it appears
again): the same draw count on every floor, and every other placer's objects
(placer, position, bounds, turn, meshes) the same, compared with the old code
built on the same `prop_meshes.bin`.

## The parts (`r16.py`)

- `Real__wineGlassBowl`, `Real__wineGlassFoot`: the glass in two parts (cylinder
  texture coordinates, so the wine's marks map round the bowl and up it);
  `Real__wineGlassDregs`, the last of the wine dried to a dark lens in the
  bowl's bottom; `Real__wineGlassLying`, the whole glass on its side, resting
  on its foot's rim and its bowl's belly (modelled lying: a part turned in the
  game is measured by its turned box, and would be lifted or dropped).
- `Real__bottleBase` (the push-up, the heel, the wall broken off in five
  teeth), `Real__bottleNeck` (finish, neck and shoulder in one piece, lying),
  `Real__bottleShardA`..`E` (pieces of the 3 mm wall cut from the bottle by
  boolean, keeping its curve: convex up, on their bellies, one on its edge),
  `Real__bottleChips` (26 small chips round where it burst).
- `Real__kettleBody` (body, lid, the bail's ears, a dent in the side and one
  at the foot), `Real__kettleSpout` (seamed on low, open at its mouth),
  `Real__kettleBail` (the wire handle hooked through the ears),
  `Real__kettleGrip` (the grip on the bail and the lid's knob),
  `Real__kettleTrivet` (a cork round, 200 mm).
- `Real__panBody` (modelled hanging: its bottom on the wall at z = 0, the
  cooking face out, the handle up), `Real__panNail`.
- `Real__breadBoard`, `Real__breadCrust` (a dome on a flat foot, five slashes
  opened into grooves and ears, stale lumps), `Real__breadCrumb` (the cut
  face, caved in a few millimetres), `Real__breadCrumbs`.
- `Real__sewBed`, `Real__sewArm`, `Real__sewSteel` (every bright part),
  `Real__sewWheel` and `Real__sewWheelKnob` (both about the axle at
  `SEW_AXLE`, so the game turns them in a pivot), `Real__sewSpool`.
- `Real__woodStack3|4|5Bark`, `…Split`, `…EndL`, `…EndR`: each stack (the
  old loop's 3, 4 or 5 logs: a bottom row and one or two in its grooves,
  each upper log as low as the logs under it let it lie) modelled whole, its
  bark, split faces and two sawn ends a part each. The ends are a part an
  end so each end's rings centre on its own pith: `r16.py` prints each
  stack's piths in the end parts' texture coordinates (`_print_piths`),
  copied into `WOOD_PITHS` in `rustic.rs` (re-copy them if a stack changes).

## The game side

- **Wine glass:** the glass is `Patch::Glass`, as the windows' panes and
  the r05 mirror (the room seen in it by Fresnel, bright at the graze, clear
  square on; its dust from a grey map). The wine's dried film, its tide ring
  at the old fill level's height, the tears run down from it, fingerprints
  and the lipstick print (the old 35% draw) are a second, transparent layer
  over the bowl. The foot writes depth: the placement measures a prop by the
  meshes that write depth, and the dregs alone read as hovering (the old
  glass was dropped 55 mm into the floor that way). Knocked over (the old
  35%): the lying glass and a dried stain soaked into the floor in front of
  its mouth (a lambert decal, its own object as the old pool was; the old
  tilt's draw sets how far it ran).
- **Kettle:** the old colour draw picks cream enamel (a dark band at the lid
  seat, the classic enamelware), oxblood enamel or stainless steel; the
  enamel's map: mottled glaze, dust, a brown scorch at the foot, limescale run
  white down from the lid and under the spout, chips to the black ground
  coat rusting in their middles (most at the rim, the chime, round the dent).
  The old brass-knob draw makes the grip and knob turned wood, else phenolic.
  The steam keeps all its draws and its breathing animation, now a soft lit
  wisp (lambert, crossed sheets), not a glowing sphere.
- **Skillet:** seasoning near-black, scoured duller, flaking at the rim; rust
  where it was put away wet: a ragged pool of it low in the pan, blooms and
  spots, at the pour lips and in the hanging hole; the handle polished by
  hands. The nail's hole 1.93 m up, the pan's middle at 1.68 m.
- **Bread:** the crust's bake browns gone dull and grey, cracked; mould
  colonies (grey-green spore mass, white cottony fuzz, a yellowed stain round
  them, black pin mould) thickest toward the cut end and over the cut face;
  the board's beech greyed, scored by the knife, darker where the loaf lay.
- **Broken bottle:** the old colour draw picks green, amber or clear glass
  (`Patch::Glass`, its body colour a film over it, so the breaks and edges
  catch the light); the old tabs' draws turn the base, neck and chips; each
  of the old six shards' draws picks a piece (by its size and shape draws),
  where it lies (kept off the base and the neck) and its turn.
- **Sewing machine:** black japanning dusted and scratched, chipped to the
  metal here and there; the plated parts tarnished; gold decals (the name
  on the arm between scrolls, scrolls on the bed's front corners, a badge on
  the pillar) rubbed thin and crazed; the spool's thread colour from where it
  stands. The handwheel still turns slowly by itself (the old rate's draw),
  now about its own axle.
- **Firewood:** the old bark colour draw picks the species (oak, ash, elm,
  birch); the old per-log turn draws seed the grain; bark with ridges and
  fissures (birch: white with lenticels), split faces fibrous and grey, the
  sawn ends' rings round their piths, heartwood darker for oak and elm,
  checks out from the pith, the bark's band round them.

Every painter is seeded by something placed (its position, or a value of the
placer's own draws), so what is painted differs floor to floor without a
level draw.

## Evidence

Each prop shot on its census floor with the census camera and one or two
close cameras of its own (the census camera frames the glass, the bottle
and the sewing machine small), the same cameras on the old build and the
new; and from four sides on further floors for its other variants (the
cream, oxblood and steel kettles, with and without the trivet; the glass
standing and knocked over; green, amber and clear bottles; stacks of 3, 4
and 5).
