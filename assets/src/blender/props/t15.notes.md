# t15 notes: P3, the rare: toys on the floor

Nine placers the census graded `cartoon`, made again as the real toys they
stand for (`docs/PROPS.md` 1, "toys stay toys": moulded vinyl or plastic,
turned wood, faded, scuffed, in the muted colours a toy has after years on
a floor; no maker's name, no character), after batch `r17`'s toys and
`r03`'s duck. The parts are placed as modelled (`prop_part_as_modelled`, a
scale of one). Each placer keeps its draws call for call; each moved into
a module of batch `t15` beside its crate's file (`placers_p05/src/toys/t15.rs`,
`placers_p24/src/toys/t15.rs`, `placers_p24/src/games/t15.rs`,
`placers_p07/src/t15.rs`, `placers_p12/src/t15.rs`).

The census's verdicts: the action figure "a ball on a block, no sculpt or
paint detail; a placeholder mannequin" (review2); the toy dinosaur "a
purple blob body with stub legs and a cone" (review3); the small toy
dinosaur "it reads as a bird or seal blob, not a dinosaur" (review6); the
spinning top "a green cone with a rainbow pie-chart", standing on its
point (review7); the princess wand "a thin gold stick with a flat pale
star" whose star glowed (basic material) (review3); the baby rattle "a bead
on a pin... no handle a hand could hold" (review4); the teething ring "a
thin grey wire ring... reads as an empty magnifying-glass frame. A teether
is chunky, soft, gel-filled plastic" (review1); the pet toy "a wood-grain
sphere set in a thin grey ring" (review3); the toy microphone "a black
sphere on a blue cylinder, with no grille, band or cable" (review6).

## The parts (`t15.py`)

Built in millimetres and scaled to metres as each part is finished.

- `Real__actionFigure{Body,Skin,Kit,Helmet,Eyes}{Stand,Lie}`: the torso,
  pelvis, collar and chest pockets lofted from superellipse sections; each
  thigh (a cargo pocket on it), shin, upper arm and forearm its own shell,
  so the joints read as the cuts they are (the waist's O-ring gap, the
  T-hip, the knee and elbow); the forearms swivelled forward in the battle
  grip. The head lofted (jaw, cheeks, crown) with a nose, a brow, a chin,
  ears and a neck; the fists bored for a weapon's grip (an exact boolean).
  The boots (a shaft and a lofted foot, toed out) and the belt, buckle and
  pouches. The helmet a lathed steel pot with its rim, tilted back. The eyes
  two dots of paint laid on the face by a ray. Each part twice: standing on
  its boots, and fallen on its back (turned about x until the helmet and
  the heels lie level, resting there), so the game turns it only about y.
- `Real__smallTrexBody`, `Real__smallTrexTeeth`, `Real__smallTrexEyes`;
  `Real__toyStegoBody`, `Real__toyStegoPlates`, `Real__toyStegoEyes`;
  `Real__toyBronto{Body,Eyes}`: each body a metaball sculpt (the spine a
  chain of ellipsoids close along a Catmull-Rom curve, each one shrunk by
  what a chain that close swells, measured: 1.33 at a third of its radius;
  the legs, arms, toes, neck and head ellipsoids and capsules), relaxed,
  its skin pushed into shallow folds by noise, decimated, its soles pressed
  flat on the floor. The tyrannosaurus's mouth carved open, its teeth cones
  set along both jaws where rays from inside the mouth meet them. The
  stegosaurus's plates two staggered rows of rounded diamonds, thick at the
  root and thin at the edge, leaning out, sunk into the back where a ray
  meets it; four tail spikes. Blender holds a metaball element's size under
  20 units (a longer capsule is cut short), so `meta` can build in larger
  units and scale back (the bone's shaft).
- `Real__spinTop{Wood,PaintA,PaintB,PaintC,Lines}`: one lathe profile (the
  turned point, the cone, the rounded shoulder, the domed top, the stem)
  cut into the bands painted on it, each band an open strip of the lathe
  with a profile point exactly at its edges; lying as it fell, turned about
  z until its tip and its shoulder both touch.
- `Real__fairyWand{Star,Rod,Knot}`, `Real__fairyWandRibbon{0,1,2}{S,M,L}`:
  the star faceted (each arm's ridge rising to a raised centre, the edge
  1.4 mm thick), the rod's foot 6 mm into its socket; the knot round the
  rod under the star; each ribbon a thin slab (both faces) from the knot,
  off the rod to its own side and down to the floor, then lying in a loose
  curve fanned off the rod, a slight twist where it turned over, its cut
  end curled up; three fans, each at three lengths.
- `Real__babyRattle{Ball,Handle}`: the ball with eight shallow flutes about
  its axis and the halves' seam; the handle lathed (a socket flaring into
  the ball, a waist, a swell), the ring, the gate's cap; tipped about z
  until the ball and the ring's far edge both touch.
- `Real__teethingRing`: an oval-section ring pressed a little flat where
  it lies, its two weld flanges, two sectors ribbed round the tube and two
  of nubs on its top.
- `Real__petBone`, `Real__petBoneSeam`: the bone a metaball shaft and
  four knobs, flattened, tooth dents pressed in, the squeaker's pip on
  top; the seam the bone cut level at its middle (bmesh bisect) and its
  outline followed a shade outside it. `Real__petBall`, `Real__petBallBand`:
  the ball its seam ridge on a tilted great circle, fourteen tooth pocks
  and a gouge pushed in; the band a stripe 8 mm wide on another great
  circle, a shade proud.
- `Real__toyMic{Grille,Band,Handle,Knob}`: the grille a sphere, a band
  round its middle, the handle lathed (the collar, a taper, three grip
  rings, the rounded butt) with the switch's plate and its slot cut, the
  slider; rolled 35 degrees about its length and tipped until the grille
  and the butt both touch.

## In the game

Every surface is painted at its real scale (the texture laid on from the
side each vertex faces most, a tile a few centimetres across, repeating):
a per-pixel painter (`paint_tile`, `par_rows`, no canvas call in the loop)
whose seed mixes in where the thing lies, so each floor's is its own.
Moulded plastic is faded unevenly, grimed in soft patches, scuffed in
patches of fine scratches and dusty; paint (the figure's boots and helmet,
the top's bands, the microphone's silver, the wand's glitter) is rubbed
through in small patches to what is under it.

- **Action figure**: the old colour picks the uniform's plastic (a flight
  suit's navy, olive drab, a faded red, grey, desert tan) and its helmet's
  paint; the flesh plastic of the head and hands; the boots and belt black,
  rubbed to the uniform's plastic; the helmet's paint rubbed to the flesh
  plastic under it; the eyes dark paint. The old 60% fallen picks the
  `Lie` parts, else `Stand`.
- **Toy dinosaur**: the old colour picks the PVC (green, blue, rust,
  mustard, purple, faded), its scales a cell pattern darkened along their
  edges; the old 50% back plates pick the stegosaurus, else the
  brontosaurus.
- **Small toy dinosaur**: the old colour picks the PVC; the teeth an
  off-white paint, the eyes dark.
- **Spinning top**: the old cap colour picks the first paint, the next two
  following it round the old rainbow (muted); the paint chipped to the
  beech; the beech's fine grain under the lacquer. The old 60% spinning and
  its rate are drawn and dropped: a top on the floor of an empty building
  has stopped and fallen (the census found it on its point). Its heading
  comes from where it lies (the old top drew none).
- **Princess wand**: the old star colour picks the glitter (gold, a pinkish
  silver, rose, blue): flakes catching the light and dark ones, rubbed off
  in patches; the rod white plastic yellowed, its foil stripe in the
  star's colour, worn; each ribbon's old colour picks its satin and its old
  length which of the three lengths it is. Lit (`phong!`): the old star was
  unlit and glowed.
- **Baby rattle**: the old pastel picks the ball's (faded), a few printed
  stars on it rubbed half away; the handle cream gone yellow.
- **Teething ring**: the old pastel picks the PVC (faded, glossy); the
  water inside gone cloudy, black mould in clusters of specks.
- **Pet toy**: the old bone colour picks the vinyl (yellowed), tooth
  scrapes and grime where it was held; the old ball colour the rubber
  (faded), and the old 40% white stripe the off-white band.
- **Toy microphone**: the old handle colour picks the plastic; the grille's
  holes 2 mm apart in staggered rows, its silver rubbed through to the grey;
  the band and the slider dark.

## Draws

Every placer makes the old calls in the old order, no more and no fewer:
the chance, the tile, the colour pick(s), the old 60% fallen (the figure),
the old back plates (the toy dinosaur), the spinning chance and its rate
(the top), each ribbon's colour and length (the wand), the old tooth marks'
count and each mark's two or three draws, the old roll and the old stripe
(the pet toy), the old wobble's phase (the teething ring), the position,
the turn; their values pick the new variants, or are dropped with a
comment. The painters draw nothing from the level's stream. Checked with
`--dump-level` against the old build on each prop's census floor (99/5,
1999/9, 202/8, 3/7, 77/8, 77/9, 31337/9, 11/11) and on the 25 other census
floors where one of them appears (1999/7, 20/8, 5/12 and 6/14 (the figure
fallen), 13/12 and 77/14 (the brontosaurus), 18/12, 15/7, 19/14, 13/6 (the
bone), 2/12, 10/8, 13/11, 1/11, 20/13, 303/9, 14/10, 18/10, 20/14, 9/14,
13/9, 18/14, 1999/8, 202/11, 9/12): the same draw count on each, and every
other placer's objects (pos, min, max, rot, kind, meshes, interact), the
marks, the entities, the exit, the player and `counts.solidsHash` the
same; only these placers' own objects differ (on 77/14 and 18/14 the
vintage map's too, from batch `t02`'s parts, which the old build's mesh
file did not have yet: its place is the same).

## Left as it was, and what changed beside the prop

- The teething ring's old wobble and the spinning top's old spin were
  animators (`g.animate`); they are gone (the draws kept), so
  `counts.animators` is one less on a floor with a teething ring or a
  spinning top that spun, and the ring is now a static prop (`interact`
  false in the dumps).
- The wand's old ribbons trailed from the stick's far end; a real wand's
  are tied under its star.
- The old teething ring's stub handle is gone: a water-filled teether is
  a ring.
