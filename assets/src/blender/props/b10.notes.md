Other:

- Wall placers, all 14 of them (FramedRecipes, AbstractLandscapes, StillLifes, KitchenClocks, HangingColanders, HerbSieves, EmbroideryHoops, CrossStitchSamplers, Family/Baby/Wedding/GraduationPhotos, BirthAnnouncements, VintagePostcards): they put the group at `wx(x) + dx * 0.485` (0.487 / 0.483 for some) from the tile centre, and with CELL = 4 that is not at the wall face. The face (+Z) does point into the room. A ray check on level 4, seeds 5 and 7 (private build, `propDump` walls, 28 placements) found the frame's back against a wall (1.4–2.9 cm from its centre) in 16 cases. In 12 cases the nearest wall behind it was 0.9–3.5 m away, so the prop hangs in mid-room at eye height. Examples: seed 7 FamilyPhotos 2.98 m, WeddingPhotos 3.46 m, VintagePostcards 1.53 m, BirthAnnouncements 1.53 m, HangingColanders 1.6 m, CrossStitchSamplers 0.88 m; seed 5 HerbSieves 3.17 m, EmbroideryHoops 3.44 m, BabyPhotos 3.44 m. The gallery also showed a kitchen clock floating in a corridor with its back to the camera (seed 5), and a postcard frame standing free (seed 7). These props sit 0.62 tiles from the tile centre, outside settleProps' 0.2-tile reach, so they are never snapped to a face.
