# t14 notes: P3, the rare: a play area's soft toys and dolls

Seven placers the census graded `cartoon`, made again as the real soft
toys and dolls a play area leaves, found where they fell (`docs/LORE.md`,
the Maze keeps what it collected; `docs/PILLARS.md` 2, real objects, not
toys): never posed for a scare, never gore. A doll's head or limb is a real
doll's part come off at its joint (a neck's rim, a limb's groove and lip,
the hollow dark inside). The parts are placed as modelled
(`prop_part_as_modelled`, a scale of one). The placers live in modules of
their own, beside the files they came from: `placers_p04/src/t14.rs` (the
mohair teddy), `placers_p05/src/toys/t14.rs` (the elephant),
`placers_p21/src/placers/t14.rs` (the teddy's arm),
`placers_p26/src/toys/t14.rs` (the plush teddy, the small doll),
`placers_p22/src/placers/t14.rs` (the doll's head, its limbs). Each keeps
its draws call for call.

The census's verdicts: the teddy bear "a sphere head with round ears,
cylinder arms and a blob body, in smooth glossy brown with no fur or
stitching" (review6); the plush bear "smooth, slightly glossy spheres ...
no fur, seams or sag ... a plastic toy, not a worn plush" (review4); the
teddy paw "a dark brown smooth egg ... three pale flat hexagon pads lying
loose on the floor ... a placeholder egg and three tokens" (review1); the
elephant "an egg body and ball head, one flat ear spiking up ... reads as a
pig or rabbit" (review7); the small doll "a sphere head with two dot eyes,
stick arms held out in a T-pose ... standing upright" (review3); the doll
parts "white capsules with ball joints ... a red ring at the cut end. No
hands or feet" (review5); the doll head "a sphere, half black for hair and
half white, with painted blue dots for eyes and a red mouth" (review5).

## The parts (`t14.py`)

- **Mohair teddy** (`Real__teddyBody`, `…BodyPile`, `…Pads`, `…Head`,
  `…HeadPile`, `…Eyes`, `…Nose`): the torso (belly, chest, the hump,
  the round tummy, leaning back a little, seams down its front and back),
  each arm and leg a piece of its own run against it as a jointed bear's
  are (the arms long, curved, the paws spoon-shaped; the legs out in
  front, big oval feet, their soles forward); felt pads laid on the paws'
  fronts and the soles; the head in its own frame (origin at the neck
  joint, where the game turns it): the broad crown, the cheeks, a long
  clipped muzzle, the gusset's two seams, the ears cupped and set across
  the seams; glass eyes close by the muzzle's root; the nose eleven satin
  stitches across a shield, the mouth a stitch down and two out. The
  `Pile` parts are the surface pushed out 1 mm, drawn stippled (an
  alpha-tested layer of fibres' ends): it softens the outline as a pile
  does, and none of it stands where the mohair is rubbed bare.
- **Plush teddy** (`Real__plushBearFur`, `…Pile`, `…Accent`, `…EyeL`,
  `…EyeR`, `…EyeHoles`, `…Nose`, `…Mouth`, `…Bow`): the body, legs and
  limp arms one sewn skin (metaballs, the stuffing lumpy, seams down the
  front and the back), the head its own skin sewn on at the neck, fallen
  forward and to its left; ears cupped; the velour muzzle, inner ears and
  soles; safety eyes (12 mm domes), the hole each goes through (seen where
  one is pulled out), a moulded rounded-triangle nose, the mouth's
  stitches; a 25 mm satin ribbon laid round the neck and tied in a bow
  slipped round to its left, its loops and tails laid on the plush.
- **Teddy's arm** (`Real__teddyArmFur`, `…Pile`, `…Pad`, `…Stuffing`,
  `…StuffingWisps`, `…Tuft`, `…TuftWisps`): the arm sewn of two pieces
  (its edge seam), curved toward its paw's inside, the paw broad and flat,
  cut aslant at the tear; flaps of the torn plush turned out round it and
  the seam's broken threads; the stuffing a soft cloud of small tufts run
  together with fibres standing out of it, a stippled layer of loose
  fibres over it; a loose tuft (placed three times).
- **Elephant** (`Real__elephantBody`, `…Pile`, `…EarL`, `…EarR`,
  `…Eyes`, `…Tag`): the bean body slumped on its belly, the haunches, the
  head with a domed brow, the trunk down to the floor and its end turned
  up, four stubby legs splayed with round soles, the seam along the back
  and over the head; a twisted plush tail with a tuft; each ear a D of two
  layers sewn round, floppy, in its hinge frame (origin at its root on the
  head's side, where the game hangs it); plastic eyes; the law tag folded
  in two and laid on its flank from the rear seam.
- **Small doll** (`Real__smallDollHead`, `…Face`, `…EyeR`, `…EyeL`,
  `…Wig` in r03's head frame scaled by 0.47; per pose A (on its back) and
  B (on its front) `Real__smallDoll{A,B}Body`, `…Dress`, `…Socks`,
  `…Shoes`, `…Lids`, `…Lashes`): r03's head sculpt (read from r03.py, its
  eye openings cut, its mouth closed: no bottle hole) split into the face
  and the rest; r03's sleep eyes; a wig cut in a bob, the fringe straight,
  hanging to the jaw; a toddler's body (the torso and its belly, the arms
  bent a little with mitten hands and a thumb, chubby straight legs), each
  limb posed at its joint (its swing about the joint's axis), the head
  turned on its neck, the whole laid on the floor so that the head and the
  feet both rest on it (`sd_pose`, which prints the head's place for the
  game); the dress fitted to the posed doll (a bodice, puffed sleeves on
  the arms, the skirt gathered at the waist and fallen out onto the floor
  either side, in folds); ankle socks with turned-down cuffs; one-strap
  shoes. On its back its eyes are shut; on its front, its head on its
  cheek, half shut.
- **Doll's head** (`Real__dollHeadNeckLip`, `…NeckHole`, `…LidsUp`,
  `…LashesUp`, with r03's `Real__dollHead`, `…Face`, `…EyeR`, `…EyeL`,
  `…Lids`, `…Lashes`, `…Hair`): the neck's end a collar over r03's stub,
  flaring to a rim rolled in (the head stands on it), the dark inside;
  lids and lashes for a head standing on its neck (the right eye open, the
  left stuck half shut).
- **Doll's limbs** (`Real__dollLimbArm`, `…ArmHole`, `…Leg`, `…LegHole`):
  r03's limbs' sculpt (metaballs: the fat upper arm and forearm, the elbow
  bent, the wrist's crease, the palm, four fingers moulded together and
  curled, a thumb; the thigh, the bent knee, the calf, the heel, the foot,
  five toes), each from a groove and a lip at its open end (a lathe), the
  hollow dark inside; lying on its side.

Blender holds a metaball's resolution at 5 mm or more, so `meta()` builds
the elements larger and scales the surface back down (its `res` is kept):
r03's and t08's small metaball parts came out at 5 mm cells.

## Materials and print (the placers)

- Mohair: cinnamon, dark brown, gold, white gone to ivory, a dusty rose
  (the old colours), its pile in streaks and clumps, its tips paler, rubbed
  to the woven backing in patches (on the head, the muzzle and brow),
  faded, dusty; the pile's tips' layer reads the same wear. Felt pads gone
  grey-brown, pilled, a hole worn through to the wood wool. Glass eyes
  (phong, they catch the light), black floss.
- Plush (the bear, the arm, the elephant): the old colours as plush
  (browns; the elephant powder blue, lilac, fawn, grey), streaked pile,
  matted flat in patches, faded, grimed, an old stain; velour cream or tan;
  black plastic eyes and nose scuffed dull; the ribbon a dull red or blue;
  the stuffing a white gone grey with dust, wispy; the tag's satin printed
  with the usual words of a stuffed toy's law label (no maker named).
- The small doll: hard plastic in the old skin tones (pale, tan, brown,
  pink pale), yellowed, scuffed; its face sprayed and brushed (blush,
  brows, painted lashes, a closed rosebud mouth, nostrils), rubbed; sleep
  eyes blue or brown; the wig blonde, mid brown, auburn or dark brown,
  matted; the dress in the old colours as faded cotton, gingham, sprigged
  or plain, grimed, stained; socks gone grey; black oilcloth shoes.
- The doll's head and limbs: r03's vinyl, yellowed, grimed, specked, a
  child's ballpoint; the head's face painted as r03's, worn its own way;
  the moulded hair in the old hair colours, muted, rubbed through.
- Painters mix where the thing lies into their seed, so each floor's
  differs; none draws on the level's stream.

## Draws kept

Every placer makes the old calls in the old order: the teddy (its chance,
the tile, the colour, its jitter, its turn, the head-turn's phase), the
plush bear (the chance, the tile, the colour, the missing eye and which,
its place, its turn), the arm (the chance, the tile, the colour, the three
puffs' four draws each, now a tuft's size, turn and place; the scatter;
the old tilt, now the arm's turn from its tufts), the elephant (the chance,
the tile, the colour, the ears' phases, its place, its turn), the small
doll (the chance, the tile, the skin, the dress, its place, its turn,
lying or not, now its pose), the doll's head (the chance, the tile, the
hair, tipped or not (now lying or standing on its neck), its place, its
turn), the limbs (the chance, the tile, how many; each its kind, place,
turn and tilt (now a little more turn)). Checked on each prop's census
floor and every other floor of the 272 where it is (`--dump-level`: the
level's draws, `counts.solidsHash` and every other placer's objects the
same).
