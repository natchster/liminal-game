# t21 notes: P3, the rare: four larger things

Four placers, never framed by the 272-floor census (rare, or instanced out
of its dumps), made again as the real things they stand for
(`docs/PROPS.md`). The parts are placed as modelled
(`prop_part_as_modelled`, a scale of one). What an earlier batch modelled is
placed, not modelled again: the lantern's wick and flame are t09's
(`Real__brassStickWickB`, `Real__brassStickFlameB`, its flame texture and
its tarnished brass), the aquarium's dead plant is r18's
(`Real__deadPlantStemA`-`C`, `Real__deadPlantFallen`), every glass is the
windows' `Patch::Glass` as r16's wine glass. Each placer lives in a `t21.rs`
of its own (`placers_p01`, `p14`, `p16`, `placers_p23/src/mystic/`) and
keeps its draws call for call.

What the old ones were (from their code; never census-graded): the lantern
an 8 cm box of two hexagonal caps, four sticks and four flat glass planes
round a cone of unlit yellow, a glowing halo sphere, on a 3 mm rod; the
desk a stack of boxes on cylinder legs, black slabs for pigeonholes, an
emissive brass; the aquarium a blue box of water with green strips for
plants and two to four cartoon fish (a squashed sphere and a cone each) in
toy colours, swimming; the stained glass a canvas of hexagons in toy
primaries with a yellow medallion and a cross, unlit (`basic_mat`, it
glowed), and a coloured point light in front of it.

## The parts (`t21.py`)

  `…Canopy`, `…Chain1`, `…Chain4`, `…Chain16`): its frame has the light
  (the flame's 10 mm over its root) at y = 0. The brass one part: the tray
  a square profile turned round (a moulded rim, its underside an inverted
  pyramid), the drop turned, the posts with cast knobs at their heads, the
  bands round the panes' foot and head, the roof a pyramid of sheet with a

## The game side

- **Lantern**: the old draws (the chain's length, the flicker's phase and
  rate, the slide off the tile, the turn, whether it sways and how) are
  kept in order. The light is the old light (0xffb060, 0.7, 1.5, decay 2)
  at the old height (lantern_y), now 10 mm over the flame's root; its
  flicker (the light's intensity, the flame's height from its root) is the
  old one's; the old 60% sway now swings the lantern and its chain from the
  ceiling's hook (the old one turned about the floor under it), with the old
  phase and period; `brass_lantern_positions` (its creak) is pushed as
  before. The glowing halo sphere is gone (a glow that was not a flame); the
  flame is t09's candle flame (a basic material: a flame). Its brass
  (t09's `tarnish_tex`, sooted up its roof) dull and brown; the panes'
  soot on their inside, heaviest over the flame toward their heads; the
  wax ivory gone grey (t09's). The chain is built link by link from the
  ring up into the ceiling's cup, so it follows each draw's length.
- **Desk**: the old draws (the tile, the wood, a roll in each of the six
  pigeonholes at 30%, the papers at 60%, how many and each one's place and
  turn, the inkwell at 40%, the slide and the turn) are kept; the wood draw
  picks one of four veneers (walnut, a mahogany stained dark, golden oak,
  red mahogany) under a finish worn through to paler wood, crazed and
  bloomed grey; the leather green or oxblood, its gilt line mostly gone,
  scuffed, an ink blot and a glass's ring. Each pigeonhole's draw (under
  0.3) puts envelopes (under 0.15) or a roll on a letter in it. The papers
  on the fall are half sheets of note paper written in a faded hand (no
  words: the strokes of a running hand), on the desk, not the floor. The
  brass's old emissive is gone.
- **Aquarium**: every old draw is kept in order (the plants' colours and
  blades, the fish's 60% and each fish's six, the bubbles' seven each, the
  light's 60%, the slide and the turn). Nothing is alive in it. The fish
  draw says whether water stayed (60%): if not, it is long dry, its glass
  ringed with a line of scale at each level the water fell through and the
  film between them, its gravel crusted white; if so, a little green water
  is left (the first fish's height says how much, 3-11 cm), a scum on it,
  the glass's rings above it. Each plant draw is one of r18's dead stems
  (its colour picks which), lying over on the gravel the way it fell
  (toward the side with room, its blade's lean turning it), scaled 0.88-1
  by its blade's height; the other two blades' places and turns are r18's
  fallen leaves on the gravel. Kept: the pump still runs (the old
  `aquarium_positions` hum is pushed as before), so where there is water its
  bubbles (each the old bubble's draws for size, place by the stone, height,
  offset and speed, at an air stone's 20-33 cm/s) rise from the stone and
  the old bubble animator moves them (registered always, as the old); the
  old fish animator (registered when the fish were drawn) now stirs the
  water's face where they break. The hood and its lamp only where the old
  light was on (60%): the old light (intensity 0.30, range 1.8, decay 2) a
  cool white (0xd0e0ea, not the old saturated blue) at the tube, 5 cm from
  the old spot (which was inside the hood's housing now; there its light
  burnt a spot on the flap); the tube a lit lamp. Dropped: the fish, their
  colours and their swimming; the toy-blue water.
- **Stained glass**: the old draws (the wall, the slide along it, the
  light's colour) are kept; the light's colour now chooses which colour
  leads the panel (amber border with blue jewels, green border with ruby
  jewels, or amber with green), the light itself is gone (old glass on a
  wall gives none: lit by the room, never glowing). The glass painted per
  piece: each piece its sheet's tone, cathedral ripples along its roll,
  seeds in the field's rolled glass, the black cement along the lead, dust
  on the flat cames' tops, the grime of years at the panel's foot, one field
  piece cracked across; phong (its sheen). The frame's pine walnut-stained,
  the grain along each member, mitred, rubbed pale on its arrises. The
  panel hangs where the old one hung (its middle at 1.70 m, its frame the
  old frame's 340 x 480 mm).
