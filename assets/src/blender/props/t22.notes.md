# t22 notes: P3, small everyday things (two of them common)

Eight placers made again as the real things they stand for
(`docs/PROPS.md`). None was ever framed by the 272-floor census: the
butts and the wet floor sign stand on many floors (210 and 85 of 440 probe
floors) but were instanced out of its dumps; the rest are rare. Their
floors came from a probe of seeds 5001-5040, levels 4-14. The parts are
placed as modelled (`prop_part_as_modelled`, a scale of one). Each placer
lives in a `t22.rs` of its own (`placers_p31/src/floor/t22.rs`,
`placers_p30/src/t22.rs`, `placers_p02/src/t22.rs`,
`placers_p19/src/t22.rs`, `placers_p23/src/mystic/t22.rs`,
`placers_p25/src/music/t22.rs`, `placers_p25/src/t22.rs` wired through
`#[path]` in `dressing.rs`, `placers_p04/src/t22.rs`) and keeps its draws
call for call.

What was there: a butt was a dark box and a pale box; the sign two flat
yellow cards with no print (a remodelled sign already stood in the core
props: batch `r01`); a swab a 40 mm stick between two balls; the clips
tori fitted with an old Gem part; candy corn three stacked cones in toy
yellow and orange; the boa twelve magenta spheres in a row; the maracas a
saturated ball standing on its head with a stick up out of it; the cube a
black box with 54 coloured squares, every facelet a random colour.

## The parts (`t22.py`)

Every part is modelled lying as it lies, its lowest point on y = 0: the
game turns it only about y.

- **Butts** (`Real__cigButtA..D`, 196-296 triangles each): one mesh a
  butt, lofted along x from its mouth end (rings of ten): the filter's end
  rounded, the tipping a shade proud of the rod, the burnt end a ragged
  ring closing to a charred stub. A stubbed out, its last 6 mm crushed
  flat and kinked up 24 degrees; B smoked to the tipping, its filter
  trodden oval; C dropped half smoked and gone out, its end burnt
  raggedly; D trodden flat. **Ash** (`Real__cigButtAsh`, 220): a column
  fallen whole and broken in two, four flakes.
- **The sign's hinge, split** (`Real__wetSignHingeHalf`, 152): r01's hinge
  (pin and four knuckles) halved between the panels, as each moulding
  carries two knuckles; the sign is otherwise r01's `Real__wetSignPanel`.
- **Swabs** (`Real__swabA..D`, 180-240): a 2.2 mm paper stick (six sides)
  and two lofted teardrop tufts, lumpy; B its stick kinked 32 degrees, C
  one tuft pressed small and twisted, D one tuft pulled off (the stick's
  end bare and frayed, a wisp of cotton).
- **Clips** (`Real__paperclipGem`, `…Open`, `…Jumbo`, 392 each): a 0.86 mm
  wire swept along the Gem's line (three U bends, its two ends inside);
  pulled open (the inner loop as it was, the big bend opened to a hook,
  the outer loop off it); a jumbo, its outer leg bent up 12 degrees.
- **Candy corn** (`Real__candyCornA..C`, 212-284): lofted from the base to
  the tip through a full lens section, the base rounded over, the tip
  blunt; B snapped at 72% (a rough break), C gone soft and slumped.
- **Maracas** (`Real__maracaHead` 1232, `Real__maracaHandle` 416): the
  head a lathe (a turned collar, then an egg 1.12 times as long as wide),
  the handle a lathe (a knob, two beads, the grip), both tipped 7 degrees
  about z as a maraca lies on its head and its knob.
- **Cube** (`Real__twistyCubies` 2808, `Real__twistyStickers` 756): 26
  rounded cubies with the gaps between the layers; 53 stickers (rounded
  squares, top faces), two peeling at a corner, one gone.
- **Boa** (`Real__boaCoreA..C` 122, `Real__boaFeathersA..C` 660): 75 mm
  lengths of boa: the dense fluff round the cord (a lumpy loft) and 80
  chandelle feathers round it, each a curling rachis with fine barbs (thin
  blades), leaning back along the cord.

## The game side

- **Butts**: one painted strip for all four (`butt_tex`: a row a butt, u
  along it from the mouth end, laid by x in the game): the filter's end
  stained, cork-mottled tipping (or white, one pile in four: a smoker's
  brand), its printed line where it laps the paper, the lips' stain, the
  paper gone cream and browned by tar toward the ragged black edge, the
  char and ash, grime underneath. Ash beside one pair in three. Two by two,
  a butt and the next are the same kind (the second a little longer or
  shorter, within 4%), so no butt is the only one of its kind on its chunk:
  every butt is drawn instanced, as the old ones were.
- **Sign**: the core's model, placed by this placer; its print (the core's
  layout: CAUTION, the slipping figure, WET FLOOR, PISO MOJADO, ATTENTION
  SOL GLISSANT) painted into the panel's own texture over the moulded
  plastic, rubbed and scratched, the mop water's grime at its foot (the
  core lays it on as a transparent decal, which would keep the sign out of
  instancing); two signs (how worn), the old turn's draw choosing; its
  strokes from a stream of the painter's own seeded by which, so a sign is
  the same whichever floor first made it. No wet patch under it (the
  core's is a transparent decal).
- **Swabs, clips, candy corn**: shared parts and materials, two by two
  as the butts (every one instanced). The swabs' strip: cotton greyed with
  dust, the paper creamier, the used tuft stained. Clips: bright plating,
  dulled, rusted through. Candy corn: its bands with wavy boundaries,
  glazed (phong), or stale (bloomed white, dusty).
- **Maracas**: the head's lacquer in the old colour (faded: red, blue,
  yellow, green, purple), chipped to the wood; bare beech, greyed. Both lie
  side by side, head to tail or heads together.
- **Cube**: the old 54 sticker draws turn its faces (27 turns: a face and
  a quarter, half or three quarters each), so a scrambled cube is one a
  hand could make; still solved when the old draw said so. Its sticker
  atlas is painted for each cube, faded, scratched, worn at the corners.
- **Boa**: twelve lengths laid along the old curve (the old 4.5 cm apart),
  turned along it, the three lengths taking turns (so each is instanced);
  the old dye colours faded. Its barbs drawn from both sides, their normals
  turned out from the cord and up, so it shades as a soft rope.

## Draws kept

Each placer makes the old placer's calls in the old order: the butts' tilt
draw chooses the kind (or the second of two's size); the sign's three
(where, its turn and which); the swabs' and corn's per piece; the clips'
two scale draws (which clip, its finish); the candy corn's, the maracas'
(the old tipped-over draw now chooses heads together or head to tail),
the cube's 54, the boa's four per puff (size, a nudge along the curve, one
kept, its turn). Checked: the trace marks (each placer's draws), the
solids hash and every other placer's objects of each placer's own floor,
and of all 281 probe floors where any of the eight placed (`--flags
noinstance`), before and after: the same, except 5012/5 where the sign,
which had stood 2 cm into a store's shelving (its old panels were planes,
left out of the placement pass), is now left out by it. With instancing
on, the same 281 floors list the same objects as before: none of the
eight is left in the world un-instanced but the cube, as before.
