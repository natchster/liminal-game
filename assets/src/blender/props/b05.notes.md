## Wall-prop note (placement, placer bug)

**placeAnalogClocks, placeFramedPhotos, placeDiplomas, placeThermometers** position the object at `wx(x) + dx * 0.485` (or `0.494`) and turn it with `rotation.y = atan2(dx, dz)`.
- These offsets assume 1 m tiles, but `CELL = 4`, so each object hangs in mid-air about 1.5 m from the wall.
- The rotation also points local +Z (the face) *toward* the wall.
- In game: the clock and diploma float in the middle of the room, and the gallery camera has to look at them from the wall side.
