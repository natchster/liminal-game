# t16 notes: P3, the rare: a play area's toys

Eight placers the census graded `cartoon`, made again as the real toys
they stand for, years on a floor (`docs/PROPS.md` 1, "toys stay toys"):
moulded plastic and rubber, steel and wood, faded and scuffed, in no real
maker's colours or marks. The parts are placed as modelled
(`prop_part_as_modelled`, a scale of one); a thing that lies is built
lying and the game turns it only about y. The placers live in modules of
their own beside the files they came from, and each keeps its draws call
for call:

The census's verdicts: the jump rope "three perfect concentric green rings
with two wooden handles sticking out on opposite sides, not joined to rope
ends" (review7); the skipping rope "a perfect, even ellipse with the two
wooden handles sticking out from opposite sides ... its two ends would be
together" (review1); the deflated ball "a smooth orange-brown dome with a
black asterisk drawn on top ... none of the creases or panel seams"
(review4); the tricycle "a red bar with a seat, a vertical post ... one
large black disc wheel and a second small wheel lying apart ... the parts
do not join" (review6); the skateboard "a flat, saturated-green capsule
deck with no grip tape or graphics and no trucks, on two white discs"
(review5); the kite "a flat hot-pink diamond ... no spars or frame"
(review6); the xylophone "full-saturation rainbow bars ... all one length
(a real one steps down)" (review2); the crayon drawing "a sun and four
stick figures ... all the same size and evenly spaced, in crisp
uniform-width lines that ignore the grout; no waxy, broken crayon stroke"
(review1).

## The parts (`t16.py`)

- **Jump rope** (`Real__jumpRopeCord`, `…Handles`): the cord laid as a
  plan wound three turns whose middles wander (so the loops cross each
  other), out of handle A and back to handle B beside it, smoothed and
  relaxed; where it crosses itself the later pass rides over the earlier,
  the lift spread along it (`lay_cord`); it rises into each handle's bore.
  The handles turned (a neck, the swell of the grip, a bead, a domed end),
  bored for the rope.
- **Skipping rope** (`Real__licoriceRopeCord`, `…Handles`): the PVC laid
  the same way in one springy loop that crosses itself, its two capped
  handles side by side (26 mm apart) pointing away from it; the handles'
  grip ringed, flared at the foot, open (hollow) there.
- **Deflated balls** (`Real__deflatedFootball{White,Black}`,
  `Real__deflatedBasketball{Skin,Lines}`): the football's 32 panels from
  the truncated icosahedron, each fanned and split, put on the sphere,
  puffed between its seams and sunk at them (each panel its own shell, so
  its seam creases); the basketball a sphere with its channels laid on as
  6 mm strips (two great circles and two side curves). Both gone soft
  (`deflate`): the skin above a tipped plane folded in on itself to a dish
  (a mirror about the plane, the fold rounded), the rim waved (the
  basketball's) and creased in lobes, the whole sagged, its bottom spread
  flat on the floor; the parts floored together (`on_floor`). The
  basketball's channels are found on the gone-soft skin and lifted off it
  along its outside (which faces up in the dish).
- **Tricycle** (`Real__trike{Frame,Plated,Rubber,Grips,Seat}`): built
  standing: the 10 in front wheel and two 7 in rears (a solid tyre torus,
  a rolled rim, spokes laced from the hub's two flanges, the hub, axle
  nuts), the crank axle bent out of the hub each side to its pedal (a
  grooved rubber block), the fork's blades from the axle up the steering
  axis (72 deg) to the crown, the steerer, stem clamp and swept handlebar
  with its grips, the head tube, the frame's one bent tube down to the
  rear axle housing, its struts, the pressed step with its ribs, the seat
  post and a moulded saddle; the steering swung 30 deg as it fell. Then
  laid on its left side at the turn that rests it lowest (`rest_pose`: a
  roll of 75 deg and a pitch of 14.6 deg, its middle of mass lowest over
  its lowest point), floored, centred.
- **Cruiser** (`Real__cruiser{Deck,Trucks,Bushings,Wheels}{,Flip}`): the
  deck lofted along its length from its section (a dished top, its rail
  rounded, flat below), its ends round, waisted a little, the tail kicked
  32 mm and the nose 10 mm; three ribs and three cross ribs under it. Each
  truck: the baseplate, the hanger cast across (tapering to the axle), its
  pivot cup, the axle, the kingpin and its nut, the two bushings (their
  own part), the axle nuts and the wheels' bearing shields, the four
  bolts' flat heads on the deck's top and their nuts below. The wheels
  lathed (rounded lips, the bearing recess). The `Flip` set is the same
  turned over about its length and tipped (2.25 deg) as it settles on its
  tail and nose.
- **Kite** (`Real__kite{Sail,Spars,String,Bow}`): the sail a bilinear
  grid over the diamond, lying on the spars and sagging to the floor
  between them (a plateau over each spar so none shows through), crumpled;
  a ragged bite torn out of its lower left edge (the cells inside dropped,
  the cells round it pulled onto the ragged edge, their edge curled up);
  the spars 5 mm dowels, the spine riding over the cross spar where they
  are lashed. The strings: the perimeter string bare across the tear, the
  lashing, the bridle on the face to its towing knot, the line off the
  kite's side lying slack to a frayed end, the tail from the foot through
  its bows, the snapped bowstring's loose end. The bow a tissue strip
  pinched at its knot, its wings puffed; the game ties three on the tail
  where the tail was laid (`t16.py` prints the places and the tail's
  way).
- **Metallophone** (`Real__xylo{Frame,Bars,Pins,Felt,Mallet,MalletHead}`):
  the frame's two rails splayed so each lies under the bars' nodes (22.4 %
  in from their ends), joined by end blocks, every edge eased; the felt
  strip on each rail; the eight bars (rounded corners, eased edges)
  stepping from 100 to 68 mm; a pin's domed head over each node; the
  mallet's dowel from its head's bore down to the floor.

## The game side

- **Jump rope**: the old rope colour (`choice`) picks the cotton's dye,
  faded (a mottled map with the braid's fine light and dark, grime); the
  handles beech under a worn lacquer. Draws: chance, `pick_open`, the
  colour, `jitter` (2), the turn: as before.
- **Skipping rope**: the old colour picks the PVC (red, blue, yellow, a
  yellowed white) and the handles' plastic to go with it; both phong (PVC
  has a sheen), a dust map multiplied over. Draws as before.
- **Deflated ball**: the old `is_soccer` picks the football or the
  basketball; the old patch count (3 or 4) how grimy it is. The
  football's PVC yellowed and scuffed (its black panels the same map on a
  near black); the basketball's rubber pebbled, gone brown, rubbed pale
  in patches. `scatter` places it (its three draws) before the painters,
  which are salted by where it lies. Draws as before.
- **Tricycle**: the old frame colour picks the enamel (red, blue, yellow,
  green), faded and painted with chips of two sizes to the grey primer and
  rust, a rust halo round them and runs below; the plating dull, pitted
  and bloomed with rust; the tyres grey rubber; the grips yellowed vinyl;
  the saddle dark red vinyl. The group stands at y = 0 (the old one was
  lifted 82 mm; the parts are built lying, their lowest point on the
  floor). Draws as before.
- **Skateboard**: the old colour picks the plastic (six, the black among
  them), faded, its moulded grip, scraped white at its nose, tail and
  rails, dark where feet stood; the old 15% upside down takes the `Flip`
  parts (no turn about z). Draws as before.
- **Kite**: the first old colour is the paper's, the second the bows'
  tissue and the print's ink (a near black when the two are the same).
  The sail's painter: a border line, a five pointed star and a ring
  printed over it, rubbed and faded, the hem darker, creases, a tide line
  where water stood, a shoe's tread across it, dust. The three old bow
  turns tie the three bows a little askew on the tail. Draws as before
  (colour, colour, three bows, place, turn).
- **Metallophone**: the bars' enamel laid on from above by texture
  coordinates set in the game (u along x, v along z, over the bars'
  bounds), so each bar's sides take its own colour: eight colours, faded,
  chipped round the edges and corners, worn to the metal where the mallet
  struck, scratched, grime round the pins, and each note's letter printed
  at its foot (the letters drawn on the canvas after its pixels, on the
  painter's thread). The frame pine, its grain along the rails. Draws as
  before (pick, place, turn).
- **Crayon graffiti**: the old plane (60 x 50 cm) at the old mount (the
  settling probes the wall across the same width; `solidsHash` the same),
  now `lambert` (the old `basic_mat` glowed in the dark). Its painter
  draws a child's picture: the ground scribbled green, a sun in a corner
  filled with the crayon on its side and rayed, a house (its roof filled,
  a door, a window with its cross) or a tree, two to four figures holding
  hands (round heads, dot eyes, a smile, hair scribbled, a dress or a
  line, fingers at the free hands), flowers, sometimes a cloud, a bird, a
  younger hand's scribble, or a band where someone tried to scrub it off.
  The wax is laid dab by dab into buffers of its own (pressure and colour,
  streaked along each stroke by the crayon's facets), then the wall's
  tooth decides where it caught (its pits show through), dusted; the
  pixels are filled on every core (no canvas call in the loop). Its
  composition comes from a small stream of its own seeded by the wall it
  is on and the level's seed: no level draw. No words, no arrows, nothing
  of hers. Draws as before (chance, the candidate, the mount's jitter).
