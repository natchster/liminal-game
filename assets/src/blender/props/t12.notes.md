# t12: what a party room leaves (P3, the rare)

Six placers of the party room, remodelled at their real sizes
(docs/PROPS.md, "How a prop is remodelled"; "the party and the shrine"):
cheap paper, card, foil and crepe, years on: faded, crumpled, dusty.

- `place_small_party_horns` (`placers_p01/src/t12.rs`)
- `place_small_paper_crowns`, `place_hanging_streamers`,
  `place_paper_garlands`, `place_small_pinatas` (`placers_p25/src/t12.rs`)
- `place_birthday_hats` (`placers_p26/src/t12.rs`)

The old functions are gone from `lib.rs`, `dressing.rs`, `music.rs` and
`party.rs`; those re-export the new ones under their old paths, so the
registrations are untouched. Every placer keeps its draws call for call
(the comments at each draw say what it now chooses, or that it is kept for
its place in the stream); every placer that registered an animator still
does, in the same cases (the streamers' and the piñata's sway, now about
their tape and their hook, and barely). What each new one chooses that the
old one did not (a horn trodden, a crown come open, which tassels are gone)
comes from a salt of where it stands, no draw.

Print is painted per thing in the canvas (`canvas_tex_opt`, its own
stream; per-pixel painters run on every core with `par_rows`, the text on
the canvas first), with a salt from where it stands so no two floors are
alike. The parts carry no texture coordinates, so the placers lay them:
the cones' print by where a vertex is round the cone and up it, the
outside and the inside told apart by the normal (the inside is plain card,
darker the deeper in); the crown's by where a vertex is along its card
(its ring's angle, and a bent point unfolded about its fold line), so the
printed jewels sit in the points.

## The party hat (`place_birthday_hats`)

- **Real one:** the common printed card party hat: a cone of 300 g/m2
  board, 7 in tall over a 4.5 in rim, rolled from a printed sector and
  glued along a flap, a 12 in round elastic, a foil fringe pompom at the
  point (custom printed cone hats, 7 in, cardstock, elastic chin strap:
  <https://www.qstomize.com/products/custom-party-hats>; retail sets run
  6.5 to 8.5 in tall, 5 to 5.5 in across, elastic straps 12 in).
- **Made:** `partyHatCone`, 175 x 115 mm, card 0.4 mm inside and out, the
  glued flap's 15 mm overlap and its edge, a V crease pressed into one side,
  the point bent over, the rim gone wavy; `partyHatTassel`, 56 strands of
  2 mm foil fringe, crinkled and curling over; `partyHatElastic`, the cord
  knotted inside through two holes 12 mm up and lying on the floor in a
  loose loop round the front. A hat lying on its side (the old 30%) has its
  own elastic (`partyHatElasticLying`: over the cone's flank, a loop on
  the floor before the open rim) and fringe (`partyHatTasselLying`: the
  strands under it pressed flat), modelled in the cone's frame for the
  game's turn (HAT_LIE, about z, and its lift HAT_LIFT, which `t12.py`
  prints as it builds).
- **Print:** laid out flat as it was printed (a texel's place on the
  sector it was rolled from), so the dots are round from rim to point and
  break at the flap's edge as a real one does: the old hat colour chooses
  the print (white dots on cerise; blue and cream stripes from the point;
  confetti on mustard; green stripes with fine dots; pink with dots and
  HAPPY BIRTHDAY round the rim in Arial Rounded). Worn: faded unevenly,
  rubbed through to the card in specks, the ink cracked white along the
  crease, dust over it and grime where the rim stood.
- **Draws:** the hat colour's pick chooses the print; the band colour's
  the fringe's foil (silver, gold, blue, pink); the position, turn and
  tipped draws as they were.

## The party horn (`place_small_party_horns`)

- **Real one:** a 9 in party horn: a card cone covered in printed foil, a
  plastic mouthpiece with its reed, a foil fringe round the bell (Beistle's
  9 in foil horns, foil or cardstock with plastic mouthpieces,
  <https://www.target.com/p/beistle-foil-horns-9-25-pkg-multicolored/-/A-1002732788>;
  the same with a silver fringe and a white mouthpiece,
  <https://www.walmart.com/ip/710296591>). The bell's size from the
  makers' photographs: about a fifth of its length (48 mm).
- **Made:** `partyHornCone` (the card cone, 200 mm, 48 mm bell to 9.6 mm,
  its foil flap's edge, a soft dent) and `partyHornConeTrod` (its bell half
  trodden flat, folded at two edges); `partyHornMouth`, its spigot in the
  cone, a flange, the tube pressed to a flat lip; `partyHornFringe` /
  `partyHornFringeTrod`, the uncut band glued over the bell's edge and 64
  strands (2.2 mm) splayed past it, those under it pressed flat. Built
  standing on its bell; the game lays it on the floor resting on its rim
  and its lip (HORN_LIE, HORN_LIFT, printed by `t12.py`). One in three is
  trodden (a salt of where it lies).
- **Print:** the old colour is the foil's (rose, blue, gold, green,
  purple; dark in diffuse, coloured in the highlight), printed flat as the
  hat's: stripes that spiral on the cone, stars, a dark diamond hatch on
  gold, dots. Worn: dulled, scuffed through to the card, cracked white
  along a trodden horn's folds, grime along the side it lies on, dust over
  the rest. The fringe is gold or silver foil, the mouthpiece white plastic
  gone cream.
- **Draws:** the colour's pick chooses the foil and its print; the position
  and turn draws as they were.

## The paper crown (`place_small_paper_crowns`)

- **Real one:** a party crown die-cut from foil card or printed card: about
  3.5 in tall, a band with points, adjustable by a tab through slots, 20 to
  24 in round (gold foil paper crowns, 3.5 in tall,
  <https://www.funcarnival.com/products/gold-foil-paper-crown-dz>; a locking
  band 21 to 24 in, <https://www.christmascentral.com/club-pack-of-72-gold-mardi-gras-with-adjustable-band-party-crowns-4/>;
  a band with 13 slots, <https://www.canadiantire.ca/en/pdp/crown-8422084p.html>).
- **Made:** the card 0.6 mm thick, both faces and its cut edge: a 30 mm
  band under eight points, 89 mm tall, 575 mm with its tab. `paperCrown`
  is fastened (530 mm round), set down on its band and trodden: pressed
  oval, a crease across the band, three points bent over at their roots
  (out, in, out), the card wavy; `paperCrownOpen` has come unfastened and
  sprung open in a loose curl standing on its edge, two points bent. Two
  in five are open (a salt of where it lies).
- **Print:** the old colour chooses the card: rose, gold or pink foil
  (phong, dark diffuse, coloured highlight), or blue, green or purple
  printed card; on each, printed edging along the band and up the points'
  sides, a row of dots, a faceted jewel in each point (ruby, emerald,
  sapphire), a pearl at each tip; the back plain grey card. Worn: the foil
  dulled, scuffed through to the card, cracked white at the bent points'
  folds and the crease, grime at its foot, dust.
- **Draws:** the colour's pick chooses foil or card and its colour; the
  position and turn draws as they were.

## The streamers (`place_hanging_streamers`)

- **Real one:** crepe paper streamer, 1.75 in (44.5 mm) wide off an 81 ft
  roll (Creative Converting's,
  <https://www.webstaurantstore.com/creative-converting-071031-81-classic-red-streamer-paper-case/286STRM81RD.html>;
  Party City's, <https://www.staples.com/party-city-crepe-paper-streamer-roll-red-am18202-40-pc/product_24678615>),
  twisted as it is hung and taped up in swags. Years on the swags have torn
  and each length hangs from its tape, the crepe stretched, its end torn.
- **Made:** four lengths (`streamerA`-`D`: 1.62, 1.36, 1.12, 0.92 m), each
  draped by Blender's cloth (as `r13b`'s apron): its taped end held flat
  against the ceiling (a collider), the rest let fall from the way the swag
  ran, the twist set in the paper (the rest shape), so it hangs as paper
  hangs, turning down from the tape and twisting; the crepe's crinkle and
  its frilled edges added after, the end torn ragged. Each is taped to the
  ceiling with `r06`'s masking tape (`Real__posterTape`), not modelled
  again.
- **Paint:** the crepe's grain (fine ridges across it), patches faded
  pale, a tide line where water ran down it, dust; over the old colour
  faded as crepe's dyes fade.
- **Draws:** each streamer's colour pick, length (the modelled length
  nearest it), place and turn, and its sway draws, as they were; it stirs
  about its tape (0.7 degrees), where the old strip swung about its middle.

## The tassel garland (`place_paper_garlands`)

- **Real one:** a tissue paper tassel garland: each tassel a sheet of
  tissue folded, fringed and rolled, its middle twisted into a loop on a
  twine (<https://liagriffith.com/tissue-tassel-garland/>,
  <https://www.lauraradniecki.com/how-to-make-a-tissue-paper-tassel-garland/>;
  kits sell tassels of 4.5 x 13.5 in, 16 to 20 on 9 to 10 ft of twine).
  Here 24 to 26 cm tassels on a 3 mm jute twine swagged between two pieces
  of tape on the ceiling.
- **Made:** three tassels (`garlandTasselA`-`C`: 40, 36 and 26 strands,
  C torn short), the strands draped by Blender's cloth with self collision
  from the twisted neck, so they hang in limp clumps; the loop round the
  twine and the twisted neck. Three twines (`garlandTwineS/M/L`, 1.55,
  1.90, 2.25 m spans), each a catenary sagging 0.21 to 0.29 m, its ends
  laid flat under the tape and a cut tail; the game hangs the tassels on
  the same catenary (GT_CAT, printed by `t12.py`). Taped with
  `Real__posterTape`.
- **Draws:** the span draw chooses the twine nearest it and scales the
  whole garland to it (uniformly, within 11%); the count draw is the
  tassels'; each tassel's colour pick its tissue (the old palette faded);
  the position and turn draws as they were. About one in ten tassels is
  gone from the twine (a salt; its pick still made).

## The piñata (`place_small_pinatas`)

- **Real one:** a small star piñata: a 13 x 13 in star, as sold
  (<https://www.target.com/p/red-star-pi-ata/-/A-1007963984>), made the
  classic way: a ball (a pot, or papier-mâché) with seven cones, four up
  and three down (<https://cutoutandkeep.net/projects/star-shaped-piata-classic-one-7-cones>),
  every surface under rows of fringed tissue glued on in layers
  (<https://sunnystitching.blogspot.com/2012/05/>), streamers at the tips;
  hung by a cord.
- **Made:** the ball 170 mm, the cones 118 mm (31 mm at the base); the
  fringe as geometry, strand by strand (10 mm strips): on the ball row on
  row hanging down it (`pinataFringeBody`, with the skin under it), on the
  cones from tip to base lying towards the base (`pinataFringeCones`, with
  the card under it); some strands torn off, all lifted at their ends. The
  tissue streamers from the tips (`pinataTails`, `pinataTailsB`, in the
  two tissues) draped by Blender's cloth against the body, so the upper
  cones' hang over it. Split open on one side where it was hit
  (`pinataBroken`: the papier-mâché's torn edge, ragged; `pinataInside`:
  the hollow, dark; empty), one cone crushed, its point bent over. The
  cord (`pinataCordS/M/L`, 0.45, 0.60, 0.75 m) over a brass cup hook
  screwed into the ceiling (`pinataHook`).
- **Draws:** the body and accent colour picks are the ball's tissue and
  the cones' (faded); the string length draw chooses the cord nearest it;
  the position, turn and sway draws as they were; it turns, barely, about
  its hook, where the old one swung about the floor.
