# t04: the washroom's fittings and two things on its walls (Props P3)

Seven rare placers the census graded `cartoon`, remodelled from the real
things (`docs/PROPS.md`, "How a prop is remodelled"). The parts are placed as
modelled (`prop_part_as_modelled`, a scale of one), each in its prop's own
frame (x across its front, y up, z out of its front; a wall thing's wall at
z = 0). Each placer keeps its draws call for call. Checked with
`--dump-level` against the old code (built with the same `prop_meshes.bin`)
on every floor where one of them stood among the census's 272 (seeds 4242,
77, 7, 202, 303, 1999, 11, 99, 555, 31337 over levels 4–14; seeds 1–20 less
7 and 11 over levels 6–14): 36 floors, the same draw count, the same
`counts` (`solidsHash` among them), marks, exit and player, and every other
placer's objects (placer, kind, position, turn, bounds, meshes, index) the
same. Each prop's own position and turn are unchanged.

## The parts (`t04.py`)

- **Mirror** (`Real__smudgeMirrorFrame`): r05's channel profile swept round
  406 × 508 mm, mitred, its hem over the glass, two locking screws under it.
- **Window**: `Frame` (all the painted softwood in one part: the moulded
  architrave on three sides standing on the window board, the board with its
  rounded nose and horns, the apron, the lining, the sash cut for its four
  lights and arrissed, an ovolo bead round each pane, the hinge knuckles),
  `Iron` (the cockspur fastener: plate, boss, lever and spur, screws; its
  striker on the lining).
- **Faucet**: `Body` (the flanges, the arms, the valve bodies, hex bonnets
  and stems, the cross body, the swivel, the drain plug), `Spout` (the L
  spout, its collar and aerator; the game swings it about its swivel),
  `Handles` (two four-arm handles, each left at its own turn), `IndexHot`,
  `IndexCold`, `Hanger` (the basin's steel hanger: its lugs, four lag screws
  with washers), `Waste` (escutcheon, the cut tube, the knurled slip nut).
- **Towel bar**: `Metal` (the bar, the two posts on their rosettes, the
  domed heads), `Towel` (draped by Blender's cloth: the folded towel's strip
  laid over the bar, gathered a little at it and pinned along its top,
  settled against the bar and the wall over 160 frames, relaxed, then made a
  4.8 mm slab with a rounded rim).
- **Spray bottle**: `Body` (lathed: the push-up, the heel, the shoulder, the
  support bead and neck; a cylinder's frame so its print wraps round it),
  `Head` (the ribbed closure, the shroud lofted from the closure forward to
  the nozzle, the hump over the piston, the trigger), `Nozzle` (the turning
  cap, its orifice and grip fins).
- **Hair dryer**: modelled upright and laid on its side as it fell (turned
  till the handle's foot is down too): `Body` (the barrel lathed, the handle
  lofted, its hang loop), `Grille` (the intake's rings and spokes, the
  outlet's), `Switches`, `Strain` (built laid: out of the handle's foot and
  down onto the floor, its ribs); `Plug` (moulded body, grip ribs, the
  cord's stub) and `Prongs` (polarized blades with their holes). The flex is
  the game's: a tube along a path from the strain relief's end
  (`HD_CORD_START`, printed by the build) to the plug.
- **Strobe**: `Housing` (the plate, the shell with its rounded front, the
  lens's seat, the window under the lens open to the reflector), `Lens`
  (eight horizontal prisms), `Reflector` (a trough), `Tube` (the xenon tube
  and its end caps).

## In the game

- **Mirror**: the frame bright-polished stainless gone dull; the glass the
  windows' (`Patch::Glass`, silvered: Fresnel 0.86, the room's light in it),
  its silver only beginning to go at the foot and lower corners; over it a
  film: a soapy cloth's arcs dried in wandering streaks and smears, the run
  of the last wipe beading at its foot, a haze and the basin's dried
  splashes over the lower half, toothpaste flecks, greasy smudges at the
  sides, dust along the top, the frame's lip seen again in the glass.
- **Window**: the paint an old gloss gone yellow-grey, crazed, chipped to the
  grey wood or the old undercoat (most on the board and the frame's foot),
  dirt in the corners; the iron black, dull. Each pane `Patch::Glass`,
  frosted (milk 0.9, no reflection to speak of), dust along its foot and in
  its corners, fly specks, now and then a web in a corner. The old 50%
  glow's draw now says whether a lamp is lit in the room beyond: the panes
  glow warm, brightest toward the middle of the window, and the old point
  light (unchanged) is there; else the room behind is dark and the panes a
  milky grey.
- **Faucet**: chromed brass dulled, tarnished and limed (a map the chrome's
  colour multiplies), the index buttons' red and blue enamel faded, the
  spout swung aside by an angle from where it hangs. The basin is gone: its
  hanger black and rusting, the waste chromed and cut off; on the wall a
  transparent decal of what it left: the paint its back kept a shade lighter
  with a line of dirt round it, the caulk along its rim torn away in strips,
  splashes and soap above the rim, a run of lime and rust from the left
  arm's flange, rust runs under the hanger's screws, dirt round the waste.
- **Towel bar**: the chrome dulled and pitted; the terry painted per pixel:
  the pile, the dobby border across each end (flat, ribbed, corded at its
  edges) and the stitched hem, the old colour (cream, pink, blue or green)
  gone grey, dingy and darker low down where hands were dried, a tide line,
  mildew specks in clusters.
- **Spray bottle**: the HDPE natural gone yellow, milky; the liquid left (a
  quarter to two thirds, from where it lies) seen darker through it, to its
  level standing or along its lower side when knocked over; the label
  (Halden Janitorial Supply; ALL-PURPOSE CLEANER, GLASS CLEANER, DEGREASER
  or BATHROOM CLEANER by the old colour; directions, a GHS07 pictogram and
  WARNING, 16 FL OZ (473 mL)) scuffed at its edges, a drip run from the
  neck, grime where it was held, dirt round the foot. The sprayer cream, its
  nozzle dark.
- **Hair dryer**: the plastic the old colour (pale pink, burgundy, blue,
  grey, black or almond), scuffed and dusty, slightly glossy; the grilles
  furred with lint; switches, strain relief, flex and plug dark (light on a
  pale dryer); the prongs brass-nickel.
- **Strobe**: the red faded toward its top and dusty, FIRE printed white and
  worn; the lens clear, the reflector and tube behind it. When it fires (the
  old animator: its timing, decay and draws unchanged) the light is a xenon
  white instead of red, and the tube and the lens light with it
  (emissive, from the light's own intensity, in the same animator).

the painter's own seed), never from the level's stream.
