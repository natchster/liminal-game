# b07 notes (office + industrial)

92 parts, 29 268 triangles, wrapping 97 primitives, 30 of 34 placers covered.
Every part is within budget. Checked part by part and in the game.

## Per placer

- **place_small_rotary_phones**: made `base` (bakelite body on a base-plate lip), `dial` (finger wheel, stepped rim, centre card), `hole` (dark finger-hole plug with a dished top), `post` (cradle prongs), `handset` (bowed banana grip), `cup` (ear/mouth pucks), `cord` (ribbed coiled cord on the 270° arc). Nothing left as a primitive. Placement is fine.
- **place_rotary_phones**: made `base` (body with a round dial seat and two cradle horns), `dial` (drilled finger wheel with ten holes over 270°, so the dark body shows through), `dialCenter` (domed hub), `handsetBar` (arched handle), `ear` (bell cups), `cord` (ribbed coil). Nothing left. **Problem:** in the off-hook case the cord only rotates about Z (`rotation.z = atan2(hz, hx) - π/2`), so it points up out of the floor instead of along the floor to the handset (seen in game). Minor: the on-hook handset floats about 7 mm above the cradle horns.
- **place_ceramic_mugs**: made `body` (open mug with foot and rolled rim, so the liquid and inside discs now show), `saucer` (foot ring, well, raised rim), `handle` (D-loop). Left: the two `circle_geo!(0.032)` liquid/inside discs (flat surfaces) and the stain (transparent decal). **Problem:** the handle is rotated `z = +π/2`, which puts its arc *inside* the mug body, so no handle is ever visible. It should be `-π/2`.
- **place_floor_newspapers**: untouched. Its only mesh is a textured `DoubleSide` plane.
- **place_floor_books**: made `cover` (hardcover board with rounded fore-edge corners and a hinge groove; used for both covers) and `pages` (concave fore-edge, page-bundle ridges). Nothing left. Placement is fine.
- **place_rusted_pipes**: made `pipe` (pitted pipe with end couplings, open bores and a mid coupling) and `bracket` (bolted cast support). Nothing left. Minor: the bracket is not rotated with the pipe and hangs mid-air under it, 4 cm off the wall.
- **place_junction_boxes**: made `box` (enclosure with lipped cover and side knockouts), `screw` (hex heads), `conduit` (EMT with connector nuts and open ends), `wire` (limp torn wire), `tip` (frayed copper strands). Nothing left. **Problem:** the up-conduit starts at y = 0.15 while the box top is at 0.10, so it floats 5 cm above the box (seen in game).
- **place_cardboard_boxes**: made `body` (bowed sides, flap seam under the tape, crushed edges; top kept flat for the tape). Left: the tape plane (flat quad). Placement is fine.
- **place_mops**: made `stick` (handle with ferrule, rounded end, hang hole), `head` (ragged string mop), `bucket` (open janitor bucket with ribs, so the water disc now shows), `rim` (smooth torus), `wire` (bail strands), `handleTop` (bail bar with grip sleeve). Left: the water circle (transparent). Placement is fine.
- **place_rolled_carpets**: made `roll` (one part for all three roll sizes): a wound strip whose spiral shows at the ends, inner wraps telescoped back, and a step where the outer layer ends. Nothing left. Minor: `roll2`/`roll3` `rotation.y = tilt` only spins the roll about its own axis in the Z-aligned case, so the intended yaw does nothing there. `roll2` sinks about 2 cm into `roll1`.
- **place_extension_cords**: made `seg` (round flex with domed ends and a slight wave), `plug` (moulded body, strain-relief boot, grip ribs), `prong` (chamfered blades). Nothing left. **Problem:** each segment uses `rotation.z = π/2` then `rotation.y = curAng`, which orients it along (−cos a, 0, sin a) while the path advances along (cos a, 0, sin a). The three segments never join (seen in game, one ran into a wall). Also the plug and prongs are never rotated to the cord heading, so the prongs float off the plug.
- **place_rusted_tin_cans**: made `body` (double-seam rims, recessed lid and base, beads, dents) and `label` (paper band torn open between 20° and 70°, ragged hems, peeling edge). Left: the two `rim` discs (`cyl_geo!(0.028, 0.028, 0.002)`), which sit hidden inside the body. Placement is fine, including the tipped variant.
- **place_mop_buckets**: made `body` (open bucket with rib and lip; straight taper kept so the water disc fits as before), `handle` (bail folded onto the rim, hooked ends), `stick`, `mopHead` (lumpy strand bundle). Left: the base circle (flat, now hidden under the floor) and the water circle (transparent). **Problems:** the mop head sits at (0.244, 0.506), detached in mid-air, while the stick runs from (0.244, 0) to (−0.044, 0.526) (seen in game). The water disc (r 0.070 at y 0.10) is also wider than the bucket at that height (≈0.068), so its edge pokes through the wall.
