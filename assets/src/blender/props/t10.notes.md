# t10 notes: P3, the rare: the parlour's and the office's big things

Seven placers the census graded `cartoon`, made again as the real things
they stand for (`docs/design/PROPS.md`). The parts are placed as modelled
(`prop_part_as_modelled`, a scale of one). What an earlier batch already
modelled is placed, not modelled again: the bookshelf's books are r02's
cloth hardbacks (stood up, their spines painted for the shelf), the
mirror's glass is r13a's approach (`Patch::Glass`, silvered, its silver
going), its frame's gilding `pictures::moulding_tex` (t01), the snifter's
glass the windows' glass as r16's wine glass. Each placer lives in a
`t10.rs` of its own crate (`placers_p02`, `p13`, `p14`, `p15`, `p20`) and
keeps its draws call for call.

The census's verdicts: the bookshelf "a wall shelf with curved ends,
holding books of uniform stick shape in red, blue, green, yellow, peach and
black ... toy-primary colours with no title bands or wear" (review6); the
locker "a single tall narrow brown-grey slab ... no louvres, door seam or
hinges ... a placeholder box" (review6); the record stack "four flat
sleeves ... standing on edge with no support ... no artwork, no text and
no ring-wear" (review7); the fireplace "the stack stops about a metre below
the ceiling and the whole piece stands in front of the wallpaper like a
dollhouse piece. The brick is uniform, with no soot" (review7); the cuckoo
clock "a plain brown box with a modern white office-clock face ... Nothing
is carved; it is not ornate" (review7); the mirror "a lemon-yellow frame of
four thin bars with lumpy corner rosettes ... The yellow reads as plastic,
not gilt" (review7); the snifter "a reddish ball sits on a brown saucer
under a faint speckled grey dome. There is no stemmed glass bowl" (review6).

## The parts (`t10.py`)

- **The 45s** (`Real__recordSleeve`, `…Vinyl`, `…VinylLow`, `…Label`): the
  sleeve two sheets of paper (a square round the 95 mm hole) held apart by
  the record, folded shut along three sides, its mouth open, the top sheet
  cockled; the disc a ring revolved from its section (the raised label
  area, the grooved band, the rim's bead, the 38 mm hole); a plainer disc
  for the ones the sleeves hide; the label a paper ring on its top.
- **The snifter** (`Real__snifterBowl`, `…Foot`, `…BrandyLow/Mid/High`,
  `…CoasterWood`, `…CoasterCork`, `…CoasterLeather`, `…Rocks`,
  `…RocksDregs`): the glass revolved from its section, 1.2 mm thick, its
  bowl's thick bottom, the rim fire-rounded; the foot and stem a part of
  their own (drawn as thicker glass); the brandy the bowl's inside to its
  surface, open there (the surface is the old animated disc, sized to the
  bowl at that height); three pours for the old fill's range.
- **The mirror** (`Real__ornateMirrorFrame`, `…Crest`): the frame a profile
  (outer bead, cove, a rising ogee to its crest, a sight bead, the lip over
  the glass, its rebate) swept round the rectangle and mitred, its bounds
  exactly its outer size so `moulding_tex` lays its gilding by the same
  profile; the crest a ribbed shell carved in relief, C-scrolls tapering
  as they curl, acanthus leaves (lobed, folded at the midrib, curled),
  three leaves breaking out at each corner round a boss, a small shell for
  the apron.
- **The bookshelf** (`Real__bookshelfSides`, `…Boards`, `…Back`): the sides
  a profile (the ogee scroll of the foot, the top's rounded corner)
  extruded and bevelled; the boards housed 6 mm into them, their fronts
  rounded; the back in the rebates.
- **The locker** (`Real__lockerCase`, `…Door`, `…Latch`, `…Plate`): the
  case of sheet (sides, back, top, bottom), the welded channel frame round
  the door, four angle legs on round feet, three five-knuckle hinges; the
  door a pan (its face, its returns), six louvres top and bottom (slots
  punched, each under a pressed hood, its ends pinched), the latch's pocket
  pressed in; the latch's cup rim, the lift handle and the padlock staple;
  the plate and its two rivets.
- **The cuckoo clock** (`Real__cuckooCase`, `…Carving`, `…Dial`, `…Hour`,
  `…Minute`, `…PendRod`, `…PendBob`, `…Chains`, `…Weights`): the case on
  its back board, a cornice and a plinth moulding, the cuckoo's door shut,
  the dial's turned seat; oak leaves (round lobes, folded, curled and
  twisted, two rings) round the dial with their stems under its seat,
  acorns, leaves on the top and hanging under the case, a bird with its
  wings raised; bone hands with a pierced loop and a spade tip; the
  pendulum's wire and hook, its carved leaf; the chains link by link,
  their free ends with rings; the weights lathed pine cones, their scales
  lifted in two spirals, a hook on each.
- **The fireplace** (`Real__fireplaceMantel`, `…Insert`, `…Grate`,
  `…Firebox`, `…Hearth`, `…Ash`, `…LogA`–`D`): see the docstring; the
  insert a cast plate with the arch cut through it, beads swept round the
  arch, leaf sprays and a shell in its spandrels, framed panels and
  rosettes down its sides, the canopy curving out under the arch's crown;
  the basket's bowed bars between its cheeks, finials, its floor bars, the
  pierced fret; the charred ends lathed, eaten away in the middle,
  alligatored.

## The game side

- **Bookshelf**: the old draws (how many books; each book's colour, spine
  width, spine height and lean) choose each book: its width and height
  pick one of r02's three hardbacks and scale it a little (0.94–1.04),
  its colour a faded cloth. The books stand from the left as many as fit
  (the ones that do not fit are drawn and left out); the last one leans on
  its neighbour's shoulder when it was drawn leaning and there is room.
  Each shelf paints its spines in one atlas (cloth faded where the room's
  light reached it, gilt rules and titles, a leather label, now and then a
  library's label with a call number, the head rubbed and dusty; black ink
  on the light cloths). Its middle stays at the old one's height (1.48 m),
  its width the old one's.
- **Locker**: the old colour draw picks its paint (medium grey, hunter
  green, dark brown, sand); the paint mottled and faded, scratched through
  to the primer and the steel, rust at its foot and its edges with
  streaks below, grime round its latch; the louvres show its dark inside.
  Its back on the wall where the old one's was; `locker_positions` as
  before.
- **Record stack**: the old draws pick each sleeve's ink, each label's
  print and ground, whether one is out, and where and how it is turned.
  The singles now lie in a stack (they stood on their edges unsupported);
  each laid a little askew (from where the stack is, no draws); the bare
  one lies across the stack, its far rim on the floor. One atlas for the
  stack's sleeves (white or kraft paper, three company designs, the ring
  worn into it, creases, stains, dust, a name in ballpoint), one for the
  labels; the grooves one texture shared by every disc.
- **Fireplace**: the old draws for its logs (how many, each one's place)
  lay the charred ends in the basket. Cold, its firebox black and sooted;
  or, on the old 55% draw, someone has lit it: a low wood fire, the bed of
  embers glowing under the logs and in the cracks of their undersides,
  five roots of flame among them (each the old flame's draws: its colour
  says how tall it burns, its two jitters where it rises), each a tongue
  and a smaller one beside it drawn from a gradient (orange root, yellow
  body, red tip thinning to nothing; additive, a flame); the old point
  light (0xff8030, 0.85, 3.0, 2) at the fire, the old flicker animator
  (its phases the old draws, its timing the old: the light's intensity,
  each flame's height from its root), and `lit_fireplace_positions` pushed
  so its crackle plays. The soot of years is a soft decal on the wall above
  the shelf; the mantel's paint is sooted over the opening's head.
- **Cuckoo clock**: the hands stop where the old draws put them; the
  pendulum swings (and is heard) as before when it was drawn swinging.
- **Mirror**: the old placement and slide; its gilding is the shared
  `moulding_tex` (Gilt) laid by the modelled profile, its crest a gilt of
  its own; the glass silvered (`Patch::Glass`), its silver going black in
  from the edges (worst at its foot), foxed in clusters, a bloom where damp
  stood behind it; dust on it, fly specks.
- **Snifter**: the old fill draw picks one of three pours; the old ripple
  draws still move its surface; the coaster draw picks the coaster, the
  ash and the whisky glass as before (its dregs, its place).
