# b15 notes ("toys": toy instruments, nursery toys, doll and teddy leftovers)

Build: 105 parts, 24 044 triangles, 0 errors, 1 warning (`SmallToySwords__tip`, 30 % frame deviation, on purpose, see below). Every part is within its triangle budget.
The map has 106 entries. They cover 108 constructors in 29 placers (the gallery's private build reports "108 constructors wrapped"). One entry covers both `segLen` rods of the triangle, one covers both castanet shells, and the two crib rails are split with `occ`. `SmallPiggyBanks__eye` is shared by the piggy bank and the dino.
4 placers are untouched.
I checked it with `prop-codemod --check` (no b15 problems except "not built" against the shared meshes), the contact sheets (`shots/props/b15.png`, `b15_p1.png`, `b15_p2.png`) and the gallery on level 4 (`b15_game_a..e.png`) and level 2 (`b15_game_l2_*.png`). I also took close-ups, 3 random runs per placer, with a private scratch script that calls `propShot` with a narrow fov (`b15_close_1..8.png`).
Whole objects sinking or hovering are left to the central placement pass and not logged. Below are part-to-part problems and wrong rotations only.
