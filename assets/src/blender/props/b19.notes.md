# b19 notes (victorian)

103 parts, 30 842 triangles, wrapping 111 primitives, 13 of 13 placers covered.
Build: 0 errors, 0 warnings; every part is within its own budget.
Checked part by part and in the game (level 4 seed 7, level 2 seed 3, and close-ups).

Shared parts: `TripodTelescopes__knob` serves all three telescope knobs, `Birdcages__bar` serves the cage
bars, the cage-door bars and the log basket's wire bars, `Fireplaces__log` serves the fireplace logs and
the log-basket logs, `OrnateCuckooClocks__pinecone` serves the pendulum bob and both weights,
`Humidors__trim` serves both gilt trims (box rim and lid).
