# t01 notes: P3, the rare: the pictures on the walls

Eight placers the census graded `cartoon`, made again as the real things
they stand for (`docs/PROPS.md` 1, "its picture a picture"): the framed
photo kept at a desk (`place_framed_photos`, p04), the baby's photograph
(`place_baby_photos`, p08), the graduation portrait
(`place_graduation_photos`, p09), the family portrait
(`place_family_portraits`, p21), the old portrait in oil
(`place_ancient_portraits`, p23), the portrait miniatures
(`place_miniature_portrait_clusters`, p17), the small landscape
(`place_abstract_landscapes`, p07) and the still life (`place_still_lifes`,
p07). Each placer lives in a module of the batch's own (`t01.rs` in its
crate; p21 `placers/t01.rs`, p23 `mystic/t01.rs`); the parts are placed as
modelled (`prop_part_as_modelled`, a scale of one), every part in the frame
of the wall it hangs on (x across its face, y up from the picture's middle,
z out of the wall, z = 0 the wall's face). Each placer keeps its draws call
for call.

The census's verdicts: the framed photo "a white field holding one flat grey
triangle (a mountain) and a faint sun disc; no photograph at all ... a
placeholder picture icon" (review1); the baby photo "a rounded white square
burnt out to pure white ... a blank placeholder card" (review4); the
graduation photo "a featureless dark egg with a white dash for a mouth,
under a floating mortarboard bar" (review3); the family portrait "four
white circles over grey trapezoids, a restroom-sign pictogram of a family"
(review7); the old portrait "a flat grey avatar silhouette ... the missing
profile photo icon ... four flat bars that overlap at the corners"
(review7); the miniatures "the same flat grey polygonal oval and nothing
else: no face, no sitter" (review1); the landscape "four lollipop trees ...
a child's clip-art, not a painting someone hung" (review2); the still life
"glossy pastel spheres with specular dots ... clip-art bubbles, not paint"
(review5).

## The pictures

Every picture is a real one: twenty old photographs and paintings released
as CC0 by the Rijksmuseum, fetched from Wikimedia Commons and checked by
the SHA-1 Commons publishes (`assets/src/textures/sources.json`,
`pictures`: each one's file, page, record, maker, crop and cell). The
photographs of people are before 1930 and of sitters nobody named (studio
cabinet cards and snapshots, 1870-1920); the paintings are minor works
(anonymous Dutch and Hague School painters, still lifes by Willem Hekking
and Jacob van Walscapelle, miniatures by Helant, Ritter and de Visser),
none a famous image. `build.py`'s `pictures()` (`pictures.py`) crops each
and lays it in its cell of one atlas, `crates/game/assets/textures/pictures.jpg`
(1600 x 1440, 610 KB; cells 320 x 400 for an upright picture, 400 x 320 for
a wide one, 160 x 200 for a miniature), ungraded; `MANIFEST.json` records
the licence (CC0-1.0) and the makers. `visuals::pictures` (`CELLS` mirrors
the layout) decodes the atlas once, when a floor first shows a picture,
and paints each print as it hangs: a photograph's blacks lifted, its paper
yellowed most at its edges, its dark tones silvered there, foxing, now and
then a crease across a corner (its emulsion cracked along it) or a damp
tide line from the foot; an oil darkened under amber varnish, cracked into
islands (a canvas's weave under the paint), grimed most at the foot, a
flake or two of paint lost to the ground. Every pixel is computed on every
core (`par_rows`), the strokes after on the one thread, from a stream mixed
with where the thing hangs, so no two prints are the same.

## The finishes (`visuals::pictures`)

Each moulding's finish is painted as its front is mapped (the part's
bounds), from where on its profile each pixel lies (the profile's face is
mirrored in the placer: `FACE`): wood stain with its grain along each rail
and a mitre at each corner, the stain rubbed paler on the crests and the
outer arris; ebonised black rubbed brown there; gold leaf (each square a
shade of its own) gone brown, burnished on the crests and rubbed through to
the red bole, chipped to the white gesso on the arris; paint brushed along
the rails, yellowed, chipped to the white ground and further to the wood.
Grime gathers in the hollows, dust on the top rail. The glass is the
windows' glass (`Patch::Glass`, a part of the windows' dirt map of its own);
the family portrait's is cracked from a knock at a corner (radials, rings
between them, the crushed spot). Nothing glows: the old miniatures'
emissive gold is gone.

## The draws

Each placer makes the same draws in the same order as the old one; what
the old drew for its look now chooses the new one's: the framed photo's
frame colour (walnut, black, brass, aluminium), the baby frame's pastel,
the graduation frame's and tassel's colours, the miniatures' shared or own
case colour, ring radius (the miniature's scale), silhouette shade (a salt
for its painting), hook (pin or ribbon) and decor bar (the bow); every
tilt is the tilt about its nail or hook. Which picture each shows and how
it has aged come from where it hangs (`vary_of`), not from new draws. The
miniatures' frame-tilt animator drew from the old cluster's fallback
torus rings, which exist only with the props switched off; the new cluster
counts what those rings would have been and draws (and animates) exactly
when the old one did.

Checked with `--dump-level`: on every census floor that has one of the
eight (seed, level: 77/12, 303/13, 11/8, 17/12, 17/9, 31337/10, 31337/12)
and a second floor for each where there is one (1/9, 11/10, 10/7, 13/13,
19/10, 18/8, 303/6, 15/6, 7/13, 2/14, 555/12), the draws and every other
placer's objects (placer, index, position, bounds, turn), the counts
(`solidsHash` among them) and the marks are the same before and after.
