# t18 notes: P3, the rare: the shrine's and the séance's things

Seven placers the census never framed (rare, or instanced out of the dump;
their floors come from a probe of seeds 5001-5040, levels 4-14, run with
`--flags noinstance`), made again as what a person brought, arranged or
left (`docs/design/LORE.md`; `docs/design/PROPS.md` 1, "the party and the shrine"): real
things, never a glowing or working device. The parts are placed as
modelled (`prop_part_as_modelled`, a scale of one). The placers live in
modules of their own: `placers_p22/src/placers/t18.rs` (the censer),
`placers_p02/src/t18.rs` (the tarot fan, the chalk pentagram, the talking
board, the chain), `placers_p23/src/halloween/t18.rs` (the crystal ball),
`placers_p17/src/t18.rs` (the memorial shrine); each keeps its draws call
for call.

The old ones: a censer of a half sphere and a dome on three 2 mm rods with
an ember light under it; a talking board as a flat decal 0.64 x 0.40 m and
a planchette fitted to a three-sided cylinder; five card planes fanned with
each turned against its place on the arc, a purple back with a diamond; a red pentagram decal of
`basic_mat` (it shone in the dark) with a pulsing red point light; a
translucent ball of `basic_mat` with an orbiting white "spark" and a
pulsing blue light inside; a shrine of boxes with a grey rectangle for its
photograph and a dark disc for a head, its brass emissive; five to eight
tori of one colour.

## The parts (`t18.py`)

- **Censer** (`Real__censerBowl`, `…Lid`, `…Chains`, `…DropLinks`,
  `…Hook`): the bowl one lathe (the foot, its stem and knop, the bowl, the
  rolled rim, its inside), three pierced tabs under the rim each with a ring;
  the lid a shell 1.3 mm thick (its outside, its inside, its foot), ten round
  holes round the dome and eight above them, six slots round the cupola
  (cut), the three guides on its rim and the finial's ring; the chains: three
  cable chains of 10 x 7 mm links of 1.5 mm wire, each link turned a quarter
  on the last, from the lugs' rings to three eyes under the domed chain
  head, the lid's chain up through the head's hole to its ring, the finger
  ring standing on the head; a pair of 20 x 12 mm lamp chain links (1.3 mm
  wire, the lower across the upper) repeated in the game; a swag hook (its
  canopy, a 4 mm wire J, the ball at its tip, a screw).
- **Talking board** (`Real__talkBoardCore`, `…Face`, `Real__talkPlanchette`,
  `…Felt`): the hardboard a slab with its corners rounded (9 mm), bowed 2.6
  mm along and 0.6 mm across and twisted a little (it lay in the damp), its
  face chipped at the near right corner; the printed face its own sheet over
  it (a polygon offset keeps it in front). The planchette swept over its
  teardrop outline (the top flat, the edge rounded over), the window cut,
  three turned legs and their felt pads.
- **Tarot cards** (`Real__tarotCard`, `…Bent`): card 0.35 mm, corners worn
  round (2.4 mm), bowed; the second with a corner dog-eared 38 degrees.
- **Crystal ball** (`Real__crystalBallGlass`, `…Stand`): the ball (80 mm)
  where it sits on the stand's lip; the stand one lathe (its foot, waist,
  rim and the cup under the ball).
- **Shrine** (`Real__shrineBoard`, `…Easel`, `…ChimeHolder`,
  `…ChimeCandleA/B/C`, `…ChimeWick`, `…Vase`, `…RoseStem`, `…RoseBud`,
  `…Cross`, `…Star`, `…RibbonBow`, `…RibbonTail`): the board with its
  arrises eased and an end sawn out of square; the easel strut for t01's
  frame (its frame), tapering, hinged on a cloth tab, reaching the ground
  behind when the frame leans back 14 degrees; the chime holder a lathe (the
  dish, its rolled rim, the socket); each candle burnt to its height, its
  crater melted through on one side, three runs (one over the socket's rim
  and down it), the wax collar in the socket's mouth, a drop on the dish;
  the wick curled; the vase a salt-glaze bottle 62 mm; the stem crooked,
  thorns, two curled dried leaves; the bud shrunk and creased, its sepals
  curled back; the cross on a stepped base; the star of two pierced
  triangles on a turned base; the bow's knot, loops and short tail, and its

- **Chain** (`Real__chainLinks5`…`8`): links of a stadium centreline, the

## The game side

- **Censer**: hung from the ceiling (`WALL_H`, as the old one) by its hook;
  as many pairs of lamp chain as bring the bowl's rim where the old bowl hung
  (`WALL_H - chain_len - 0.05`, within half a pair, 15 mm), the old chain
  length's draw choosing how many. The brass dull and brown (`tarnish_tex`),
  its chains darker. It sways about the hook as the old one swayed (its two
  draws, its amplitude); the old one swung about the floor under it, so its
  chains' tops moved on the ceiling.
- **Talking board**: the face painted in a serif face (Times) on a tan
  ground: a double rule, the sun by YES and the moon by NO, the letters in
  two arcs, the numerals, GOOD BYE; then its years per pixel: the ground
  mottled, the print rubbed through in an oval where the planchette went
  round, scratches, grime at the edges, a tide line, the knocked corner gone
  to the brown board, dust (`talk_board_tex`, 1024 x 683). The planchette
  maple gone amber. Its old offsets are now in the board's own frame (it
  always lies on the letters; the old one could lie off the board's edge),
  at the board's warped height, still: the old drift is gone (a planchette
  that moves by itself is a working board), its three draws kept.
- **Tarot fan**: five cards fanned about a point 10 mm in from their feet
  (the old fan turned each card against its place on the arc); the face-up
  one (the middle, as before) laid back on top; one card dog-eared. The face
  printed from the atlas (`print_tex`, aged as an engraving's paper: edges,
  spots, now and then a crease); the backs the Rider's crackled brown within
  a cream border (`tarot_back_tex`), aged alike. Which face, and which card
  is bent, from where the fan lies.
- **Chalk pentagram**: one decal as before (1.4 m, its place, its turn),
  painted per pixel (`pentagram_tex`, 1024 px: 1.37 mm a pixel): two rings
  each drawn in two strokes whose ends overlap, a star of five strokes bowed
  a little and overshooting their ends; each stroke 15-20 mm, its edge
  ragged, its grains caught on the floor's tooth, pressed unevenly and broken
  where it skipped; scuffed through where feet crossed it; its dust about
  it. Lambert, two thirds chalk white (no brighter than chalk, linear 0.83)
  over the place's chalk colour (`chalk_color_by_weather`, as r08's): the
  haunted floors' dark red chalk vanished on their dark carpet.
- **Crystal ball**: t09's cast glass (`Patch::Glass`) with a clear glass's
  body (0.18), the old colour a faint tint in it, dust on its crown and
  smears round its middle (`ball_dust_tex`); the stand walnut stained dark,
  worn to the wood on its rims (`stand_tex`).
- **Shrine**: the board stained by the old wood colour (`board_tex`: grain,
  a knot, worn pale, dust); the book's cloth black gone brown; the
  photograph one of three portraits (CC0), aged as t01 ages a photograph,
  under t01's glass, in walnut or ebonised wood; the candles by the old
  count, the old height choosing how far each has burnt (A, B or C), the old
  colour their wax's (`wax_tex`); the lit ones (the old 50%) with t09's
  flame and the old light and flicker; the four stems by their old draws (the
  colour their stem's, the length how deep it stands in the vase, the lean
  its lean, the foot's offset, the bud's turn its turn about the vase); the
  cross or the star as before (60/40), brass, no emissive; the ribbon (40%)
  a bow on the frame's corner, its tail stirring as the old tail did.
- **Chain**: one part by the old count of links; by the old colour
  galvanized gone dull with white rust, rusted bare steel, or black oiled
  steel blooming rust (`chain_tex`). Its position registered for its jangle
  as before (its middle now).

Every painter mixes where its thing lies into its seed (`salt`,
`pictures::vary_of`), so no two floors' things are painted alike, and adds
no level draw.

## Behaviour kept and dropped

- Kept: the censer's sway (its draws; now about its hook); the shrine's
  candle flames, their light and flicker (their draws), and its ribbon's
  stirring (its draws); the chain's registered position for its jangle.
- Dropped (their draws kept as `let _ = rnd();`): the pentagram's pulsing
  red point light (chalk does not shine), the crystal ball's pulsing inner
  light and its orbiting spark (glass does not shine), the censer's ember
  light (no draw; the censer is cold, nothing burns in it), the shrine's
  emissive brass (no draw), the planchette's drift (a planchette that
  moves by itself is a working board; its three draws kept).
