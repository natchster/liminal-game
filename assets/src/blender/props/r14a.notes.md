# r14a notes: P2, the common: things worn and dropped

Five placers the census graded `cartoon` (the single boot, the shoe pair,
the shoe box, the dropped glove, the single glove), made again as the real
things they stand for (`docs/PROPS.md`). The parts are placed as modelled
(`prop_part_as_modelled`, a scale of one). Alike things are modelled once:
one glove serves both glove placers, and the shoe and the boot are one
construction (one last, one loft, one sole) built twice. Each placer keeps
its draws call for call.

The census's verdicts: the boot "an L of a bare cylinder for the shaft and
a capsule for the foot. There is no sole, heel or seam" (review6); the
shoes "smooth, slipper-like orange-brown forms with black soles. The opening
is a black oval with a bar across it, and the laces are drawn on as XXX
decals" (review5); the shoe box "a bare light-brown block ... No lid
overhang, no seam, no print" (review5); the gloves "a block cuff, a slab
palm, four separate sausage fingers and a stub thumb ... a toy hand"
(review6).

## The parts (`r14a.py`)

- **The glove** (`Real__gloveBack`, `…Under`, `…Cuff`, `…Tips`): its
  outline is the zero line of a field (the palm and the wrist rounded
  polygons, each digit a capsule along a bowed axis, smoothly joined),
  traced by marching squares and simplified; filled by a constrained
  Delaunay triangulation (`mathutils.geometry.delaunay_2d_cdt`) with the
  lines the parts meet along as constraints (the wrist seam, the fingertip
  caps, the knuckle strap, the keystone thumb's seam), so each part's edge
  is clean. Two layers: the top stands off the rim by a rounding (a ring
  of points 1.8 mm in rounds the sewn edge), its digits round flattened
  tubes creased at the joints, the back's slack in long folds; the bottom
  lies on the floor. The middle and ring fingers lift a little at their
  tips (the curl a hand left). The tips part is a skin 0.4 mm over the back.
- **The shoe and the boot** (`Shoe` in `r14a.py`, from `DERBY` and
  `BOOT`): the sole's outline (an asymmetric last: the inner waist drawn
  in, the toe off to the big toe's side) inset by the welt is the
  featherline, at the sole's top (a heel, a waist, the toe spring); the
  topline round the opening and down the lacing (the inner side a little
  higher). The upper is lofted between them column by column, and the
  columns are laid out so the panels' seams run along them: the backstay
  (1.6 mm proud), the quarters (0.9 mm proud, lapped over the vamp, their
  front edge running back from the throat to the welt) and the vamp, which
  fans from the throat round the toe. Each column bulges along the
  surface's normal by an amount that varies smoothly round the loop (the
  vamp's dome, the counter's straighter back) so the copies of a seam
  column meet. Then wear: four flex creases across the vamp at the ball,
  the toe spring of wear, the boot's shaft in at the Achilles and slumped,
  the heel counter trodden down on the outer side, lumps of old leather.
  A rim rolls over the topline into the lining (the boot's collar padded
  8 mm), the insole fills its foot; the tongue lies under the laces; the
  laces criss-cross from eyelet to eyelet and are undone (the ends slack
  down the quarters, their aglets); the eyelets are rings set in the
  facing, the boot's speed hooks curl toward the lacing, empty. The sole
  is a slab on the outline (its edge rounded; the boot's lug ends notched
  into it), the heel a block on the rear of it; their downward faces are a
  part of their own, the tread, printed in plan.
- **Lying**: a shoe or a boot fallen over is modelled lying
  (`…Side`): turned about its length to where its centre of mass sits
  lowest over what it rests on (the derby 105 degrees, onto the welt and
  the collar's side; the boot 89.5), then set on the floor. Modelled so, the
  placement pass, which rests things by their meshes' oriented boxes, finds
  it already resting (a rolled upright model's boxes would have lifted it
  1.5 cm off the floor).
- **The shoe box** (`Real__shoeBoxBase`, `…Lid`, `…Label`, `…LidPrint`,
  `…Tissue`): an open tray of board 2.2 mm thick (its rim the doubled wall
  rolled over), the lid a tray turned over; the long walls bowed by damp, a
  knock in one; the lid's top sagged, its skirt splayed, its -x +z corner
  trodden in 24 mm with the fold across it (and the base's under it);
  the label and the print are thin plates following the walls they are on;
  the tissue a crumpled sheet in the base (only when the lid is off).

## In the game

- **Boot**: the old colour picks the leather (black, dark brown oiled,
  wheat nubuck, oxblood) with its lining, sole, laces (tan waxed or black)
  and hooks; the old 25% tipped picks the boot lying on its side. Its
  leather painted: mottled, grain, creases with pale lips, the dye worn
  through to the crust in streaks (most low on it), dust, tide marks.
  Lying, its lug tread shows: chevroned lugs worn grey, grit in the grooves.
- **Shoe pair**: the old colour picks the leather (mid brown, black, tan,
  dark brown, oxblood, bone gone grey, navy); the left one stands, the
  right one lies on its side (as before), turned by the old yaw draw, the
  old tilt now turning it a little more. Lying, its leather sole shows:
  worn dark and smooth at the ball, frayed at the toe, pale in the waist
  where the size is stamped, the heel's black top piece worn at the back
  with its nails.
- **Gloves**: the single glove's colour picks a work glove (brown split
  leather, grey cotton jersey, red canvas back with tan leather and a
  striped knitted wrist, blue-grey canvas back with tan leather and a blue
  wrist, a tan cowhide driver); the dropped glove's picks a dress glove
  (black, brown, dark brown or camel leather, three points stitched on the
  back) or a knitted one (maroon, charcoal, oatmeal); its old 40% trim knits
  a stripe round the wrist (a leather one then has a knitted wristlet). The
  back's painting follows the model's digits: the joints creased across,
  the knuckles rubbed, the fingertips soiled. The dropped glove's old
  finger tilts vary its painting; its old tilt is drawn and dropped (it
  lies flat now, `let _ = rnd();`).
- **Shoe box**: the old colour picks the board (kraft, light kraft, old
  kraft, grey board, white board gone cream) and its ink; 35% lid off as
  before, lying beside it, face down or up by where the box is (no new
  draw); a lid on sits a little askew the same way. Board painted with
  fibres, fading, a damp tide line low on the sides, scuffs, every face's
  edges rubbed pale at the folds and grimed at the foot; the end label
  (MEN'S DERBY, style, colour, leather, width, the size boxed, a barcode;
  no maker), yellowed, a dirty corner; the lid's print ("Fine Footwear",
  a ruled border; no maker) rubbed off where it was handled.

Every painter draws on a stream of its own, varied by where the thing lies
(or the old draws), so no two are alike from floor to floor. Materials are
lambert (the eyelets' and hooks' metal phong), their colour painted in the
map; nothing emits.
